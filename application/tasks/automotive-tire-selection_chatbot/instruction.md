# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You need new tires. Share your vehicle type and driving conditions.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for the best all-season tires for a 2018 Honda CR-V, driven mostly on wet highways and occasional light snow, within a $150/tire budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your vehicle (2018 Honda CR-V) and driving conditions (wet highways, occasional light snow), but don't reveal your budget or specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'top 10 tires'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific vehicle and conditions.
- Mention your budget of $150/tire only after the chatbot suggests options that exceed it, or if asked directly.
- Express concern about hydroplaning and snow traction, referencing your daily commute on a highway known for pooling water.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific tire model recommendation with price and rationale for your CR-V and conditions, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific vehicle and driving conditions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific vehicle (2018 Honda CR-V) and driving conditions (wet highways, light snow), and you left with a specific tire model name and price under $150/tire.
