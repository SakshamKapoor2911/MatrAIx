# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You cook Italian dishes twice a week and want to grow basil, oregano, and thyme on your sunny kitchen windowsill.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step plan for growing basil, oregano, and thyme on a sunny kitchen windowsill, including pot sizes, soil type, watering schedule, and when to start harvesting.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your kitchen windowsill and cooking habits, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just use any pot with drainage'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific herbs: basil, oregano, and thyme.
- Mention you have limited counter space and a budget of $30 for supplies.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete plan with specific pot sizes, soil mix, and watering schedule for basil, oregano, and thyme, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific herbs or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific herbs (basil, oregano, thyme) and your sunny windowsill location, and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as buying specific pot sizes or a recommended soil mix.
