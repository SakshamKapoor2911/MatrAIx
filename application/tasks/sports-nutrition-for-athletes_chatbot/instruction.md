# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You swim for an hour every morning and feel fatigued afterward. You need guidance on pre-swim snacks and post-workout recovery meals to improve energy.

## Your goal
Get concrete recommendations for a pre-swim snack that provides sustained energy for a 1-hour morning swim, and a post-swim recovery meal that reduces fatigue and aids muscle recovery.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you swim for an hour every morning and feel fatigued afterward, but don't reveal your specific question about snacks/meals until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'eat a banana before and protein after'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific morning routine and fatigue issue.
- Mention that you prefer quick, easy-to-prepare options with minimal cooking, and have a budget of around $10 per week for extra snacks/meals beyond your regular groceries.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific pre-swim snack (e.g., oatmeal with banana) and a post-swim recovery meal (e.g., Greek yogurt with berries) tailored to your morning routine and budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific fatigue issue or given actionable advice beyond generic tips.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific morning swim routine and fatigue (not generic fitness tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific snack and meal plan with preparation steps and cost estimate.
