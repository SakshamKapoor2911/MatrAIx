# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You swim for an hour every morning and feel fatigued afterward. You need guidance on pre-swim snacks and post-workout recovery meals to improve energy.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable recommendations for a pre-swim snack (to eat 30-60 minutes before) and a post-swim recovery meal (within 30 minutes after) that will reduce fatigue during the swim and aid recovery afterward.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by describing your fatigue without giving full details; wait for the chatbot to ask about timing and intensity.
- Push back if the advice is too generic (e.g., 'eat a banana' without specifics on portion or timing).
- Mention you have a moderate budget and prefer simple, whole foods.
- Ask for alternatives if the first suggestion doesn't suit your taste (e.g., dairy-free options).

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to ensure the chatbot probes enough to tailor advice.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear, actionable plan for both pre- and post-swim nutrition that includes specific foods, portions, and timing, OR after 6 exchanges without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides at least one concrete pre-swim snack (e.g., half a banana with peanut butter, or a small smoothie) with timing (30-60 min before) and a post-swim meal (e.g., Greek yogurt with berries and granola, or a turkey sandwich) within 30 minutes after, and explains why these choices help with fatigue.
