# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You struggle with boundaries in a relationship. Share the relationship type and what feels crossed.

## Your goal
Get concrete advice on how to set and communicate a specific boundary with my romantic partner regarding their habit of checking my phone without asking.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by describing the relationship as a romantic partnership of 2 years.', "- Initially vague about the boundary issue; only mention 'feeling uncomfortable with something they do' until the chatbot asks for specifics.", "- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'just talk to them' without specific phrasing or scripts.", "- Mention that you've tried hinting but it didn't work, and you're worried about sounding controlling."]

## Interaction requirements
At least 3 back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific script or step-by-step approach for stating the boundary about phone checking, or after 6 exchanges if not resolved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it offers a clear, actionable script for stating the boundary (e.g., 'I feel uncomfortable when you check my phone without asking; I need you to ask first'), includes tips on tone and timing, and addresses the fear of sounding controlling.
