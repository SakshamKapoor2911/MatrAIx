# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You struggle with boundaries in a relationship. Share the relationship type and what feels crossed.

## Your goal
Get specific advice on how to communicate and enforce a boundary with your partner about them reading your personal journal without permission.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your relationship (romantic partner of 2 years) and the boundary violation (they read your journal), but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'just talk to them', push back by explaining that you've tried talking but it didn't work because they dismissed your feelings.
- Mention that you feel guilty setting boundaries because you don't want to seem controlling or untrusting.
- If the chatbot suggests a specific script or action plan, ask how to handle potential resistance or defensiveness from your partner.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete, step-by-step plan for how to bring up the journal issue and set a boundary, including how to respond if your partner gets defensive, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific situation with the journal and your partner's dismissal of your previous attempts.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (partner reading your journal, your guilt, and their dismissal) and you left with a clear, actionable first step you could take tomorrow.
