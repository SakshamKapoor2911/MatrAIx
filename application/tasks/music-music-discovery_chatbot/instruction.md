# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You're tired of your Spotify playlists and want to find new indie folk artists similar to Iron & Wine and Bon Iver.

## Your goal
Discover 3-5 new indie folk artists similar to Iron & Wine and Bon Iver, with specific song recommendations.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially mention only that you like indie folk and Iron & Wine/Bon Iver.
- If the chatbot asks for more details, mention that you prefer acoustic, mellow, and lyrically rich music.
- Push back if the suggestions are too mainstream or not similar enough (e.g., if The Lumineers or Mumford & Sons are suggested).
- If budget is mentioned, indicate you're open to free streaming or low-cost options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides at least three new artist names with specific song recommendations that match your taste, or after 5 exchanges without satisfactory suggestions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identified at least three indie folk artists (e.g., Gregory Alan Isakov, Fleet Foxes, The Paper Kites) and suggested specific songs (e.g., 'The Stable Song', 'Mykonos', 'Bloom'), and you felt the recommendations fit the acoustic, mellow style of Iron & Wine and Bon Iver.
