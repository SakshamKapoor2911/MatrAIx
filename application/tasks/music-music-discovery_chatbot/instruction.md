# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You're tired of your Spotify playlists and want to find new indie folk artists similar to Iron & Wine and Bon Iver.

## Your goal
Get recommendations for indie folk artists similar to Iron & Wine and Bon Iver, specifically those with a lo-fi, intimate sound and poetic lyrics.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing that you're tired of your Spotify playlists and want to find new indie folk artists similar to Iron & Wine and Bon Iver, but don't reveal your specific preference for lo-fi, intimate sound until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of well-known indie folk artists (e.g., Fleet Foxes, The Lumineers), push back and ask for lesser-known artists with a lo-fi, intimate sound like early Iron & Wine.
- Mention that you've already listened to Bon Iver's later albums and prefer the raw, stripped-down style of 'For Emma, Forever Ago' and Iron & Wine's 'The Creek Drank the Cradle'.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides at least 3 specific artist recommendations that match your lo-fi, intimate sound preference, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific preference for lo-fi, intimate sound and keeps recommending mainstream indie folk.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendations referenced your specific preference for lo-fi, intimate sound similar to early Iron & Wine and Bon Iver's 'For Emma, Forever Ago', and you left with at least 3 new artists to check out.
