# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to preserve photos from your recent trip to Japan in a scrapbook. You need advice on layout design, acid-free materials, and journaling ideas.

## Your goal
Get a clear, step-by-step plan for creating a scrapbook of your Japan trip: suggested layout for 20 photos, a list of acid-free materials to buy, and 3 journaling prompts for captions.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your Japan trip photos (e.g., 20 prints, mix of landscapes and street scenes) and your desire to scrapbook them, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., "use acid-free paper"), push back and ask how to apply it to your specific photos (e.g., "Which acid-free adhesive works for glossy photo paper?").
- Mention you have a budget of $30 for materials and want to prioritize must-have items.
- Ask for examples of journaling styles that match the mood of your photos (e.g., poetic vs. factual).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific layout plan for your 20 photos (e.g., grouping by city, using a timeline), a list of 3-5 acid-free materials with prices under $30, and 3 journaling prompts tailored to your photo types, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific photos, budget, or journaling needs.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific Japan trip photos (landscapes and street scenes), gave concrete material recommendations within your $30 budget, and provided journaling ideas that matched the tone of your photos (e.g., haiku for landscapes, anecdotes for street shots). You left with an actionable first step (e.g., buy a specific album brand, print photos in a certain size, or write one journal entry).
