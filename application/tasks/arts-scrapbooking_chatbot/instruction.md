# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to preserve photos from your recent trip to Japan in a scrapbook. You need advice on layout design, acid-free materials, and journaling ideas.

## Your goal
Get specific advice on layout design, acid-free materials, and journaling ideas for a scrapbook preserving photos from a trip to Japan.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Mention that you have 50 photos from Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka.', '- Specify a budget of $50 for materials.', "- Push back if the advice is too generic (e.g., 'use acid-free glue' without brand names).", '- Ask for examples of journaling prompts related to travel.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive specific product recommendations (brands for acid-free paper, glue, and pens) and at least 3 journaling prompts tailored to Japan travel, or after 5 turns exhausted.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends at least one brand of acid-free paper (e.g., Canson, Strathmore), one brand of acid-free glue (e.g., UHU, Lineco), and provides 3+ journaling prompts related to Japanese culture or travel (e.g., 'What was your favorite meal in Kyoto?').
