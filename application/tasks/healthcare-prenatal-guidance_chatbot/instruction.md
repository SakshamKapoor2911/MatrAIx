# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You are pregnant or planning to become pregnant. Share your stage and any questions about prenatal care.

## Your goal
Get personalized advice on prenatal nutrition, specifically which foods to eat and avoid during the first trimester, and whether to start taking a prenatal vitamin with DHA.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by saying you're in your first trimester (8 weeks) and have morning sickness.
- Mention you're vegetarian but can eat dairy and eggs.
- Express concern about getting enough iron and folate.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'just eat healthy'); ask for specific examples.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End after receiving a list of 3-5 specific vegetarian-friendly foods rich in iron and folate, and a clear recommendation on whether a prenatal vitamin with DHA is necessary.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide a list of specific vegetarian foods (e.g., lentils, spinach, fortified cereals) for iron and folate, and explicitly state that a prenatal vitamin with DHA is recommended for fetal brain development. If it does not address both the food list and DHA recommendation, the interaction is unsuccessful.
