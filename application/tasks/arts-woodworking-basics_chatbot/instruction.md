# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a small garage with basic tools and want to build a simple bookshelf as your first project, but you don't know what wood to use.

## Your goal
Get a clear recommendation on what type of wood to use for a simple bookshelf, considering your small garage, basic tools, and budget constraints, and a step-by-step plan for the first day of building.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you have a small garage with basic tools (a circular saw, drill, and sander) and want to build a simple bookshelf, but you don't know what wood to use. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of wood types without addressing your specific constraints (small garage, basic tools, budget), push back and ask how it applies to your situation.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget (under $50 for wood) and have limited space, so you need wood that is easy to cut and assemble with your tools.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific type of wood (e.g., plywood, pine, or MDF) with a reason tailored to your tools and budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (small garage, basic tools, budget under $50).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific constraints (small garage, basic tools, budget under $50) and you left with a clear recommendation for a wood type and a first step (e.g., 'go to the hardware store and buy a 4x8 sheet of 3/4-inch plywood').
