# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
Your laptop is having issues. Share the symptoms and model.

## Your goal
Diagnose and resolve laptop issues (e.g., frequent freezing, blue screen errors) for a specific model (e.g., Dell XPS 13 9310).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by sharing only general symptoms (e.g., 'My laptop freezes often').
- Provide specific model only after being asked.
- Push back on generic advice (e.g., 'I already tried restarting').
- Mention limited technical knowledge and need for simple steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific actionable solution (e.g., 'Update your graphics driver') or after 5 messages without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot was helpful if it correctly identified potential causes based on the model (e.g., Dell XPS 13 freezing could be driver-related) and gave step-by-step instructions to resolve the issue.
