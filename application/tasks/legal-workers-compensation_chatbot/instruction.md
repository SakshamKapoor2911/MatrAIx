# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You were injured at work. The chatbot explains workers compensation rights and process.

## Your goal
I want to understand my workers' compensation rights after a workplace injury, specifically what benefits I'm entitled to, how to file a claim, and the timeline for receiving benefits.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Start by stating you had an injury at work but don't immediately specify the type or severity; wait for the chatbot to ask.", "- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice without specifics (e.g., 'I need to know what to do for my specific injury').", "- Mention you're worried about medical bills and lost wages, and ask about coverage for both.", "- If the chatbot suggests consulting a lawyer, ask if that's necessary or if you can handle it yourself."]

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received a clear, step-by-step explanation of the claims process and a list of benefits you're entitled to, or after 5 turns if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide specific information about workers' compensation benefits (e.g., medical expense coverage, wage replacement percentage, time limits for filing) and outline the claim process (e.g., reporting injury to employer, filing form, deadlines). It should address your concern about medical bills and lost wages. If it only gives generic advice or fails to provide concrete steps, it is not successful.
