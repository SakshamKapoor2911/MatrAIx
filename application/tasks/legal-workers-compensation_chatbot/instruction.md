# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You were injured at work. The chatbot explains workers compensation rights and process.

## Your goal
Find out if I can choose my own doctor for a work injury and what happens if I do.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your injury (fell off a ladder at a warehouse job, hurt your back and wrist), but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about workers comp, push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: you're a temp worker and your employer hasn't provided any forms.
- Mention that you're worried about costs and that your employer is being vague about medical care.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot explicitly tells you whether you can choose your own doctor and what the consequences are, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific question about choosing a doctor.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (temp worker, back and wrist injury, employer not providing forms) and you left knowing your options for choosing a doctor and the next step to take.
