# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are hosting a baby shower. Share the date and guest list size.

## Your goal
Finalize a baby shower plan: confirm the date works for key guests and settle on a guest list size that fits your budget and venue.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation as hosting a baby shower, but don't reveal your specific question (e.g., which date or guest count) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic party planning tips, push back and ask how it applies to your specific constraints: a tight budget and a small venue that maxes out at 20 people.
- Mention that you're worried about inviting too many people and overspending, and ask for help balancing the guest list with the budget.
- Keep the conversation focused on practical decisions; avoid discussing decorations or games unless directly relevant.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you've agreed on a specific date (e.g., June 15) and a final guest list size (e.g., 15 guests), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific budget and venue constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific budget of $200 and venue capacity of 20 people (not generic tips) and you left with a clear date and guest count you can act on.
