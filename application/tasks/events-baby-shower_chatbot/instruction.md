# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are hosting a baby shower. Share the date and guest list size.

## Your goal
Get advice on hosting a baby shower with specific details: date is June 15, 2024, guest list size is 25 people.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Provide the date and guest count early in the conversation.
- Ask for suggestions on themes, decorations, food, and activities suitable for 25 guests.
- If the chatbot asks for more details, provide them (e.g., budget, venue).
- Do not accept generic advice; ask for specific recommendations.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a concrete plan or checklist covering theme, decorations, food, and activities tailored to June 15 and 25 guests, or after 6 exchanges without helpful specifics.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it offers specific suggestions for a baby shower on June 15, 2024, for 25 guests, including at least one theme idea, decoration tips, food options, and an activity. If it only gives generic advice or fails to incorporate the date and guest count, it fails.
