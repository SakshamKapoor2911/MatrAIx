# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You're getting married next month in California and want to change your last name to your spouse's, but you're unsure of the steps beyond the marriage license.

## Your goal
Get a clear step-by-step plan for legally changing your last name after marriage in California, including which documents to update and in what order, and learn about any time limits or costs involved.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're getting married next month in California and want to change your last name, but you're unsure of the steps beyond the marriage license.
- Don't reveal that you specifically want to use the marriage license for the name change unless the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'update your Social Security card, driver's license, etc.'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific timeline (next month wedding) and California-specific procedures.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and want to know if there are any hidden fees or free options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a concrete list of steps tailored to California (including whether you need a court order or can use the marriage certificate) and a rough timeline, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your California-specific concerns or timeline.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced California-specific procedures (e.g., using the marriage certificate for Social Security and DMV, no court order needed) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'obtain certified copies of your marriage certificate after the wedding').
