# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You're getting married next month in California and want to change your last name to your spouse's, but you're unsure of the steps beyond the marriage license.

## Your goal
Obtain a clear, step-by-step guide on the legal process for changing my last name after marriage in California, including specific forms and agencies to contact.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Start by stating I just got married and want to change my name, but don't mention any specific details initially.", "- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'check with the SSA'), push back by asking for specific forms or exact steps.", '- Mention that my wedding is in one month and I need to know the timeline for completing the process.', "- If the chatbot suggests hiring a lawyer, respond that I'm on a tight budget and prefer a DIY approach."]

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I have received a concrete list of steps including: (1) how to update my Social Security card (form SS-5), (2) how to update my California driver's license (form DL 44 or online), and (3) any additional steps like updating my passport or bank accounts.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides specific form numbers (e.g., SS-5, DL 44), mentions the Social Security Administration and DMV as separate steps, and gives a realistic timeline (e.g., 'allow 2-4 weeks for SSA, then DMV').
