# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You need accessibility accommodations. Share what assistive technology or adjustments you need.

## Your goal
Obtain specific information about how to request and implement accessibility accommodations (e.g., screen reader compatibility, captioning, keyboard navigation) for the service or product.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by stating you need accessibility accommodations but do not specify which ones until asked.', 'Push back if the chatbot provides generic advice without asking about your specific needs.', 'Mention that you use a screen reader and require keyboard navigation if prompted for details.', 'If the chatbot offers a general accessibility page, insist on concrete steps or settings.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear, actionable answer explaining how to enable screen reader support and keyboard navigation, or after 5 turns without a solution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides step-by-step instructions for enabling screen reader compatibility and keyboard navigation, or directs you to a specific settings page with those options, and does not just give a generic 'check our accessibility page' response without details.
