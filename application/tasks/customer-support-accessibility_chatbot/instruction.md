# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You need accessibility accommodations. Share what assistive technology or adjustments you need.

## Your goal
Get a clear confirmation from the chatbot that my specific accessibility needs (screen reader, high contrast mode, and closed captions) will be fully supported on the platform, and receive a step-by-step guide on how to enable them.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your need for accessibility accommodations in general terms, e.g., 'I have some accessibility requirements I need help with.' Do not reveal your specific assistive technologies until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of accessibility features without addressing your specific needs (screen reader, high contrast mode, closed captions), push back and ask how they apply to your specific requirements.
- Mention that you rely on these accommodations daily and need them to work seamlessly; express frustration if the chatbot seems dismissive or provides incomplete information.
- Include a budget constraint: you are not willing to pay extra for accessibility features, as they should be included.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a detailed, step-by-step guide on enabling screen reader, high contrast mode, and closed captions with confirmation that all three are fully supported, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific requirements (screen reader, high contrast mode, closed captions) and continues to give generic advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific assistive technologies (screen reader, high contrast mode, closed captions) rather than generic tips, and you left with an actionable first step you could take to enable these features on the platform.
