# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning a birthday party. Share the age budget and guest count.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for a child's birthday party including a realistic budget breakdown, a guest list strategy, and a timeline for planning.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: planning a birthday party for your 8-year-old daughter, with a budget of $300 and 15 guests (10 kids and 5 adults). Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., '10 tips for a great party'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific budget of $300 and the mix of kids and adults.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and need to prioritize spending on activities vs. food vs. decorations.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific budget breakdown for food, activities, and decorations under $300, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your $300 budget or the kid/adult guest mix.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $300 budget and 15 guests (10 kids, 5 adults) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a prioritized list of spending or a timeline for booking.
