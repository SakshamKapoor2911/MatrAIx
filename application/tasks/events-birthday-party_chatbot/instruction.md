# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning a birthday party. Share the age budget and guest count.

## Your goal
Plan a birthday party with specific budget and guest count constraints.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Share the age of the birthday person (e.g., 30th birthday).
- Specify a budget (e.g., $500).
- Provide guest count (e.g., 20 guests).
- Ask for suggestions on venue, catering, and decorations within budget.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
Receive a concrete plan (venue, catering, decorations) that fits within the $500 budget for 20 guests.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provides specific recommendations for venue, catering, and decorations that total ≤ $500 and accommodate 20 guests, including the age of the birthday person.
