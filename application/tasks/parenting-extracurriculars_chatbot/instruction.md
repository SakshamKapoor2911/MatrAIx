# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your child wants to start activities. Share their age interests and schedule.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for introducing two activities to your 5-year-old daughter: beginner swimming lessons and a Saturday morning art class, balancing her interest in both without overscheduling.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 5-year-old daughter's enthusiasm for swimming and art, but don't reveal you're torn between the two until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle like 'try different activities,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: limited weekend time and a child who gets overwhelmed with too many commitments.
- Mention your budget constraint: you can only afford one paid activity per season, so you need to choose wisely.
- Stay polite but firm; don't accept vague suggestions without concrete steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a specific schedule that includes both swimming and art class on alternating weeks or a trial plan, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (limited weekend time, budget for only one activity, and your child's tendency to get overwhelmed).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific constraints (limited weekend time, budget for one activity, child's overwhelm) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a trial schedule or a decision framework.
