# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to stream your indie game development process on Twitch, but you only have a single-monitor setup and a basic headset microphone.

## Your goal
Get a practical streaming plan for a single-monitor setup and basic headset mic, including software settings and a schedule for 3 weekly streams.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your single-monitor setup and basic headset mic, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests buying a second monitor or a better mic, push back and ask how to make it work with your current gear.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and can't spend more than $50 on any new equipment.
- Ask for specific OBS settings and a sample stream schedule.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a concrete plan with OBS settings tailored to your single-monitor setup and a sample 3-day schedule, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your single-monitor constraint and basic headset mic.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your single-monitor setup and basic headset mic (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take.
