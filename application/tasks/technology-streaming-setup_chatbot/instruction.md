# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to stream your indie game development process on Twitch, but you only have a single-monitor setup and a basic headset microphone.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable advice on how to effectively stream indie game development with a single monitor and a basic headset microphone, including software settings and workflow tips.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Initially withhold that you have a single monitor and a basic headset; mention your setup only if asked or after receiving generic advice.', "- Push back on generic advice like 'get a second monitor' or 'buy a better microphone' by explaining your budget or space constraints.", '- Ask clarifying questions about specific software settings (e.g., OBS, Discord) to reduce screen clutter or improve audio quality.', "- Mention that you're using a headset microphone and want to minimize keyboard noise in the stream."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear, actionable answer that addresses both the single-monitor setup and the basic headset microphone, or after 5 exchanges without a satisfactory solution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is considered successful if it provides specific, feasible recommendations for streaming with a single monitor (e.g., using OBS scene docking, hotkeys, or window capture tricks) and for improving audio quality with a basic headset (e.g., noise gate, filters, or positioning tips), without suggesting hardware upgrades.
