# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a single parent with two kids earning $35,000/year. You want to know if you qualify for SNAP or Medicaid.

## Your goal
Determine whether you qualify for SNAP or Medicaid based on your income and family size, and get clear instructions on how to apply.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: single parent, two kids, $35k/year. Don't ask about specific programs yet.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'check your state's website'), push back and ask how your income compares to the limits for SNAP and Medicaid.
- Mention that you're worried about the application process being too complicated or time-consuming.
- If the chatbot quotes income limits, ask whether your $35k gross or net income is used.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a clear yes/no for SNAP and Medicaid based on your $35k income and family size of 3, with specific next steps, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your income and family size.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it referenced your specific $35k income and family of 3, and you left knowing whether you likely qualify and what to do next (e.g., apply online, gather documents).
