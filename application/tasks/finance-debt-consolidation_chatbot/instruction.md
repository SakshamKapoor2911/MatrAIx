# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You have multiple debts and want to simplify payments. Share your debt amounts and interest rates.

## Your goal
Get a consolidated debt repayment plan that minimizes total interest and simplifies monthly payments by combining my three debts: $5,000 at 18% APR, $8,000 at 22% APR, and $3,000 at 15% APR.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially only mention that you have multiple debts, but do not disclose amounts or rates until asked.
- Push back on generic advice like 'just pay more each month' or 'consider a balance transfer' without specifics.
- If the chatbot suggests a debt consolidation loan, ask about fees, APR, and how it compares to current rates.
- Mention a strict monthly budget of $500 for debt payments.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a concrete plan (e.g., which debt to pay first, or a specific consolidation loan option) that accounts for all three debts and the $500 budget, OR after 5 exchanges without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
A successful response must: (1) identify all three debts with their exact amounts and rates, (2) recommend a specific strategy (e.g., avalanche vs. snowball, or a consolidation loan with APR below 18%), (3) incorporate the $500 monthly budget, and (4) provide a step-by-step payment schedule or loan terms.
