# Volunteering Chatbot

## Your situation
You have $500 to donate this year and care most about animal welfare and environmental conservation, but you want to ensure your money has real impact.

## Your goal
Get a shortlist of 2-3 high-impact nonprofits in animal welfare and environmental conservation that fit a $500 budget, with clear metrics for how the donation will be used.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your interest in animal welfare and environmental conservation and that you have $500 to donate, but don't reveal that you want specific recommendations until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of charities, push back and ask how your $500 would specifically be used by each (e.g., 'How many trees planted per $100? How many animals treated?').
- Mention that you want to avoid overhead-heavy organizations and ask for transparency on administrative costs.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete recommendation of 2-3 specific nonprofits with estimated impact per $500, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget constraint of $500 or your criteria of measurable impact.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $500 budget and your dual interests (animal welfare and conservation) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., names of organizations to research or donate to).
