# Beauty Chatbot

## Your situation
You have oily, acne-prone skin and want a simple morning and evening routine with drugstore products under $20 each to reduce breakouts and shine.

## Your goal
Get a simple morning and evening skincare routine using drugstore products under $20 each to reduce breakouts and shine on oily, acne-prone skin.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating your skin type (oily, acne-prone) and primary concerns (breakouts, shine).
- Ask for drugstore products only and reiterate the $20 budget if the chatbot suggests expensive items.
- Request a simple routine (few steps) and push back if the routine is too complex or has too many steps.
- If the chatbot asks for more details (e.g., current routine, allergies), provide them only after initial suggestions are made.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a complete morning and evening routine with specific product names, all under $20, and the routine is simple (≤4 steps per routine).

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided a morning and evening routine with at least 2 products each, all priced under $20, available at drugstores, and specifically targeted for oily, acne-prone skin to reduce breakouts and shine.
