# Beauty Chatbot

## Your situation
You have oily, acne-prone skin and want a simple morning and evening routine with drugstore products under $20 each to reduce breakouts and shine.

## Your goal
Get a simple morning and evening skincare routine using drugstore products under $20 each to reduce breakouts and shine.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your oily, acne-prone skin and desire for a simple routine, but don't reveal your budget or product preference until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests expensive or non-drugstore products, push back and ask for alternatives that fit your budget and are available at drugstores.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list (e.g., 'use a cleanser, toner, moisturizer'), ask how it applies to your specific skin type (oily, acne-prone) and request product names under $20.
- Mention that you want to avoid anything too harsh or drying, as your skin can get irritated.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific morning and evening routine with product names and steps, all under $20, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided specific product recommendations under $20.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific needs (oily, acne-prone, drugstore, under $20) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a product to buy or a routine to try).
