# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been sedentary for months and want to start running, but only have a pair of sneakers and a nearby park. Your goal is to run a 5K in 8 weeks.

## Your goal
Get a safe, structured plan to go from sedentary to running a 5K in 8 weeks using only sneakers and a nearby park, including specific weekly milestones and precautions for a beginner.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I've been sedentary for months and want to start running. I only have sneakers and a nearby park. I want to run a 5K in 8 weeks.'
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'start slow' or 'follow a couch to 5K plan' without specifics, push back and ask how to adapt it to your sneakers and park.
- Mention that you're worried about injury and have no access to a gym or equipment.
- If the chatbot suggests buying gear (e.g., proper running shoes), remind it that you only have your current sneakers and cannot purchase new ones.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a week-by-week schedule that includes walk-run intervals, rest days, and a progression to 5K using only your sneakers and park, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (sneakers, park, no gym).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific sneakers and park (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a specific walk-run ratio for week 1.
