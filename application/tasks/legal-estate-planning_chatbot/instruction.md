# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a home worth $400,000 and have two young children. You want to ensure they are cared for if something happens to you.

## Your goal
Get a detailed plan for a will that names a guardian for my two children and ensures my $400,000 home is passed to them appropriately.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by asking general questions about wills and estate planning.', '- Only after the chatbot asks, mention that you have two children aged 3 and 5.', '- Mention the home value ($400,000) only when discussing assets.', '- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice without addressing guardianship or the specific value of the home.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear recommendation on how to name a guardian and how to transfer the home (e.g., via will or trust), or after 7 turns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a concrete legal step (e.g., 'name a guardian in your will') and addressed the $400,000 home specifically (e.g., 'consider a trust to avoid probate').
