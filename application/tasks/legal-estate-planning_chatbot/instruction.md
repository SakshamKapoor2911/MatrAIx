# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a home worth $400,000 and have two young children. You want to ensure they are cared for if something happens to you.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for a simple will that names guardians for my two young children and specifies how my $400,000 home should be managed for them.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you own a $400,000 home and have two young children, and you want to ensure they are cared for if something happens to you. But don't reveal your specific question about guardianship or the home until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about estate planning, push back and ask how it applies to your specific $400,000 home and the need to name guardians for your children.
- Mention that you have a limited budget for legal fees, so you want cost-effective options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific recommendation for naming guardians and managing the $400,000 home in a will, including cost estimates, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for guardianship and the home.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $400,000 home and the need to name guardians for your two young children (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as drafting a simple will or consulting a specific type of lawyer.
