# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a cruise. Share your preferred region duration and budget.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for a 7-day Caribbean cruise from Miami in January with a budget of $2,000 per person, including cabin type and tips for excursions.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're considering a cruise, but don't reveal your preferred region, duration, or budget until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of popular cruises, push back and ask how it applies to your specific budget and January timing.
- Mention that you're flexible on cabin type but want to stay within budget, and ask about hidden costs like gratuities or excursions.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific cruise recommendation (line, ship, itinerary, cabin category) that fits your $2,000 budget and January timeframe, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget or preferred region.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific budget ($2,000/person), preferred region (Caribbean), timing (January), and you left with a concrete cruise suggestion or a clear next step to book.
