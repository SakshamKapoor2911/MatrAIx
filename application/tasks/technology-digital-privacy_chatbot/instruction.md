# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
After a recent data breach, you want to remove your personal information from people-search sites and secure your social media accounts.

## Your goal
Create a concrete, step-by-step plan to remove my personal information from three specific people-search sites (Spokeo, Whitepages, MyLife) and secure my Facebook and Twitter accounts with two-factor authentication and privacy settings.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your recent data breach anxiety, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'use strong passwords'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: you already have strong passwords but need to remove data from people-search sites.
- Mention that you have limited time (only 30 minutes per week) and want a prioritized action plan, not just a list of tips.
- If the chatbot suggests paid services, ask for free or low-cost alternatives because you're on a tight budget.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a written checklist of opt-out steps for Spokeo, Whitepages, and MyLife, plus guidance on enabling 2FA and adjusting privacy settings on Facebook and Twitter, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific named sites and accounts.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific sites (Spokeo, Whitepages, MyLife) and accounts (Facebook, Twitter) by name, and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., 'opt out of Spokeo using their opt-out page').
