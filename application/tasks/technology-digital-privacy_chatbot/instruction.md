# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
After a recent data breach, you want to remove your personal information from people-search sites and secure your social media accounts.

## Your goal
Get step-by-step instructions to remove personal information from at least 3 specific people-search sites (e.g., Spokeo, Whitepages, MyLife) and secure social media accounts (Facebook, Twitter) with 2FA and privacy settings.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by stating you are concerned about a recent data breach and need help removing personal info from people-search sites.', 'Do not reveal all sites initially; mention only one or two first and wait for specific advice before asking about others.', "Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'opt out' without linking to actual opt-out pages.", 'Mention you have a limited budget, so free methods are preferred.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) covering both people-search removal and social media security.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received actionable steps for removing info from at least 3 people-search sites and specific settings changes for Facebook and Twitter.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides direct links or exact instructions for opting out of Spokeo, Whitepages, and MyLife, and explains how to enable 2FA and adjust privacy settings on Facebook and Twitter. It should also mention free methods.
