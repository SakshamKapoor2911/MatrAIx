# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You are a new customer setting up your account. The chatbot walks you through initial setup.

## Your goal
Complete the account setup process, including verifying email, setting up a password, and providing basic profile information (name, phone number).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Follow the chatbot's instructions step by step without skipping ahead.
- Provide information only when asked, starting with email address.
- If the chatbot asks for optional info (e.g., phone number), provide it.
- Do not ask questions or deviate from the setup flow.

## Interaction requirements
At least 3 back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to cover email verification, password creation, and profile details.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation after the chatbot confirms that the account setup is complete and provides a summary of the created account.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully guided the user through email verification, password creation, and profile information collection, and confirmed account creation with a summary containing the user's name, email, and masked phone number.
