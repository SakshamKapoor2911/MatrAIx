# Education Chatbot

## Your situation
You need help finding scholarships. Share your academic profile interests and background.

## Your goal
Get a personalized shortlist of 3-5 scholarships that match my specific academic profile (computer science major, 3.8 GPA, first-generation college student, interested in AI ethics) and a step-by-step plan for applying to the top two.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your academic profile (computer science major, 3.8 GPA, first-generation college student, interested in AI ethics), but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'Check Fastweb or Scholarships.com'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific profile: 'I'm a first-gen CS student with a focus on AI ethics—are there scholarships specifically for that combination?'
- Mention your budget constraint: you have limited time due to a part-time job, so you need scholarships with manageable application requirements (no more than 2 essays or letters of recommendation).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete list of at least 3 scholarships tailored to your profile (first-gen CS major interested in AI ethics) with specific application steps, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific combination of first-gen, CS, and AI ethics interests.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific profile (first-gen, CS, AI ethics) and you left with an actionable shortlist of scholarships and a clear next step for applying to at least one of them.
