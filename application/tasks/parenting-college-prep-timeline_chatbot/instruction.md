# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your son is a high school sophomore and you want to start preparing for college applications. You need a timeline for standardized tests, extracurriculars, and campus visits.

## Your goal
Get a detailed timeline for standardized tests (SAT/ACT), extracurricular planning, and campus visits for a high school sophomore.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Mention that you are starting from scratch and have no prior prep.', '- Ask for specific months/deadlines for each activity.', "- Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'start early' without dates)."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear timeline with specific months for SAT/ACT registration, extracurricular milestones, and campus visit windows, OR after 5 exchanges without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a timeline with specific months (e.g., 'Take PSAT in October of sophomore year, SAT in March of junior year') and actionable steps for extracurriculars (e.g., 'Join 2 clubs by end of sophomore year') and campus visits (e.g., 'Plan visits during spring break of junior year').
