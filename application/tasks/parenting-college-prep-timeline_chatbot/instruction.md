# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your son is a high school sophomore and you want to start preparing for college applications. You need a timeline for standardized tests, extracurriculars, and campus visits.

## Your goal
Create a personalized 2-year timeline for my son's college prep, covering when to take the SAT/ACT, how to build extracurriculars, and when to schedule campus visits.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your son's situation: he's a high school sophomore, good student but not sure about his interests. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to your specific son who hasn't decided on a major yet.
- Mention that you have a limited budget for test prep and campus visits, so you need cost-effective options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a concrete timeline with specific months for test prep, extracurricular milestones, and campus visit windows, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your son's undecided major and budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (sophomore, undecided, limited budget) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, like 'sign up for the PSAT in October' or 'research three local colleges for a visit next spring'.
