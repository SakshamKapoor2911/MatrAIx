# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You need software for a specific task. Share what you are trying to accomplish.

## Your goal
Find a software tool that can automate the conversion of handwritten meeting notes into searchable digital text, with support for multiple handwriting styles and integration with Google Drive.

## Constraints on your behavior
["Start by describing the task: 'I need to convert handwritten notes from my meetings into searchable text.'", 'Withhold specific details about handwriting styles and Google Drive integration until asked.', 'If the chatbot suggests generic OCR tools, push back by mentioning the need for accurate recognition of messy handwriting.', 'Mention a preference for cloud-based solutions and a budget of under $50/month if relevant.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot recommends a specific tool that meets all criteria (handwriting recognition, Google Drive integration, under $50/month) or after 5 chatbot responses.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it recommends a tool (e.g., Evernote, OneNote, or a specialized app like GoodNotes or Notability) that explicitly supports handwriting recognition, integrates with Google Drive, and is within the budget. The recommendation should be actionable (e.g., a specific app name and how to use it).
