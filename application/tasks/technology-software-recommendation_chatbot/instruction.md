# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You need software for a specific task. Share what you are trying to accomplish.

## Your goal
Find a software tool that can automatically organize and tag my collection of 500+ family photos and videos, with facial recognition and location-based grouping, and that runs locally on my Windows PC without requiring cloud upload.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your concrete situation: 'I have over 500 family photos and videos scattered across folders on my Windows PC. Many are duplicates or have no date/location metadata.'
- Don't reveal your specific requirements (facial recognition, local-only, no subscription) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests cloud-based software like Google Photos or Adobe Lightroom, push back: 'I need something that runs entirely offline because I don't want my family's photos stored on someone else's server.'
- Mention that you're willing to pay a one-time fee up to $100, but not a monthly subscription.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific local software (e.g., PhotoMove, DigiKam, or XnView MP) that supports facial recognition and offline use, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your requirements for local-only operation and facial recognition.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific needs (local-only, facial recognition, one-time fee under $100) and you left with an actionable recommendation you could download and try.
