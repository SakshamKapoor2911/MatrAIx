# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a first date coming up and want it to go well. Share how you met.

## Your goal
Get advice on how to make a great impression on a first date, specifically regarding conversation topics and body language, based on your unique meeting story.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing how you met (e.g., at a friend's party, bonded over a shared hobby like board games), but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'be yourself' or 'ask open-ended questions', push back and ask how that applies to your specific situation (e.g., the fact that you already know you share a hobby, or that you're both shy).
- Mention that you're a bit nervous and want to avoid awkward silences, especially since you've only met once before.
- If the chatbot suggests topics, ask how to transition naturally from the hobby you already discussed to deeper topics.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific, actionable tip for transitioning from your shared hobby to more personal topics, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific meeting story (e.g., the board game hobby) or your nervousness about silences.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific meeting story (e.g., the board game you played) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific question to ask or a way to bring up a new topic naturally.
