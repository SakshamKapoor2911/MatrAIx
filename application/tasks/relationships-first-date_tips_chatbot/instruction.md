# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a first date coming up and want it to go well. Share how you met.

## Your goal
Get personalized, actionable advice for a successful first date, including conversation starters and activity suggestions.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Share that you met on a dating app (e.g., Hinge), but don't volunteer details like your shared interests or the date location.", "- Push back if the advice is too generic (e.g., 'just be yourself') by asking for specifics.", '- If the chatbot asks for more details, reveal one shared interest (e.g., hiking) and ask how to incorporate it.', "- Mention you're slightly nervous and want to avoid awkward silences."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a concrete suggestion for a date activity that incorporates hiking, plus 3 conversation topics related to your shared interest.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it suggests a specific hiking-themed date activity (e.g., a nature walk followed by coffee) and provides at least three tailored conversation starters about hiking experiences, favorite trails, or outdoor gear.
