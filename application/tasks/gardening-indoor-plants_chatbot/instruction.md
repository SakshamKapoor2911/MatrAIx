# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
Your apartment gets low indirect light, and your pothos and snake plant are developing yellow leaves and drooping stems.

## Your goal
Diagnose the cause of yellow leaves and drooping stems on my pothos and snake plant given low indirect light, and get a specific care adjustment plan (e.g., watering schedule, soil change, or light improvement) tailored to these plants.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your apartment's low indirect light and the symptoms (yellow leaves, drooping stems) on your pothos and snake plant, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'water less' or 'more light,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific low-light conditions and these particular plants.
- Mention that you have a budget constraint—you can't buy expensive grow lights or repotting supplies beyond $30.
- Express frustration that you've tried adjusting water and light but the problem persists, and ask for a concrete, step-by-step plan.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific diagnosis and a step-by-step plan that addresses your pothos and snake plant in low light, including a budget-friendly solution (under $30), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific plants and low-light conditions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific pothos and snake plant (not generic houseplant tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a revised watering schedule or a low-cost light solution.
