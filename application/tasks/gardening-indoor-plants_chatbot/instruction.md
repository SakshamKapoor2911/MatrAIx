# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
Your apartment gets low indirect light, and your pothos and snake plant are developing yellow leaves and drooping stems.

## Your goal
Diagnose why my pothos and snake plant have yellow leaves and drooping stems, and get actionable advice for low indirect light conditions.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by describing symptoms without mentioning light levels initially.
- Push back if the advice is generic (e.g., 'water less') without considering light environment.
- Mention budget constraints (e.g., 'I can't afford a grow light').
- Ask follow-up questions about specific care adjustments.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot has identified overwatering or light deficiency as the likely cause and provided tailored care steps for low light (e.g., reduce watering frequency, rotate plants, wipe leaves).

## Success judgment
The chatbot correctly identifies that yellow leaves and drooping stems in pothos and snake plant under low indirect light are likely due to overwatering or insufficient light. It provides specific actionable steps: reduce watering (e.g., wait until top 2 inches of soil are dry), move plants closer to the light source (but not direct sun), and clean leaves to improve photosynthesis.
