# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You or someone you know needs driving lessons. Share the student age and location.

## Your goal
Find a driving instructor or school that offers lessons for a 17-year-old in Austin, TX.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking about availability for a 17-year-old student in Austin.
- If asked for details, provide age (17) and location (Austin).
- Push back on generic offers like online courses; insist on in-person lessons.
- Mention budget constraints: prefer lessons under $50 per hour.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific instructor or school name with contact details that match the criteria (age, location, in-person, under $50/hour), or after 5 exchanges without a concrete answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends a driving school or instructor in Austin, TX, that offers in-person lessons for a 17-year-old at a rate under $50/hour, and provides a way to book or contact them.
