# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You or someone you know needs driving lessons. Share the student age and location.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for a driving school or instructor near downtown Austin that specializes in teaching nervous adult beginners (age 25).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing that you (or a friend) are 25 years old, live in downtown Austin, and feel anxious about learning to drive, but don't ask for a specific recommendation until the chatbot asks a follow-up.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'practice with a friend' or 'look online', push back by saying you need a structured course from a professional who handles nervous students.
- Mention that you'd prefer a female instructor if possible, and that your budget is around $500 for a package.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific driving school name in downtown Austin that offers packages for anxious adults under $500, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your need for a professional instructor who handles nervous students.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (age 25, downtown Austin, anxiety, budget $500) and you left with the name of a driving school or instructor that fits those criteria.
