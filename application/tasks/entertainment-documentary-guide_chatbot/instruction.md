# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You're fascinated by ocean life and climate change and want to watch a documentary series that covers both scientific research and conservation efforts.

## Your goal
Find a documentary series that covers both ocean life and climate change research and conservation efforts, with a preference for recent releases (post-2020) and a focus on actionable steps for viewers.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially ask for general recommendations without specifying preferred length or platform; wait for the chatbot to ask clarifying questions.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests overly broad or generic titles (e.g., 'Blue Planet' without mentioning climate change).
- Mention budget constraints only if the chatbot recommends a paid service; then state preference for free or low-cost options.
- If the chatbot provides a list, ask for details on how each series addresses conservation efforts specifically.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides at least three specific documentary series that meet the criteria (ocean life, climate change research, conservation, post-2020) with brief explanations of their conservation focus, OR after 5 chatbot responses without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends at least three documentary series that are explicitly about ocean life and climate change (e.g., 'Our Planet: Coastal Seas', 'Chasing Coral', 'Seaspiracy') and mentions how each addresses conservation efforts (e.g., highlighting coral reef restoration, overfishing solutions). The chatbot should also offer information on where to watch them (platform and cost).
