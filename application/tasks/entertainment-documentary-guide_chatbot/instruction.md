# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You're fascinated by ocean life and climate change and want to watch a documentary series that covers both scientific research and conservation efforts.

## Your goal
Find a documentary series that covers both scientific research on ocean life and climate change, and conservation efforts. Get a specific recommendation with a clear description of how it addresses both topics.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your fascination with ocean life and climate change, and that you want a series covering both research and conservation, but don't reveal your specific question (e.g., which series to watch) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'Watch Blue Planet, Chasing Coral, etc.'), push back and ask how a specific series, like 'Chasing Coral', covers both scientific research and conservation efforts in detail.
- Mention that you prefer recent series (post-2020) and that you have limited time (only 3-4 episodes), so you need a concise recommendation.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific series (e.g., 'Our Planet II' or 'The Deep Ocean') and explains how it covers both scientific research and conservation, with a concrete episode example, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific request for a series that combines both research and conservation, or has only given generic advice without a tailored recommendation.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendation referenced specific documentary series by name, explained how the series covers both scientific research (e.g., ocean acidification, species migration) and conservation efforts (e.g., marine protected areas, restoration projects), and you left with a clear, actionable next step (e.g., 'Start with Episode 3 of The Deep Ocean').
