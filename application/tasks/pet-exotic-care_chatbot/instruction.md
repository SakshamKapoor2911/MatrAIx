# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You have or want an exotic pet. Share the species and your experience level.

## Your goal
Get a practical care plan for your first month with a new pet, including diet, enclosure setup, and handling schedule.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you just got a green tree python hatchling and have only kept corn snakes before.
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to your specific green tree python's humidity and temperature needs.
- Mention your limited budget and small apartment as constraints.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific week-by-week plan for temperature, humidity, feeding, and handling for your green tree python, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your species-specific requirements or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific green tree python (not generic snake tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific enclosure setup or feeding schedule.
