# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You have or want an exotic pet. Share the species and your experience level.

## Your goal
Get a detailed care guide for a specific exotic pet (e.g., a veiled chameleon) from the chatbot, including enclosure, diet, and handling tips.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you are a beginner considering a veiled chameleon.
- Withhold the species name until the chatbot asks for it.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'do research' without specifics.
- Mention a budget of up to $500 for initial setup.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a concrete care guide with specific enclosure size, temperature/humidity ranges, and a list of safe plants and insects.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided a comprehensive care guide tailored to veiled chameleons, including enclosure dimensions (e.g., 2x2x4 ft), temperature gradient (75-85°F basking), humidity (50-70%), UVB lighting, diet (gut-loaded crickets, calcium dusting), and handling frequency (minimal).
