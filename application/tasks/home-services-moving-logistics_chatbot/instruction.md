# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning a move. Share your move distance and timeframe.

## Your goal
Get a checklist of tasks for a 2-week local move (within the same city), including packing strategy, utility transfer timeline, and recommendations for hiring movers vs. doing it yourself.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: moving 10 miles within the same city, with a 2-week deadline, but don't yet ask for specific advice. - If the chatbot gives generic tips (e.g., 'start packing early'), push back by asking how to prioritize given your short timeframe and specific items like a fragile glass dining table and a heavy treadmill. - Mention that you have a budget of $500 for moving help and need to decide whether to hire movers or rent a truck. - Keep responses concise and focused on your concrete constraints.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a step-by-step plan that addresses the glass table, treadmill, and utility transfer within your budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific items (glass table, treadmill) or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific items (glass table, treadmill) and budget ($500), and you left with a clear first step (e.g., book a truck rental or schedule movers) and a timeline for utility transfers.
