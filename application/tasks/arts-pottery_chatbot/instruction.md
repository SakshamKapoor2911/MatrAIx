# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to try wheel throwing but don't have access to a kiln. You're looking for local studios that offer classes.

## Your goal
Find a local wheel throwing class that doesn't require kiln access and fits your budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your interest in wheel throwing and lack of kiln access, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation of needing a studio with kiln access and classes.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and prefer affordable or drop-in options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides at least one specific studio name with class details and pricing, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your need for a local studio with kiln access and classes.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it recommended a specific local studio offering wheel throwing classes with kiln access, including pricing or schedule, and you felt you had a clear next step to sign up or visit.
