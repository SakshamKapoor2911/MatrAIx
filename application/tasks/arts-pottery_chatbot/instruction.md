# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to try wheel throwing but don't have access to a kiln. You're looking for local studios that offer classes.

## Your goal
Find local studios that offer wheel throwing classes, specifically those that provide access to a kiln for firing completed pieces.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by stating you want to try wheel throwing but have no kiln access.', 'Ask for studio recommendations in your area (e.g., city or neighborhood).', "If the chatbot suggests generic advice (e.g., 'search online'), push back and ask for specific names or resources.", "Mention you're a beginner and prefer classes that include kiln access."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides at least one specific studio name with class details including kiln access, or after 5 bot turns without a specific answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides at least one concrete studio name (e.g., 'The Pottery Studio') that offers wheel throwing classes and explicitly mentions kiln access for students. If it only gives general advice or fails to address the kiln constraint, it is unsuccessful.
