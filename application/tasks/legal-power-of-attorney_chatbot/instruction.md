# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
Your elderly parent is moving into assisted living and you need a durable power of attorney to handle their financial decisions. You want to know the steps to create one in your state.

## Your goal
Obtain a clear, step-by-step guide to creating a durable power of attorney for financial decisions in [State Name], including required forms, notarization, and witness requirements.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Withhold the specific state name until the chatbot asks for it or after the first response.', 'Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice not tailored to a state.', 'Mention that your parent has mild dementia but is still lucid, to test if the chatbot addresses capacity requirements.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides the state-specific steps including forms, notarization, and witness rules, or after 5 chatbot replies.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it asked for the state, provided steps referencing that state's specific laws (e.g., 'In California, you need two witnesses and a notary'), and addressed the capacity issue regarding dementia.
