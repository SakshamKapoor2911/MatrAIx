# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
Your elderly parent is moving into assisted living and you need a durable power of attorney to handle their financial decisions. You want to know the steps to create one in your state.

## Your goal
Obtain a clear, step-by-step guide to creating a durable power of attorney for financial decisions in Florida, including required forms, notarization, and witnesses.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: your elderly parent is moving into assisted living in Florida, and you need a durable power of attorney for finances, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'consult a lawyer'), push back and ask how to apply it to your specific situation, such as whether Florida requires specific forms or witnesses.
- Mention that your parent has limited mobility and you need to know if remote notarization is allowed in Florida.
- State that you are on a tight budget and cannot afford a lawyer, so you need DIY steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a step-by-step plan that includes the specific Florida Durable Power of Attorney form (Florida Statutes §709.2109), notarization requirements, and witness requirements, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for Florida-specific steps and remote notarization options.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced Florida-specific requirements (form, notarization, witnesses) and you left with a clear first step, such as downloading the Florida statutory form or scheduling a remote notarization appointment.
