# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You have accumulated 50,000 points across two airline loyalty programs and want to know how to combine them for a business-class flight to Tokyo.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for combining points from two airline loyalty programs to book a business-class flight to Tokyo, including specific steps and estimated point requirements.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you have 50,000 points split across two airline loyalty programs (e.g., 30,000 in United MileagePlus and 20,000 in American Airlines AAdvantage), but don't reveal your desire to combine them until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'transfer points to partners'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific United and American points.
- Mention that you want to avoid high fees and are flexible with travel dates.
- Keep the conversation focused on combining points, not on earning more.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific step-by-step plan for combining your United and American points to book a business-class flight to Tokyo, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific points from United and American Airlines.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific United MileagePlus and American Airlines AAdvantage points (not generic tips about transferring points) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific partner airline to transfer to or a specific booking strategy.
