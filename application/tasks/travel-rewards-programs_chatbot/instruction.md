# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You have accumulated 50,000 points across two airline loyalty programs and want to know how to combine them for a business-class flight to Tokyo.

## Your goal
Combine 50,000 points from two airline loyalty programs to book a business-class flight to Tokyo.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Do not reveal the specific airlines or point balances unless asked.', '- Push back if the chatbot suggests economy or one-way options.', '- Mention you want a round-trip business-class flight.', '- Ask about transfer partners or point pooling options.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear, actionable plan to combine the points for a business-class round-trip to Tokyo, or after 5 chatbot responses.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identifies the two airline programs (after you disclose them), explains how to combine the 50,000 points, and provides a feasible route or booking method for a business-class round-trip to Tokyo.
