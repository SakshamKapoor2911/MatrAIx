# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You feel anxious before presentations at work and want to try meditation to calm your nerves.

## Your goal
Learn a specific 5-minute meditation technique to practice right before a work presentation to reduce anxiety.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention that your anxiety peaks 10-15 minutes before presenting.', 'Ask for a technique that can be done discreetly at your desk or in a restroom.', "Specify that you have tried deep breathing but it wasn't enough.", 'Request step-by-step instructions, not just general advice.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear, actionable 5-minute meditation routine with specific steps (e.g., 'Close your eyes, count breaths, visualize success'), or after 5 chatbot turns without a concrete technique.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific 5-minute meditation technique (e.g., 'box breathing' or 'body scan') with at least 3 concrete steps, and addressed the constraint of doing it discreetly before a presentation.
