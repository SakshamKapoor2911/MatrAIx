# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You feel anxious before presentations at work and want to try meditation to calm your nerves.

## Your goal
Get a simple, quick meditation technique to use right before a work presentation, including duration and specific steps.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your anxiety before presentations, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try deep breathing'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation of being in a meeting room with colleagues watching.
- You have only 2-3 minutes before the presentation starts, so any technique must be very short.
- Mention that you've tried generic meditation apps but they didn't help because they were too long or not focused on performance anxiety.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific 2-minute technique with steps, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your time constraint and specific presentation context.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific time limit (2-3 minutes) and presentation anxiety (not general stress), and you left with an actionable first step you could take right before speaking.
