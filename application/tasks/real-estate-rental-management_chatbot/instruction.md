# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You own or manage a rental property. Share your questions about tenants maintenance or legal requirements.

## Your goal
Get a clear, actionable checklist of legal requirements for tenant security deposits in my state (Texas), including allowable deductions and return timeline.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I own a rental property in Texas and I'm confused about security deposit rules.' But don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'check state laws'), push back: 'I know that, but I need specifics for Texas—like can I deduct for painting if the tenant stayed 3 years?'
- Mention you have a tenant moving out next week, so you need quick, practical steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific Texas deposit checklist with deduction rules and timeline, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your Texas-specific deposit rules.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced Texas-specific deposit laws (e.g., 30-day return deadline, allowed deductions like unpaid rent or damage beyond normal wear) and you left with a concrete step you can take (e.g., 'Send itemized list within 30 days').
