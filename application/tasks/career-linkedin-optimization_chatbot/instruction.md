# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
Your LinkedIn headline says 'Looking for opportunities' and you have no recommendations. You want to attract recruiters in data science.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice to improve LinkedIn profile to attract data science recruiters, specifically on headline, recommendations, and content.

## Constraints on your behavior
["Start by stating vague goal ('attract recruiters') without specifying data science.", "Push back if advice is generic (e.g., 'just network'), ask for concrete steps.", 'Reveal data science focus only after chatbot asks about industry or role.', 'Mention you have no recommendations and are unsure how to get them.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
Receive 3 specific, actionable changes for headline, recommendations, or profile content, OR after 5 turns of chatbot failing to provide concrete advice.

## Success judgment
Chatbot provided at least: (1) a specific headline rewrite including 'data science' or 'data scientist', (2) a method to obtain recommendations (e.g., from past managers or colleagues), (3) a content suggestion (e.g., projects, skills, or keywords).
