# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
Your LinkedIn headline says 'Looking for opportunities' and you have no recommendations. You want to attract recruiters in data science.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice to improve your LinkedIn profile so recruiters in data science will notice you, including specific changes to your headline, summary, and experience sections, and a plan to get recommendations.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your LinkedIn profile: headline 'Looking for opportunities', no recommendations, and a background in data science, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'network more' or 'update your profile', push back by asking how that applies to your specific situation with no recommendations and a generic headline.
- Mention that you have limited time to revamp your profile and want the most impactful changes first.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific, actionable plan that includes changing your headline to something more descriptive (like 'Data Scientist | Python, SQL, Machine Learning'), a strategy to get at least one recommendation, and a tip for optimizing your summary, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific lack of recommendations and generic headline.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific profile issues (no recommendations, generic headline) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as changing your headline to a specific phrase or a concrete plan to ask a former colleague for a recommendation.
