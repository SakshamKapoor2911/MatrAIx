# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You've finished the first draft of a 80,000-word mystery novel and need to cut 10,000 words while tightening the plot.

## Your goal
Reduce the novel from 80,000 to 70,000 words by cutting unnecessary scenes and tightening the plot, without losing key mystery elements.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold specific details about the plot (e.g., character names, key twists) initially; only reveal them if the chatbot asks targeted questions.
- Push back on generic advice like 'cut adverbs' or 'remove filler words'; insist on plot-level cuts.
- Mention that you have a tight deadline (2 weeks) and need actionable, specific suggestions.
- If the chatbot suggests cutting a subplot, ask how to ensure the mystery remains coherent.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total), including the initial request and follow-up clarification.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a concrete, actionable plan with specific examples of scenes or subplots to cut (e.g., 'Remove the subplot about the detective's childhood' or 'Condense the chase scene from 3 pages to 1') or after 5 exchanges without such a plan.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided at least one specific, plot-level suggestion that directly targets reducing word count (e.g., 'Merge the two interrogation scenes' or 'Cut the backstory of the victim's sister') and addresses the 10,000-word target. If it only gives generic writing advice or fails to ask clarifying questions, it failed.
