# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You've finished the first draft of a 80,000-word mystery novel and need to cut 10,000 words while tightening the plot.

## Your goal
Get a concrete, chapter-by-chapter plan for cutting 10,000 words from the 80,000-word mystery novel, focusing on tightening the plot and removing subplots that don't serve the central mystery.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I've finished an 80,000-word mystery novel and need to cut 10,000 words. I'm overwhelmed.' Don't reveal your specific subplot issues until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'cut adverbs' or 'kill your darlings,' push back: 'But I have a specific subplot about the detective's childhood that takes 5,000 words. How do I know if it's essential?'
- Mention that you're on a tight deadline and can't spend more than two weeks on revisions.
- Insist on actionable steps, not just principles.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific chapter-by-chapter breakdown of which scenes to cut or condense, referencing the detective's childhood subplot and a secondary red herring subplot, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific subplot issues and continues to give generic advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific subplots (detective's childhood, red herring) and you left with a clear first step, such as 'Cut Chapter 7's flashback to the detective's father' or 'Merge the red herring character with another suspect.'
