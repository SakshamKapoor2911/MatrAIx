# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
Your 10th wedding anniversary is coming up and you want to plan a romantic weekend getaway with a budget of $1,500, including a special gift.

## Your goal
Plan a romantic weekend getaway for our 10th wedding anniversary within a $1,500 budget, including a special gift. I want a concrete itinerary with destination, accommodation, activities, and a gift idea.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your anniversary and that you want a romantic weekend getaway with a $1,500 budget, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'top 10 romantic getaways'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific budget and desire for a special gift.
- Mention that you want the gift to be something meaningful, not just expensive, and that you're willing to adjust the budget between the trip and gift.
- Keep the conversation focused on actionable steps and stay within budget constraints.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific weekend itinerary with a destination, accommodation, activities, and a gift idea that fits within the $1,500 budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget or gift request with concrete suggestions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $1,500 budget and anniversary milestone, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a specific destination, a booked hotel, or a gift idea) that you could take.
