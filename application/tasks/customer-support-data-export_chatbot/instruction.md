# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You're leaving an online service and need to download your account history, including past orders and personal information, before the account is closed. You want the data in a CSV format.

## Your goal
Download a CSV export of my account history, including past orders and personal information, before closing my account.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by stating you need to download your data before closing your account, but don't specify CSV or the exact data types until asked.
- If the chatbot gives generic instructions (e.g., 'Go to settings'), push back and ask how to get specifically past orders and personal info in CSV format.
- Mention you have a deadline (e.g., need the data by end of week) to add urgency.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a direct link or clear steps to download a CSV with past orders and personal info, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed CSV format or the specific data types.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it gave specific instructions for downloading past orders and personal information in CSV format, and you left with a clear action (e.g., a link or steps) you could take immediately.
