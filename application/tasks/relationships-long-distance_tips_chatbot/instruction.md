# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You are in or entering a long-distance relationship. Share the distance and your concerns.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice on maintaining a long-distance relationship with a 1,000-mile gap and a 6-month timeline before reuniting.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating the distance (1,000 miles) and timeline (6 months).
- Express concerns about communication frequency and trust issues.
- Push back if the advice is too generic (e.g., 'just communicate').
- Mention budget constraints for visits (e.g., can only afford one trip per month).

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a specific plan covering communication schedule, trust-building activities, and budget-friendly visit tips, or after 5 exchanges without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it offers a concrete weekly communication schedule (e.g., 3 video calls, daily texts), trust-building exercises (e.g., shared online activities), and cost-saving visit strategies (e.g., booking flights mid-week, using travel rewards).
