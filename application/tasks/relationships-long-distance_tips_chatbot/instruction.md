# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You are in or entering a long-distance relationship. Share the distance and your concerns.

## Your goal
Get advice on how to maintain intimacy and connection with your partner who lives 1,500 miles away, given your specific concerns about mismatched communication styles and upcoming 3-month separation due to your job.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're in a long-distance relationship with your partner Alex, who lives in Chicago while you're in Denver, and you worry about growing apart during your upcoming 3-month field assignment in rural Montana with limited cell service.
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice. For example, start with 'We're doing okay but I'm nervous about the next few months' and wait for the chatbot to probe.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'schedule regular calls, send gifts'), push back and mention that you have limited cell service in Montana and Alex prefers texting over calling, so generic advice won't work.
- Mention that you have a tight budget due to saving for a visit, so expensive solutions like frequent flights or care packages are not feasible.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a concrete plan that addresses the limited cell service and mismatched communication preferences, including specific ideas like scheduled letter-writing or using a satellite messenger, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (rural Montana, no cell service, Alex prefers texting, tight budget).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (rural Montana, limited cell service, Alex's texting preference, budget) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific communication schedule or a low-cost tool to use.
