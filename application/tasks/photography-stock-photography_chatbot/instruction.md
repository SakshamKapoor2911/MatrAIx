# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a portfolio of high-quality travel photos from Southeast Asia and want to start selling them on Shutterstock for passive income.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step plan to upload and optimize my Southeast Asia travel photos on Shutterstock, including keywording, categorization, and initial pricing strategy.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your portfolio of high-quality travel photos from Southeast Asia (e.g., landscapes, street scenes, temples), but don't reveal your specific question about selling on Shutterstock until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just upload and add keywords'), push back and ask how to apply it to your specific photos, like a night market in Chiang Mai or a sunrise at Angkor Wat.
- Mention that you have about 200 photos and want to focus on a niche to stand out, but you're unsure which niche (e.g., architecture, street life) has higher demand.
- Budget is tight; you can't spend much on tools or subscriptions initially.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete first step (e.g., 'start by keywording 10 of your best photos using this template') that addresses your specific photos, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific photos or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific photos (e.g., mentioned keywording for 'Chiang Mai night market' or 'Angkor Wat sunrise') and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a keywording template or a niche recommendation.
