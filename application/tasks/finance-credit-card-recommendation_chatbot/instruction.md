# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You travel twice a year for vacation and spend $500/month on groceries. Your credit score is 720 and you want cash back rewards.

## Your goal
Identify a cash back credit card with no annual fee that maximizes rewards on groceries and travel, and get a comparison of at least two specific card options.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your spending habits: $500/month on groceries and two vacations per year, but don't reveal your credit score or that you want cash back until asked.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'look for a card with high cash back,' push back by asking how it applies to your specific spending on groceries and travel.
- Mention that you want to avoid annual fees and have a credit score of 720, but only if the chatbot asks about your credit or budget.
- If the chatbot recommends a card with an annual fee, ask about fee waivers or alternatives.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a shortlist of two specific cash back cards with details on grocery and travel rewards and the chatbot confirms no annual fee, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided specific card names or ignored your $500 grocery and travel spending constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $500/month grocery spending and two vacations per year (not generic tips) and you left with a clear comparison of at least two cash back cards with no annual fee.
