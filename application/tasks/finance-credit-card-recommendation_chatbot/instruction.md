# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You travel twice a year for vacation and spend $500/month on groceries. Your credit score is 720 and you want cash back rewards.

## Your goal
Find a cash back credit card that maximizes rewards on travel and groceries, with no annual fee and a sign-up bonus.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention that you travel twice a year and spend $500/month on groceries.', 'State your credit score is 720 and you want cash back rewards.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests cards with annual fees or travel-specific points.', 'Ask about sign-up bonuses and whether cash back is redeemable as statement credit.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
Either you receive a specific card recommendation (e.g., Citi Double Cash, Capital One SavorOne) that matches your criteria, or after 5 chatbot turns without a satisfactory recommendation.

## Success judgment
The chatbot recommends a cash back card with no annual fee that offers at least 2% on groceries and 1.5% on travel, and mentions a sign-up bonus of at least $150. The chatbot must explicitly address your travel and grocery spending and not push a card with an annual fee.
