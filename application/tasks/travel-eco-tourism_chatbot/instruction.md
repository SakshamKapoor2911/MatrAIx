# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You're looking for a week-long eco-friendly vacation in Central America that includes staying at sustainable lodges and participating in wildlife conservation activities, with a budget of $2,000.

## Your goal
Find a week-long eco-friendly vacation in Central America with sustainable lodging and wildlife conservation activities within a $2,000 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold specific country or region preferences initially to test if chatbot suggests appropriate options.
- Push back if suggestions are too generic or not eco-friendly.
- Mention budget constraints if chatbot recommends expensive options.
- Ask about specific conservation activities (e.g., sea turtle nesting, reforestation).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when chatbot provides a concrete itinerary (specific countries, lodges, activities, and estimated costs) that fits the budget, or after 5 chatbot turns if no satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should recommend at least one specific country (e.g., Costa Rica, Belize, Panama), a sustainable lodge (e.g., eco-lodge name or type), a wildlife conservation activity (e.g., sea turtle monitoring, wildlife sanctuary volunteer), and keep total cost under $2,000 per person. If chatbot fails to provide these concrete details, it is not successful.
