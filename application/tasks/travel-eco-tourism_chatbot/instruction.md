# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You're looking for a week-long eco-friendly vacation in Central America that includes staying at sustainable lodges and participating in wildlife conservation activities, with a budget of $2,000.

## Your goal
Find a week-long eco-friendly vacation in Central America with sustainable lodges and wildlife conservation activities, staying within a $2,000 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your desire for an eco-friendly trip but don’t specify budget or location until asked.
- If the chatbot suggests generic destinations like 'Costa Rica' without specific lodges or costs, push back and ask for details on sustainable options and how they fit your budget.
- Mention your $2,000 budget only after the chatbot gives a vague or expensive suggestion.
- Emphasize that you want hands-on conservation activities (e.g., sea turtle monitoring, reforestation) not just nature tours.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific itinerary with named sustainable lodges (e.g., Lapa Rios in Costa Rica or Pacuare Lodge in Costa Rica) and conservation activities (e.g., sea turtle nesting patrols), all within $2,000, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget or specific activity preferences.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it recommended a specific country or region (e.g., Costa Rica's Osa Peninsula or Nicaragua's Reserva Biológica Indio Maíz), named at least one sustainable lodge (e.g., Selva Bananito Lodge) and a concrete conservation activity (e.g., participating in a jaguar monitoring program), and gave a realistic cost breakdown showing the trip is possible under $2,000.
