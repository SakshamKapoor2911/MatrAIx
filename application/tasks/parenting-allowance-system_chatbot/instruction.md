# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your 8-year-old daughter wants an allowance to learn about saving and spending. You want to set up a system that ties allowance to chores and teaches budgeting.

## Your goal
Set up a chore-based allowance system for my 8-year-old daughter that teaches her budgeting and saving.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking for general advice on allowance systems.
- When given generic advice, push back by specifying you want chores tied to allowance.
- Mention your daughter is 8 years old and needs simple, concrete steps.
- Ask about handling chores not completed or spending mistakes.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific plan that includes: a list of age-appropriate chores, a suggested weekly allowance amount, and a simple budgeting method (e.g., three jars for save/spend/give).

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provides a concrete plan with: 1) a list of at least 3 chores suitable for an 8-year-old, 2) a specific weekly allowance amount (e.g., $5), and 3) a clear budgeting method (e.g., three jars labeled Save, Spend, Give) with instructions on how to use them.
