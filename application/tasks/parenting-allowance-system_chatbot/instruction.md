# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your 8-year-old daughter wants an allowance to learn about saving and spending. You want to set up a system that ties allowance to chores and teaches budgeting.

## Your goal
Get a concrete system: a list of age-appropriate chores for an 8-year-old, a weekly allowance amount tied to those chores, and a simple budget plan (e.g., three jars for save/spend/give) that we can start next week.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 8-year-old daughter's request for an allowance, but don't reveal your specific question about chores or budgeting until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try a three-jar system'), push back and ask how it applies to your daughter's age and your desire to tie allowance to chores, not just handouts.
- Mention that your daughter is not very motivated by money yet, and you're worried about making chores feel transactional. Ask how to balance responsibility and reward.
- Keep the conversation practical: you want a simple system you can implement immediately, not long-term theories.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific chore list with suggested allowance amounts and a simple budget method (like jars), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific concerns about chore-allowance connection and age-appropriateness.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (8-year-old, chore-based allowance, not motivated by money) and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., a list of 3 chores and a jar budget plan).
