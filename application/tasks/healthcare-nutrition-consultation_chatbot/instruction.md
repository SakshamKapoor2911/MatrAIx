# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been diagnosed with high blood pressure and eat fast food most days. You want to lower your sodium intake without giving up flavor.

## Your goal
Get a practical plan for reducing sodium in fast food meals while keeping them flavorful, including specific menu swaps at 2-3 common fast food chains and a list of low-sodium condiments or spices to carry.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your fast food habit and high blood pressure diagnosis, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'cook at home' or 'choose grilled chicken,' push back and ask how to apply that to your specific situation (e.g., 'But I'm always on the go, what do I order at McDonald's or Taco Bell?').
- Mention you love spicy and savory flavors, and you're worried low-sodium will be bland. Ask for specific brand names of low-sodium hot sauce or seasoning blends.
- Budget is a concern; you can't afford expensive specialty items, so ask for affordable options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a concrete list of 3 menu swaps at specific fast food chains (e.g., McDonald's, Taco Bell, Subway) and a recommendation for a low-sodium hot sauce under $5, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific fast food chains or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific fast food chains (e.g., McDonald's, Taco Bell) and budget (under $5 for condiments), and you left with an actionable first step like 'order the grilled chicken wrap at McDonald's with no added salt and bring my own low-sodium hot sauce.'
