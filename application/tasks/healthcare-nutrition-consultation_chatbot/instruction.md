# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been diagnosed with high blood pressure and eat fast food most days. You want to lower your sodium intake without giving up flavor.

## Your goal
Get 3 specific, flavorful low-sodium fast-food meal options (each under 600mg sodium) and a tip for adding flavor without salt.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you have high blood pressure and eat fast food often.
- Reject generic advice like 'just cook at home' or 'avoid fast food entirely'.
- Push back on suggestions that sacrifice flavor (e.g., plain grilled chicken).
- Mention you like spicy and savory flavors.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive 3 specific meal options with sodium counts and a flavor tip, or after 5 bot messages without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided 3 fast-food meals (e.g., from McDonald's, Taco Bell, Subway) each with sodium under 600mg and a flavor-enhancing tip (e.g., using hot sauce, vinegar, spices) that does not rely on salt.
