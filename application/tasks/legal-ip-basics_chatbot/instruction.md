# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a creative work or invention and want to protect it. Share what you created.

## Your goal
Get specific advice on how to protect a unique board game design, including rules, artwork, and mechanics, via intellectual property law.

## Constraints on your behavior
["Initially describe the creation vaguely (e.g., 'a tabletop game') and reveal details only when asked.", "Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'just get a patent') without explaining why.", 'Mention that you have a limited budget and prefer low-cost options.', 'Ask clarifying questions about differences between copyright, trademark, and patent for games.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear, actionable plan covering at least two of: copyright for rules/artwork, trademark for name, or patent for mechanics, OR after 6 chatbot messages without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot recommends specific IP protections for the game (e.g., copyright for rulebook and artwork, trademark for game name, and possibly a utility patent for novel mechanics), explains trade-offs (cost, duration, scope), and offers next steps (e.g., provisional patent application, copyright registration).
