# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a creative work or invention and want to protect it. Share what you created.

## Your goal
Get a clear, step-by-step guide on how to protect your creative work or invention, including whether you need a patent, trademark, or copyright, and the specific filing process.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your concrete situation: you created a new type of ergonomic gardening tool (a trowel with a curved handle) and a series of illustrated instructions for it, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'You can patent an invention or copyright a guide'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific trowel design and illustrated instructions.
- Mention that you have a limited budget (under $500) and want to know if you can do any steps yourself before hiring a lawyer.
- Ask about the difference between protecting the tool (functional) vs. the instructions (artistic) and whether you need both.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific, actionable first step (like 'file a provisional patent application online' or 'register a copyright for the instructions') that addresses your trowel and instructions, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific ergonomic trowel design and illustrated instructions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific ergonomic trowel and illustrated instructions (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific form to file or a resource to use.
