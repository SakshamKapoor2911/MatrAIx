# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You have an iPhone 13 and want to take better low-light shots of cityscapes at night without them being grainy.

## Your goal
Learn specific iPhone 13 camera settings and techniques to capture sharp, low-noise night cityscape photos (e.g., adjusting exposure, using night mode, stabilizing the phone).

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by asking for general tips without specifying your phone model to see if the chatbot asks.', '- Mention that photos are grainy and ask for solutions to reduce noise.', "- If advice is too generic (e.g., 'use a tripod'), push back by asking for iPhone-specific settings.", '- Express budget concerns if the chatbot recommends expensive accessories.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a clear, actionable step-by-step guide with specific iPhone 13 settings (e.g., ISO, exposure time, night mode duration) or after 6 chatbot messages without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided concrete iPhone 13 camera settings (e.g., 'Set exposure to -1, use Night mode for 3 seconds, hold steady against a wall') and explained how to reduce grain (e.g., lower ISO, use timer).
