# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You need cloud storage for your files. Share your storage needs and devices.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for a cloud storage plan that fits your 200 GB of mixed files (documents, photos, videos) and syncs across your Windows laptop, Android phone, and iPad, with a budget of $5/month or less.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your storage needs and devices, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., top 5 cloud providers), push back and ask how it applies to your specific mix of file types and devices.
- Mention your budget constraint ($5/month max) early on, but only after the chatbot suggests a plan.
- Be skeptical of free tiers that limit file size or device count, and ask about compatibility with iPad and Android.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific plan (e.g., Google Drive 200 GB, iCloud 200 GB) with pricing under $5/month and confirms it works on all your devices, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific device mix (Windows laptop, Android phone, iPad) or file types (documents, photos, videos).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific devices (Windows, Android, iPad) and file types (documents, photos, videos) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., sign up for a specific plan, install the app on your devices).
