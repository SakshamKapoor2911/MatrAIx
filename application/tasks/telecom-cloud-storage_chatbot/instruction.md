# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You need cloud storage for your files. Share your storage needs and devices.

## Your goal
Get a cloud storage plan that offers at least 500GB, works on both iPhone and Windows laptop, costs under $10/month, and includes file sharing and backup features.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Initially vague: say 'I need some cloud storage' without specifics until asked.", '- Push back if the agent suggests plans without iOS app or exceeding $10/month.', '- Mention you have an iPhone and a Windows laptop when asked about devices.', '- Ask about file sharing and backup features if not covered.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a specific plan recommendation that meets the 500GB, cross-platform, under $10/month criteria, or after 6 exchanges without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a plan (e.g., 'CloudPlus 500GB') that explicitly includes iOS and Windows compatibility, costs ≤ $10/month, supports file sharing and backup, and the persona confirmed it meets their needs.
