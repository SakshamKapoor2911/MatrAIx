# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You face an important decision. Share the options and what makes it difficult.

## Your goal
Get structured advice on how to decide between two career paths: a stable corporate job with clear growth and a risky startup role with high potential upside.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially describe the dilemma vaguely without revealing key details (e.g., 'I have two job offers but can't decide').
- Only after the chatbot asks clarifying questions, disclose the specific options and constraints (e.g., salary difference, commute, growth prospects).
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'follow your passion' without concrete reasoning.
- Mention that you have a family to support and a limited time to decide (3 days).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a decision-making framework or specific pros/cons list that addresses the trade-offs between the two options, or after 5 turns if no actionable advice is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it helps identify at least three concrete factors to compare (e.g., salary, work-life balance, career growth) and suggests a method (e.g., weighted decision matrix) tailored to the user's specific constraints (family, deadline).
