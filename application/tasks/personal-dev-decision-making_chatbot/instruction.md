# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You face an important decision. Share the options and what makes it difficult.

## Your goal
To articulate the two options you're torn between for your next career move and get an outside perspective on which trade-offs matter most, so you can make a decision with less anxiety.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current job as a mid-level graphic designer at a large agency, but don't reveal the specific options until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'follow your passion.'
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'make a pros and cons list,' push back by explaining that both options have intangible benefits (e.g., stability vs. creative freedom) that are hard to compare.
- Mention you have a family to support (two kids, spouse with part-time job) and a mortgage, so financial risk is a real concern.
- Keep the conversation focused on your specific dilemma: Option A is a senior designer role at a stable corporate firm (higher salary, less creative control) vs. Option B is a lead designer at a tiny startup (lower pay, equity, more creative input, but high risk of failure).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you feel you have a clear next step (e.g., 'I'll try to negotiate a part-time trial at the startup') OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed the specific trade-off between financial stability and creative fulfillment.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific options (senior corporate designer vs. startup lead designer) and your constraints (family, mortgage, desire for creativity), and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., 'talk to the startup's CEO about growth plans,' 'ask for a flexible schedule at the corporate job').
