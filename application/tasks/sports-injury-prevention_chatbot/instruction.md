# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You play recreational basketball twice a week and often experience ankle sprains. You want warm-up exercises and strengthening routines to prevent future injuries.

## Your goal
Get a personalized warm-up routine and strengthening exercises to prevent ankle sprains during recreational basketball.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention you play basketball twice a week and have recurring ankle sprains.', "Ask for exercises that don't require special equipment (only basic gym gear).", "Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'just stretch') or doesn't target ankles.", 'Mention you have about 10-15 minutes for warm-up before games.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
You receive a specific warm-up routine (with named exercises like ankle circles, calf raises, or balance drills) and strengthening exercises (e.g., resistance band work, single-leg stands) that you can do without special equipment, or after 5 exchanges if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a warm-up routine including at least two specific ankle-strengthening exercises (e.g., alphabet writing with toes, towel curls) and a dynamic warm-up (e.g., leg swings, walking lunges) that can be done in 10-15 minutes without special equipment, and acknowledged your basketball frequency and ankle sprain history.
