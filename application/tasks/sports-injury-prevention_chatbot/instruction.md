# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You play recreational basketball twice a week and often experience ankle sprains. You want warm-up exercises and strengthening routines to prevent future injuries.

## Your goal
Get a personalized warm-up and strengthening routine specifically designed to prevent ankle sprains during recreational basketball, with exercises that can be done at home without gym equipment.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you play rec basketball twice a week and keep spraining your ankle, but don't mention your specific request for warm-up or strengthening until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of ankle exercises (e.g., 'do calf raises and balance exercises'), push back and ask how they apply specifically to your basketball playing and ankle sprains.
- Mention that you have limited equipment (no gym access, just a basketball and a wall) and want exercises that fit into 10 minutes before games.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific warm-up and strengthening routine tailored to basketball ankle sprains that includes exercises like single-leg balance on a pillow or lateral hops, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific ankle sprains or basketball context.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific basketball ankle sprains (not generic ankle strengthening) and you left with an actionable first step you could take before your next game, such as a specific exercise sequence.
