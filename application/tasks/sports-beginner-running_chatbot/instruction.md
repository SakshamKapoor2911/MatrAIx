# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You haven't run since high school and want to start a couch to 5K program. You need shoe recommendations and a gradual schedule to avoid shin splints.

## Your goal
Get shoe recommendations for a beginner runner and a gradual running schedule to avoid shin splints.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you haven't run since high school and want to avoid injuries like shin splints.
- Mention a tight budget (under $100) for shoes.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests overly intense schedules or expensive gear.
- Ask for specifics on how to gradually increase mileage.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific shoe model under $100 and a week-by-week schedule that starts with walk-run intervals.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a concrete shoe model (name and price under $100) and a detailed weekly schedule (e.g., Week 1: 1 min run, 2 min walk repeat 5 times) that gradually increases running time.
