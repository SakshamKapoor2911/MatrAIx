# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You haven't run since high school and want to start a couch to 5K program. You need shoe recommendations and a gradual schedule to avoid shin splints.

## Your goal
Get a beginner-friendly couch to 5K schedule that gradually increases running volume, plus specific shoe recommendations (with examples of brands/models for flat feet or overpronation if relevant) to avoid shin splints.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you haven't run since high school, want to start a couch to 5K program, but are worried about shin splints. Don't reveal your specific need for shoe advice until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just run slowly and stretch'), push back by asking how the advice applies to your specific concern about shin splints and your lack of recent running experience.
- Mention that you have flat feet and a limited budget (under $100 for shoes) when the conversation turns to gear.
- Ask for a specific weekly schedule example (e.g., 'What should my first week look like?') after getting initial advice.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific weekly schedule (e.g., 'week 1: run 1 min, walk 2 min, repeat 8 times') AND at least one shoe model recommendation under $100 for flat feet, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific concern about shin splints or flat feet.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific flat feet and budget constraint (not generic shoe tips) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a specific week 1 schedule and a shoe model to try at a store).
