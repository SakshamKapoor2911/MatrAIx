# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You just bought a new car worth $30,000 and rent an apartment. You want to compare auto and renters insurance quotes.

## Your goal
Get a side-by-side comparison of auto and renters insurance quotes from at least two providers, and decide which bundle offers the best coverage for my 2023 Honda Civic and apartment belongings.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you just bought a new 2023 Honda Civic worth $30,000 and rent a one-bedroom apartment with about $20,000 worth of personal belongings. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of insurance tips, push back and ask how it applies to your specific car model and apartment rental.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget after the car purchase, so you need affordable options but don't want to skimp on coverage.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have received specific quotes or a clear comparison from at least two providers for both auto and renters insurance, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific 2023 Honda Civic and apartment rental situation.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 2023 Honda Civic and apartment rental (not generic tips) and you left with actionable quotes or a clear next step to purchase a policy.
