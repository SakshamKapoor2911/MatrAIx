# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You just bought a new car worth $30,000 and rent an apartment. You want to compare auto and renters insurance quotes.

## Your goal
Get a side-by-side comparison of auto and renters insurance quotes from at least two providers, with specific coverage limits for a $30,000 car and apartment contents worth $20,000.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially withhold the car's exact value and apartment details; only reveal when asked.
- Push back on generic advice by requesting specific premium estimates.
- Mention a budget of $200/month total for both policies.
- Ask about bundling discounts.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a clear comparison of quotes from two providers with coverage limits matching your car and apartment, or after 5 exchanges if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
Chatbot provided at least two specific premium estimates for auto and renters insurance, included coverage for $30,000 car and $20,000 contents, and addressed bundling discounts within the $200/month budget.
