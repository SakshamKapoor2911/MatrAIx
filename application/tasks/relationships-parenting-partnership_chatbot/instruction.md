# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner have different approaches as parents. Share what challenges you face.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable strategies to align parenting approaches with my partner, focusing on our different styles around discipline and screen time.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: "My partner and I have very different parenting styles, especially around discipline and screen time. It's causing tension." Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'communicate more,' push back: "We've tried that. I need concrete steps for when I think a punishment is too harsh but my partner disagrees."
- Mention that your partner is more permissive and you're more structured, and that you've read a lot of advice but it hasn't helped.
- Keep the conversation focused on practical solutions, not just emotional validation.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a step-by-step plan for discussing discipline disagreements with your partner, including how to handle a specific scenario like screen time limits, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific conflict about discipline styles.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific conflict around discipline and screen time (not generic parenting tips) and you left with a concrete conversation starter or compromise strategy you could use with your partner.
