# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner have different approaches as parents. Share what challenges you face.

## Your goal
Identify at least two specific, actionable strategies to align parenting approaches with my partner around discipline and screen time.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially describe the conflict in general terms (e.g., 'we differ on rules') before giving specifics.
- Push back if the chatbot gives vague advice like 'communicate more' without concrete steps.
- Mention that one partner is stricter on screen time and the other more lenient, and that this causes tension.
- Ask for examples or scripts to use during disagreements.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides at least two actionable strategies specifically addressing discipline and screen time differences, or after 5 exchanges without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it offered concrete strategies (e.g., 'schedule weekly co-parenting meetings to agree on limits' or 'use a shared app to track screen time') that directly address the discipline and screen time conflict, and if the strategies are specific enough to implement.
