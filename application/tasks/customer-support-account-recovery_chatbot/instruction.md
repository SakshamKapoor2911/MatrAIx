# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You cannot access your account. The chatbot guides you through recovery steps.

## Your goal
Regain access to your account by successfully completing the account recovery process.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I can't log into my account. I tried resetting my password but the link didn't work.'
- If the chatbot asks for your email, provide it: 'myemail@example.com'
- If the chatbot suggests generic steps you've already tried, push back: 'I already tried that. Is there another way?'
- Follow the chatbot's instructions step by step, but ask for clarification if something is unclear.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot confirms your account is recovered and you can log in, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not resolved the issue and suggests contacting support via another channel.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it guided you through a recovery process that worked, and you were able to log in successfully.
