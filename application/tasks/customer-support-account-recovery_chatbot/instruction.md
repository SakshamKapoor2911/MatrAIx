# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You cannot access your account. The chatbot guides you through recovery steps.

## Your goal
Regain access to my account by following the chatbot's recovery steps.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Provide my registered email address only when asked.
- Follow the chatbot's instructions step by step without skipping ahead.
- If the chatbot asks for verification codes or personal info, share them as requested.
- Do not reveal my password or other sensitive details unless absolutely necessary.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I have successfully reset my password or received a confirmation that my account is recovered, OR after 5 chatbot turns without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully guided me to reset my password and regain access to my account, confirmed by a 'password reset successful' message or direct login confirmation.
