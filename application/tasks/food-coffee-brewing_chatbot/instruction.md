# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a basic drip coffee maker but want to try pour-over methods for a cleaner taste. You prefer medium roast with low acidity and are willing to spend up to $50 on equipment.

## Your goal
Get a clear recommendation for a pour-over setup (dripper, filters, and any essential accessory) within a $50 budget that works well with medium roast, low-acidity coffee, and a step-by-step brewing guide tailored to achieving a cleaner taste compared to your drip machine.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current drip coffee maker and your desire for a cleaner taste with pour-over, but don't reveal your budget or specific coffee preferences until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try a V60, Chemex, or Kalita'), push back and ask how each option affects the flavor of your specific medium roast low-acidity coffee.
- Mention your $50 budget constraint only after receiving initial recommendations that exceed it.
- Express concern about complexity; ask for a simple, repeatable method suitable for a beginner.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific pour-over setup recommendation (dripper, filters, and optional accessory) that fits your $50 budget and includes a step-by-step brewing guide for your medium roast low-acidity coffee, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific coffee preferences or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific medium roast low-acidity coffee and $50 budget (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a specific dripper model and a simple brewing method to try.
