# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a basic drip coffee maker but want to try pour-over methods for a cleaner taste. You prefer medium roast with low acidity and are willing to spend up to $50 on equipment.

## Your goal
Get recommendations for pour-over equipment (dripper, filters, kettle) under $50 total that suit medium roast, low-acidity coffee.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by saying you have a basic drip maker but want to try pour-over for cleaner taste.
- Mention you prefer medium roast with low acidity.
- State your budget is $50 for all equipment.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests items that exceed $50 total or require additional gear.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific list of pour-over items (dripper, filters, kettle) that total $50 or less, or after 5 turns if no satisfactory answer is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends a complete pour-over setup (e.g., a plastic V60 dripper, filters, and a gooseneck kettle) that totals ≤$50 and explicitly addresses low-acidity medium roast suitability.
