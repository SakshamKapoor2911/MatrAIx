# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You sing in the shower and at karaoke but often run out of breath on long notes and can't hit high notes without straining.

## Your goal
Get a specific, actionable warm-up routine and breathing exercise to improve breath support for long notes and safely extend vocal range for high notes without strain.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I love singing in the shower and at karaoke, but I always run out of breath on long notes and strain on high notes.'
- Do not reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice; then say 'I need a warm-up or breathing exercise I can do before karaoke next week.'
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'breathe from your diaphragm, practice scales'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific problem: 'How exactly do I breathe from my diaphragm for a long note without running out of air?'
- Mention that you have only 10 minutes before karaoke to practice, so the routine must be short.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific warm-up routine with step-by-step exercises for breath support and high notes that fits within 10 minutes, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for a short routine before karaoke.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific issue of running out of breath on long notes and straining on high notes (not generic singing tips) and you left with a concrete warm-up or exercise you could do in 10 minutes before karaoke.
