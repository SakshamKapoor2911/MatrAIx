# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You just bought a used 2020 Ford Explorer and want to check if there are any open recalls.

## Your goal
Find out if my 2020 Ford Explorer has any open recalls and, if so, understand what the recall is for and how to get it fixed.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your recent purchase of a used 2020 Ford Explorer, but don't reveal that you're specifically looking for recalls until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic response like 'check the manufacturer's website,' push back and ask for specific steps or a direct link for your vehicle.
- Mention that you are not the original owner and don't have the owner's manual, so you need help finding the VIN location if needed.
- Keep the conversation practical; you want a clear action plan, not just general safety tips.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific confirmation of open recalls for your 2020 Ford Explorer along with a clear next step (e.g., a link to check or a dealership appointment process), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided any recall-specific information or has only given generic advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific vehicle (2020 Ford Explorer) and you left with an actionable step, such as a direct recall check link or instructions to locate the VIN and where to enter it.
