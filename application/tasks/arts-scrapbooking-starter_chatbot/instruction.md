# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You've never scrapbooked before. You have three types of memories you want to preserve: (1) ticket stubs and photos from concerts you attended over the past two years, (2) handwritten birthday cards from your grandmother, and (3) printed photos from a recent trip to Maine. You're unsure whether these should go in one scrapbook or separate ones, and you don't know what supplies to buy first.

## Your goal
Get a concrete starting plan: how to organize these three memory types (concert memorabilia, fragile birthday cards, and printed travel photos), and a shortlist of supplies for your first session under $50.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your memories, but do not reveal your organization question (one scrapbook vs. separate ones) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., "here are 10 scrapbooking tips"), push back and ask how it applies to your specific items (e.g., the cards are fragile and double-sided).
- Budget: mention you want to spend under $50 if supplies come up.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a clear answer on one-vs-multiple scrapbooks AND a supply list you could act on, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific items.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific memory types (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step.
