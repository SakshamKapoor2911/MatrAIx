# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You listen to indie rock bands like Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend, and want to discover similar artists.

## Your goal
Discover 3-5 new indie rock artists similar to Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you like Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend.
- Ask for recommendations that are similar in style but not mainstream.
- If the chatbot suggests generic artists (e.g., The Beatles), push back and ask for more specific indie recommendations.
- Mention that you prefer artists with clever lyrics and catchy guitar riffs.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive at least 3 specific artist recommendations that fit your criteria, or after 5 turns if you haven't received satisfactory suggestions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides at least 3 specific indie rock artists (e.g., The Strokes, Foals, Cage the Elephant) that are similar to Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend, and the recommendations are well-explained (e.g., mentioning similar lyrical style or guitar work).
