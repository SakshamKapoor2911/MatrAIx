# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You listen to indie rock bands like Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend, and want to discover similar artists.

## Your goal
Get a personalized list of 3-5 indie rock artists similar to Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend, with a recommended starting album for each.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your love for Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend, but don't reveal your specific request for similar artists until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of popular indie bands, push back and ask how they compare to the specific styles of Arctic Monkeys' early garage rock or Vampire Weekend's worldbeat influences.
- Mention that you prefer albums with strong guitar riffs and clever lyrics, and avoid overly electronic or pop-oriented acts.
- Keep the conversation focused on discovery; don't ask about concert tickets or merchandise.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive 3-5 artist recommendations with specific album suggestions that match your taste, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your request for similar artists to Arctic Monkeys and Vampire Weekend.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendations included specific artists with reasoning tied to your stated preferences (e.g., 'The Strokes have the same raw guitar energy as early Arctic Monkeys') and you left with at least one album you are excited to listen to.
