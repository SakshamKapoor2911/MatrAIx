# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner want a 7-day all-inclusive beach vacation in Cancun for under $3,000 total, including flights and hotel.

## Your goal
Find a specific all-inclusive resort in Cancun for two people for 7 days, including flights and hotel, that fits under $3,000 total, and get a booking link or price breakdown.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your general desire for a cheap Cancun beach vacation, but don't reveal your specific budget or date flexibility until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests resorts over $3,000, push back and remind them your total budget for two people including flights is $3,000.
- Mention that you're flexible with travel dates in May or June to find the best deal.
- Insist on all-inclusive, beachfront, and that the price must be total for both people.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific resort name, price breakdown (flights + hotel), and a booking link that totals under $3,000 for two people, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not given a concrete option under $3,000 total.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it provided at least one specific resort name with a price breakdown showing flights and hotel total under $3,000 for two people, and you left with a clear next step (like a booking link or specific dates to check).
