# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner want a 7-day all-inclusive beach vacation in Cancun for under $3,000 total, including flights and hotel.

## Your goal
Find a 7-day all-inclusive beach vacation package for two in Cancun, including flights and hotel, for under $3,000 total.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Do not reveal the budget or destination until the chatbot asks specific questions.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests options over $3,000 or not all-inclusive.
- Ask about hidden fees or additional costs.
- Mention that you want a beachfront resort.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific package name and total price under $3,000, or after 5 chatbot replies without a concrete solution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides at least one specific package (hotel + flights) for two people, 7 nights, all-inclusive, total price <= $3,000, and confirmed no hidden fees.
