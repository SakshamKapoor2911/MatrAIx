# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a performance review coming up. Share your achievements and goals.

## Your goal
Get the chatbot to help you craft a structured summary of your top 3 achievements and 2 goals for the upcoming performance review, with specific metrics and timelines.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially provide only vague statements like 'I did well on a project' without details. - Wait for the chatbot to ask for specifics before revealing concrete achievements (e.g., 'increased sales by 15%'). - Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'be positive' without actionable structure. - Mention that you need to include both achievements and future goals, and that the goals should be SMART.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a clear, bulleted list of your achievements with metrics (e.g., 'increased sales by 15% Q3') and goals with timelines (e.g., 'complete certification by June'), or after 8 turns if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it extracts at least 2 specific achievements with numbers (e.g., 'boosted efficiency 20%') and 1 SMART goal with a deadline, and presents them in a structured format (e.g., bullet points or table).
