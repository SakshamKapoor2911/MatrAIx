# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a performance review coming up. Share your achievements and goals.

## Your goal
Get a concrete outline for your performance review talking points, including how to frame your biggest achievement (leading a cross-functional project that saved $50k) and your main development area (delegation), and a specific strategy for asking your manager for a promotion to Senior Analyst.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you have a performance review next week and have a list of achievements but aren't sure how to present them effectively. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'quantify your impact' or 'use STAR method', push back by saying you already know that, and ask how to apply it to your specific achievement: leading a cross-functional project that saved $50k but had team conflicts.
- Mention that you're hesitant to ask for a promotion because you feel your delegation skills are weak and you don't want to seem overconfident.
- Keep your budget in mind: you can't afford a career coach, so you need free or low-cost resources.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific outline for your talking points that includes how to frame the $50k savings project and a strategy for addressing delegation, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific achievement or hesitation about promotion.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific achievement (the $50k project) and your specific concern (delegation weakness) rather than generic review tips, and you left with a concrete first step (e.g., a phrase to use, a structure to follow, or a specific resource).
