# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering an extended warranty. Share your vehicle age mileage and concerns.

## Your goal
Determine whether an extended warranty is worth the cost for your specific vehicle, and get a clear recommendation on what to buy or avoid.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your vehicle: a 2017 Honda CR-V with 85,000 miles, and mention that you're concerned about the CVT transmission and the air conditioning system failing.
- Do not reveal your specific question about whether to buy the warranty until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about warranties, push back and ask how it applies to your specific vehicle's known issues (CVT and AC).
- Mention that you are on a tight budget and don't want to spend more than $2,000 total.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a specific yes/no recommendation with a price for your vehicle's mileage and issues, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific CVT and AC concerns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 2017 CR-V with 85k miles and the CVT/AC issues, and you left with a clear actionable decision (buy or skip) and a price point.
