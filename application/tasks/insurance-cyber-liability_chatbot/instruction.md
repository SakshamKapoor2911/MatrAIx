# Insurance Chatbot

## Your situation
Your business may need cyber insurance. Share your business type and data handling.

## Your goal
Determine whether your small e-commerce business needs cyber insurance, and if so, get a recommendation for a specific policy type that covers customer payment data and inventory management system breaches.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your business as a small online store that sells handmade crafts and processes credit card payments, but don't reveal your specific question about cyber insurance until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about cyber insurance types, push back and ask how it applies to your specific business with customer credit card data and an inventory system.
- Mention that you have a limited budget (under $500/year) and want to know if basic coverage is enough or if you need a specialized policy.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific policy type that addresses your credit card data and inventory system, including a cost estimate within your budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific business type and data handling concerns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific business (handmade crafts e-commerce) and data (customer credit card info, inventory system breaches) rather than generic tips, and you left with an actionable next step (e.g., which policy to request a quote for or what to ask insurers).
