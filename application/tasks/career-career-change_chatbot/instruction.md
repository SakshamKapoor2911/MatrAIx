# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a teacher of 10 years who wants to transition into corporate training. You have no business experience.

## Your goal
To get a concrete plan for transitioning from teaching to corporate training, including specific steps to bridge the experience gap and identify relevant skills to highlight.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 10 years of teaching experience and interest in corporate training, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'update your resume', push back and ask how to translate specific teaching achievements (e.g., curriculum design, classroom management) into corporate training terms.
- Mention your lack of business experience and ask how to address that gap without taking expensive courses.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a list of 3-5 actionable steps that reference your teaching background (e.g., 'highlight your curriculum design as instructional design'), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your lack of business experience.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific teaching experience (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific skill to emphasize or a networking strategy.
