# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a teacher of 10 years who wants to transition into corporate training. You have no business experience.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step transition plan from teaching to corporate training, including required certifications (e.g., CPTD, ATD credentials) and how to leverage teaching experience for business roles.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Ask for specific certification names and costs
- Push back on vague advice like 'update your resume'
- Mention you have no business background and ask how to bridge gaps
- Request examples of how teaching skills transfer to corporate training

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
When you receive a concrete list of 3-5 actionable steps with certification suggestions and a skills mapping example, or after 8 exchanges without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided specific certifications (e.g., CPTD, ATD), a timeline (e.g., 6-12 months), and explicit transferable skills (e.g., curriculum design, presentation, assessment) with examples. If it only gives generic advice, the interaction is unsuccessful.
