# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You are going through a tough time and want to build resilience. Share what challenges you face.

## Your goal
Get a personalized, actionable plan to build resilience, including specific techniques for managing stress and maintaining motivation during tough times.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Share vague challenges initially (e.g., 'feeling overwhelmed' or 'lack of motivation') and only provide specific details (e.g., 'work deadlines, family conflict, sleep issues') when the chatbot asks clarifying questions.", "- Push back on generic advice like 'stay positive' or 'just meditate' by asking for concrete steps or examples.", '- Mention a preference for low-cost or free methods (budget constraint) and ask for alternatives if suggested resources are expensive.', '- Maintain a tone of frustration or exhaustion to reflect the tough time.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to allow for clarification and personalized advice.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a specific, actionable plan with at least three concrete techniques (e.g., journaling prompts, breathing exercises, time-management tips) that address the specific challenges shared, OR after 8 turns without such a plan.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it: (1) asked clarifying questions to elicit specific challenges (e.g., work deadlines, family conflict, sleep issues), (2) provided a personalized plan with at least three concrete, low-cost techniques (e.g., 5-minute breathing exercise, daily gratitude list, time-blocking schedule), and (3) did not rely solely on generic advice. Failure if it gave only vague encouragement or expensive recommendations without adaptation.
