# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
Your water bill has doubled this summer and you have a lawn and a vegetable garden. You want to reduce outdoor water use.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan to reduce outdoor water use for both my lawn and vegetable garden, including specific techniques and tools that fit my situation.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your doubled water bill and that you have a lawn and vegetable garden, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'water early morning'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific lawn type (e.g., fescue) and garden crops (e.g., tomatoes and peppers).
- Mention your budget constraint: you don't want to spend more than $200 on new equipment or modifications.
- If the chatbot suggests rain barrels, ask about installation complexity and whether it's feasible for your single-story house with downspouts.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a step-by-step plan that includes specific watering schedules for fescue lawn and tomatoes/peppers, and a recommendation for a rain barrel or drip irrigation kit within your $200 budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific lawn type, garden crops, or budget constraint.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific fescue lawn and tomato/pepper garden, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'install a rain barrel' or 'set a watering schedule for 1 inch per week') that you could implement immediately.
