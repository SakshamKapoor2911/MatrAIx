# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You read about 200 words per minute and want to double that for reading work reports and non-fiction books. You have 30 minutes a day to practice.

## Your goal
Achieve a reading speed of 400 words per minute for work reports and non-fiction books within 2 months, using 30-minute daily practice sessions.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention your current reading speed (200 wpm) and your target (400 wpm) early in the conversation.', 'Emphasize that you have only 30 minutes per day for practice.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests methods requiring more time or expensive tools.', 'Ask for specific techniques or exercises tailored to non-fiction and reports.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a concrete daily practice plan with specific exercises (e.g., chunking, RSVP apps) that fits a 30-minute window, or after 5 chatbot turns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provides a structured daily plan (e.g., 10 min speed drills, 10 min comprehension exercises, 10 min tracking) that respects the 30-minute limit and addresses doubling speed for work reports and non-fiction.
