# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You read about 200 words per minute and want to double that for reading work reports and non-fiction books. You have 30 minutes a day to practice.

## Your goal
Get a specific 30-minute daily practice plan to double reading speed from 200 wpm to 400 wpm, tailored for work reports and non-fiction books.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current reading speed (200 wpm) and stating you have 30 minutes a day to practice, but do not reveal your specific question about technique until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'use a pointer, minimize subvocalization'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific materials: dense work reports with technical jargon and non-fiction books with complex arguments.
- Mention that you've tried skimming but it hurt comprehension, and you need a method that balances speed and understanding.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a step-by-step 30-minute daily routine that includes specific exercises for work reports and non-fiction books, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific materials (work reports and non-fiction) and instead keeps giving generic speed-reading tips.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific reading materials (work reports and non-fiction books) and your time constraint (30 minutes/day), and you left with a concrete first practice session plan (e.g., 'Day 1: 5 minutes warm-up with a pointer on a report, 15 minutes chunking practice, 10 minutes comprehension check').
