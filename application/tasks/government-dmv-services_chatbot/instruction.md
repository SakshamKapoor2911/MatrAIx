# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You need to handle a DMV task. Share your state and what you need to do.

## Your goal
Renew my driver's license online in California without visiting the DMV office.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing that you need to renew your California driver's license, but don't mention that you want to do it online until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests visiting a DMV office, push back by explaining that you have a busy schedule and prefer an online option.
- Mention that you are concerned about eligibility requirements for online renewal, such as having a valid credit card and a printer for the temporary license.
- If the chatbot provides a generic checklist, ask how it applies to your specific situation (e.g., you have a standard license, not a REAL ID, and you recently moved within California).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a clear step-by-step guide for online renewal that addresses your eligibility concerns and recent address change, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for online renewal or your address change.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific need for online renewal, addressed your address change, and you left knowing exactly what documents and steps are required to complete the renewal online.
