# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to save $20,000 for a down payment on a house in 3 years. You currently save $200 per month.

## Your goal
Get a concrete, personalized plan to save $20,000 for a down payment in 3 years, given that I currently save $200/month. I need to know how much more I need to save each month and specific strategies to bridge the gap.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I want to save $20,000 for a house in 3 years, but I only save $200 a month. I'm not sure if that's enough.' Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle like 'cut expenses or increase income,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific numbers: 'But I need exact numbers. How much more per month do I need to save?'
- Mention that you have a tight budget and can't easily cut expenses, so you need realistic options.
- If the chatbot suggests high-risk investments, push back and say you need a safe plan for a down payment.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific monthly savings target (e.g., 'You need to save $X more per month') and a concrete strategy to achieve it, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific $20,000 goal and $200/month savings rate.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $20,000 target and $200/month savings rate (not generic tips like 'save more'), and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific monthly savings amount or a clear plan to increase income or cut expenses.
