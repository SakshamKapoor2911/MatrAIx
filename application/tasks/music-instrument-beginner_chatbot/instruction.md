# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You've always wanted to play the acoustic guitar and just bought a used Yamaha F310, but you've never played any instrument before.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step beginner practice plan for the first month, including specific exercises to build finger strength and chord transitions, and a list of three easy songs to learn.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you just bought a used Yamaha F310 acoustic guitar and have never played any instrument before, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'practice scales' or 'learn chords,' push back by asking how that applies to your specific guitar (Yamaha F310) and your complete inexperience.
- Mention that you have a limited budget for accessories, so you need affordable recommendations (e.g., tuner, picks, strings).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a concrete first-month plan with specific exercises and three easy songs, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific beginner status and the Yamaha F310.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific beginner status (never played before) and your Yamaha F310 guitar, and you left with an actionable first step you could take that day.
