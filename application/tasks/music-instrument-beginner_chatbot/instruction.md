# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You've always wanted to play the acoustic guitar and just bought a used Yamaha F310, but you've never played any instrument before.

## Your goal
Get step-by-step beginner instructions to tune, hold, and play my first chord (e.g., G major) on my Yamaha F310 acoustic guitar, with no prior musical experience.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Mention you're a complete beginner with zero musical background.", '- Specify you have a Yamaha F310 (used) and need basic setup advice (tuning, holding, first chord).', '- Push back if advice is too advanced (like barre chords or music theory) or assumes prior knowledge.', "- Ask for clarification if instructions are vague (e.g., 'tune the strings' without telling which notes)."]

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) covering tuning, holding posture, and one easy chord.

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a clear, actionable answer that includes: (1) how to tune the guitar to standard tuning (EADGBE) with or without a tuner, (2) proper sitting posture and hand positions, and (3) finger placement for a G major chord (or another basic open chord) with a simple strumming pattern.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is considered successful if it provides: (a) tuning instructions (e.g., 'tune 6th string to E, 5th to A, etc.' or using a tuner), (b) a clear description of how to hold the guitar (e.g., sitting with the body on your thigh, neck at 45°), (c) a diagram or text explanation for a G major chord (fingers on specific frets and strings), and (d) a simple down-up strumming pattern. If it omits any of these or uses jargon without explanation, the chatbot fails.
