# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You can play basic chords on piano but don't understand why certain chords sound good together or how to build progressions.

## Your goal
Learn a specific chord progression (I-V-vi-IV) in C major and understand why it sounds good, with examples of songs that use it.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by stating you know basic chords (C, F, G, Am) but not progressions.', 'Ask for an explanation of why certain chords work together, not just a list.', 'Request at least two song examples that use the progression.', 'If the chatbot gives generic advice, push back and ask for specifics.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot explains the I-V-vi-IV progression in C major, provides a reason (e.g., common tones, circle of fifths), and names two songs (e.g., 'Let It Be' by The Beatles, 'No Woman No Cry' by Bob Marley).

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identified the I-V-vi-IV progression in C major, explained its harmonic appeal (e.g., voice leading, root movement), and gave at least two recognizable song examples that use it.
