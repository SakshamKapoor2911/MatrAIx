# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
Your flight was delayed or canceled. Share the airline and delay details.

## Your goal
Get compensation or rebooking assistance from the airline for a canceled flight on Delta Airlines due to a mechanical issue.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating the flight was canceled on Delta Airlines.
- Withhold the specific reason (mechanical issue) until asked.
- Push back on generic advice like 'check the app' or 'call customer service'.
- Mention urgency if needed (e.g., need to get to a wedding tomorrow).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific actionable step (e.g., a rebooking confirmation, a compensation claim link, or a direct phone number for rebooking) or after 5 turns without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully addresses the cancellation by either rebooking the user on the next available Delta flight to the same destination or providing a clear compensation process (e.g., a link to file a claim, a voucher code, or a refund initiation). The chatbot should not just offer generic advice or redirect to a phone line without specific details.
