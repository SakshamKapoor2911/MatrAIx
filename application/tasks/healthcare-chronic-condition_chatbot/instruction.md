# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a chronic condition like diabetes hypertension or asthma. Share what you are managing and your current routine.

## Your goal
Get personalized advice on adjusting your daily routine to better manage your type 2 diabetes, specifically around meal timing and glucose monitoring.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your diabetes management routine, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation, like your erratic work schedule and difficulty with post-meal glucose spikes.
- Mention that you have a limited budget for new gadgets or supplements.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete plan that addresses your erratic schedule and budget constraints, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific meal timing and glucose monitoring issues.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific erratic work schedule and post-meal glucose spikes (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, like adjusting your breakfast timing or using a specific affordable glucose monitor.
