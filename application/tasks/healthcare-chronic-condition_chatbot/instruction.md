# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a chronic condition like diabetes hypertension or asthma. Share what you are managing and your current routine.

## Your goal
Get personalized advice on managing my type 2 diabetes, specifically on adjusting my current routine (diet, exercise, medication) to improve my blood sugar control.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by sharing that I have type 2 diabetes and briefly mention my current routine (e.g., taking metformin, walking 30 min daily, eating low-carb).
- Withhold specific details like exact blood sugar readings or recent A1C unless asked.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'eat healthy'), push back by asking for concrete examples or adjustments to my specific routine.
- Mention that I'm on a budget and prefer affordable options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when the chatbot provides actionable advice tailored to my routine (e.g., specific changes to diet, exercise, or medication timing) or after 5 exchanges if no satisfactory answer is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is considered helpful if it asks clarifying questions about my current routine (e.g., metformin dosage, walking pace, typical meals) and then offers specific, actionable suggestions (e.g., 'try adding a 10-minute walk after meals to lower post-meal spikes' or 'consider swapping white rice for quinoa for better glycemic control') that are budget-friendly.
