# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to knit a scarf as a gift but have never knitted before. You need advice on yarn and needles.

## Your goal
Get a beginner-friendly recommendation for yarn (type, weight, fiber) and needle size to knit a simple scarf as a gift, plus a step-by-step plan for the first project.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you want to knit a scarf for a friend's birthday, but you've never knitted before and feel overwhelmed by choices.
- Don't reveal your specific question about yarn and needles until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'start with a simple pattern'.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips (e.g., 'use medium weight yarn'), push back by saying you don't know what that means for your gift scarf and ask for specific brand or fiber recommendations.
- Mention you have a limited budget (under $30 total) and need supplies that are easy to find at a local craft store.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot has recommended a specific yarn (e.g., worsted weight acrylic or wool blend) and needle size (e.g., US 8 or 9) with a clear first step (e.g., cast on 30 stitches), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for yarn and needle recommendations for a beginner scarf.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific needs (beginner, gift scarf, budget under $30) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as the exact yarn and needle size to buy.
