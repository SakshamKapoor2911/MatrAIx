# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to knit a scarf as a gift but have never knitted before. You need advice on yarn and needles.

## Your goal
Get specific advice on the best type of yarn and needle size for a beginner knitter to make a simple scarf as a gift.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by stating you are a complete beginner.', 'Mention the scarf is for a gift so it should look good.', 'Ask for yarn recommendations (e.g., worsted weight, acrylic/wool blend) and needle size.', "Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'just pick any yarn') by asking for specifics."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a clear recommendation for yarn type (e.g., worsted weight acrylic) and needle size (e.g., US 8 or 9).

## Success judgment
Success if the chatbot recommends a specific yarn weight (e.g., worsted) and fiber (e.g., acrylic or wool blend) and a corresponding needle size (e.g., US 8), and explains why it's good for beginners.
