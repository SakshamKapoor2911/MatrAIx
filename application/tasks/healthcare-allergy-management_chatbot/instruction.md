# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You have allergies that affect your daily life. Share what you are allergic to and your current symptoms.

## Your goal
Get a personalized management plan for my seasonal pollen allergies, specifically for reducing sneezing and itchy eyes without drowsy medications.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Initially state only 'I have bad seasonal allergies' without specifying triggers.", '- When asked for details, reveal I am allergic to grass and ragweed pollen.', '- Mention that over-the-counter antihistamines make me drowsy and I need non-drowsy options.', '- Ask about non-medication strategies if the chatbot suggests only drugs.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to ensure sufficient detail gathering.

## Termination criteria
End when I receive a concrete plan including specific non-drowsy medication names (e.g., loratadine, cetirizine) and at least two lifestyle modifications (e.g., shower after outdoor activity, use HEPA filter).

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identifies my specific allergens (grass, ragweed), acknowledges the drowsiness issue, and provides a tailored plan with at least one non-drowsy medication recommendation and two actionable lifestyle changes.
