# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a 2-bedroom apartment with a partner, drive a car to work, and eat meat daily. You want to reduce your carbon footprint.

## Your goal
Reduce my carbon footprint by at least 20% within 6 months, focusing on transportation, diet, and home energy use.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking for general tips on reducing carbon footprint.
- When given generic advice (e.g., 'reduce meat'), ask for specific quantities and actionable steps.
- Mention that I drive 15 miles each way to work and eat meat daily, and push back if suggestions ignore my commute or diet habits.
- Express a limited budget (under $500 for initial changes) and ask for low-cost options.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I receive a personalized plan with specific actions for my commute (e.g., carpool, EV, or public transit alternatives), diet (e.g., meatless days per week), and home energy (e.g., LED bulbs, thermostat settings), and the plan fits my budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided a concrete plan that includes at least: (1) a specific reduction target for my commute (e.g., switch to carpool 2 days/week), (2) a number of meatless meals per week (e.g., 3 meatless dinners), (3) low-cost home changes (e.g., set thermostat to 68°F in winter), and (4) total cost under $500.
