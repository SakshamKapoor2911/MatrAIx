# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a 2-bedroom apartment with a partner, drive a car to work, and eat meat daily. You want to reduce your carbon footprint.

## Your goal
Get a personalized step-by-step plan to reduce your carbon footprint in three areas: apartment energy, car commuting, and daily meat consumption, with specific actionable changes for each.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: living in a 2-bedroom apartment with a partner, driving to work, and eating meat daily. But don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'use LED bulbs' or 'carpool', push back by asking how those apply to your specific apartment (e.g., old windows, no smart thermostat) and your commute (e.g., no carpool option, short distance).
- Mention you have a limited budget (under $200) for any upfront changes, so advice must be low-cost or have quick payback.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete plan with at least one actionable step for each of the three areas (apartment energy, car commute, meat consumption), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (old apartment windows, no carpool, limited budget).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific constraints (old windows, short commute, limited budget) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, like a specific change to your meat consumption or a low-cost energy upgrade.
