# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to open a food truck in Austin, Texas. You need to know what licenses and permits are required.

## Your goal
Get a complete list of all required licenses and permits to legally operate a food truck in Austin, Texas.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by asking a general question about requirements without specifying Austin or Texas.', '- If the chatbot asks for location, provide Austin, Texas.', '- If the chatbot gives a generic answer, push back and ask for specific permits for mobile food vendors.', '- Ask about health department permits, business license, and mobile vending permit explicitly.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a list of specific permits (e.g., Mobile Food Unit Permit, Health Permit, Sales Tax Permit), or after 5 exchanges if not resolved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully provided a list of at least three specific permits: Mobile Food Unit Permit from Austin Public Health, a Texas Sales and Use Tax Permit, and a City of Austin Business License. It also mentioned any additional requirements like food manager certification or commissary agreement.
