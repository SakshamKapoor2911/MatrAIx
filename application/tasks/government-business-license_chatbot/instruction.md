# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to open a food truck in Austin, Texas. You need to know what licenses and permits are required.

## Your goal
Get a clear, step-by-step list of all required licenses and permits to legally operate a food truck in Austin, Texas, including estimated costs and timelines.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by stating you want to open a food truck in Austin but don't mention specific licenses initially.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'check with the city'), push back by asking for specific permits (e.g., 'Which city department handles the mobile food vendor permit?').
- Mention you have a limited budget and need cost estimates for each permit.
- Ask about health department inspections and commissary requirements.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a list of at least 5 specific permits (e.g., Mobile Food Vendor Permit, Health Permit, Sales Tax Permit, Fire Inspection, Vehicle Inspection) with costs and timelines, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided any specific permit names or costs.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it provided specific permit names (e.g., Mobile Food Vendor Permit from Austin Public Health, Sales Tax Permit from Texas Comptroller) and actionable steps (e.g., 'Apply online at this link, cost $500, takes 2 weeks') rather than generic advice.
