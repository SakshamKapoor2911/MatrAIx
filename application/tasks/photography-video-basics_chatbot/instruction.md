# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a GoPro Hero 11 and want to record smooth action footage of mountain biking trails, but the footage is shaky.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable settings and techniques for the GoPro Hero 11 to reduce shakiness when mountain biking, such as stabilization modes, frame rates, and mounting positions.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your GoPro Hero 11 and the shaky mountain biking footage problem, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'use a gimbal' or 'record in 4K', push back and ask how it applies to your specific GoPro Hero 11 and mountain biking scenario.
- Mention that you don't want to buy additional accessories if possible, and ask for free or built-in solutions.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a concrete starting plan with specific settings (e.g., 'use HyperSmooth Boost at 2.7K 60fps' and 'mount the camera on your chest'), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific GoPro Hero 11 and mountain biking constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific GoPro Hero 11 model and mountain biking activity (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take.
