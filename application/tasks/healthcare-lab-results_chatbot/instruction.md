# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You received lab results and want to understand what they mean. Share which tests and results.

## Your goal
Understand the meaning of my recent lab results, specifically for hemoglobin A1c (6.8%) and LDL cholesterol (160 mg/dL).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking the chatbot to explain my lab results in general.
- Only share the specific test names and values after the chatbot asks for them.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice without referencing my numbers.
- Mention that I am not a medical professional and need simple explanations.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot explains what a hemoglobin A1c of 6.8% and LDL of 160 mg/dL mean in plain language, and provides actionable next steps.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully helps if it: (1) asks for specific test names and values, (2) explains that 6.8% A1c indicates prediabetes and 160 mg/dL LDL is high, (3) suggests lifestyle changes or follow-up with a doctor, and (4) avoids vague advice like 'eat healthy' without context.
