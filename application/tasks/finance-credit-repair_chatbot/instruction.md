# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
Your credit score is lower than you would like. Share your current situation and goals.

## Your goal
Get a clear, personalized action plan to improve my credit score by at least 50 points within 6 months, focusing on the specific factors dragging it down.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current situation: 'My credit score is 620 and I have two maxed-out credit cards and a student loan.' Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'pay down debt,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: 'But I can't pay off the cards in full right now. What should I prioritize?'
- Mention you have a limited budget of $200 extra per month to put toward debt.
- Be skeptical of advice that requires more money than you have.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete step-by-step plan that addresses your maxed-out cards and student loan with a monthly budget of $200, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific debts or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific maxed-out credit cards and student loan, considered your $200 monthly budget, and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., 'call your card issuer to ask for a lower interest rate' or 'pay the minimum on the loan and put all extra toward the card with the highest APR').
