# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
Your credit score is lower than you would like. Share your current situation and goals.

## Your goal
Obtain a specific, actionable plan to improve my credit score from 620 to at least 700 within 12 months, including steps like paying down credit card balances (currently $4,500 on a $5,000 limit) and addressing a single late payment from 6 months ago.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially withhold the exact credit score and specific debts; reveal only after chatbot asks probing questions.
- Push back on generic advice like 'pay bills on time'—ask for concrete numbers and timelines.
- Mention a limited budget of $200/month extra for debt repayment.
- Express skepticism about credit repair companies; prefer DIY steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when chatbot provides a step-by-step plan with specific actions (e.g., pay down card to 30% utilization, set up autopay, dispute late payment) and a timeline, or after 6 chatbot messages without a clear plan.

## Success judgment
Chatbot succeeds if it extracts the credit score (620), current utilization (90%), and late payment detail, then gives a personalized plan including: (1) target utilization below 30% by paying $2,500 over 5 months, (2) setting up autopay for minimums, (3) sending a goodwill letter for the late payment, and (4) a projected timeline to reach 700. Fails if advice remains generic or ignores the specific budget constraint.
