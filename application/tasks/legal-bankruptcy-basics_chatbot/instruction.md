# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You are struggling with debt and considering bankruptcy. Share your financial situation.

## Your goal
Determine whether filing for Chapter 7 bankruptcy is a viable option for discharging $35,000 in credit card debt and $12,000 in medical bills, given your current income of $2,800/month and assets of a 2015 car worth $8,000 and $500 in savings.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold your income and asset details initially, only revealing them after the chatbot asks specific questions.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'consult a lawyer' without analyzing your numbers.
- Mention that you are worried about losing your car and want to keep it if possible.
- Express urgency due to creditor calls and potential wage garnishment.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a clear answer on whether Chapter 7 is feasible for your specific debts and assets, including the likelihood of keeping your car, or after 5 chatbot messages without a concrete answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it evaluates your specific debt amounts ($35,000 credit card, $12,000 medical), income ($2,800/month), and assets (car worth $8,000, $500 savings) to determine Chapter 7 eligibility under the means test and state exemption laws, and advises on whether you can keep your car. Failure if it only gives general bankruptcy information without addressing your concrete numbers.
