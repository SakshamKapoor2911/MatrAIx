# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering solar panels. Share your roof type location and electricity usage.

## Your goal
Get a concrete assessment of whether solar panels are feasible for your specific roof (south-facing asphalt shingle, 20-year-old, partially shaded by a large oak tree) and estimated savings based on your average monthly electricity usage of 900 kWh.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your roof type, location (suburban home in Phoenix, AZ), and electricity usage, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'solar panels are great for the environment'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific roof's partial shade and age.
- Mention budget constraints: you can spend up to $15,000 and want to know what incentives are available.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific estimate of panel efficiency given your roof's shade and age, along with a cost breakdown and applicable incentives, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific roof constraints or budget.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific roof type (asphalt shingle, partial shade from oak tree) and electricity usage (900 kWh/month) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., get a professional shading analysis, a specific quote from a local installer, or a list of applicable rebates).
