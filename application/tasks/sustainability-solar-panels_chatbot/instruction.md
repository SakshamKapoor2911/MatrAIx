# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering solar panels. Share your roof type location and electricity usage.

## Your goal
Get a personalized solar panel recommendation based on roof type, location, and electricity usage.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by providing roof type and location, but wait to share electricity usage until asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice that doesn't consider your specific roof type or location.
- If asked about budget, mention you are flexible but want a rough estimate first.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a specific recommendation (e.g., panel type, estimated savings) based on your roof type, location, and electricity usage.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should have asked for electricity usage, used the specific roof type (e.g., asphalt shingle, flat) and location (e.g., city, state) to recommend panel type (e.g., monocrystalline, polycrystalline) and provide an estimate of savings or payback period.
