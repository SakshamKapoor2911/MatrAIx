# Beauty Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a cosmetic procedure. Share what you are interested in.

## Your goal
Get a clear, balanced overview of the pros, cons, and realistic outcomes for a specific cosmetic procedure (e.g., rhinoplasty or blepharoplasty) that fits your facial features and lifestyle, including recovery timeline and cost range.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your interest in a cosmetic procedure (e.g., 'I've been thinking about getting a nose job to refine my profile'), but don't reveal your specific concerns (like breathing issues or asymmetry) unless the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of benefits without addressing your specific facial structure or recovery concerns, push back and ask how it applies to your particular nose shape or eyelid hooding.
- Mention that you're on a moderate budget and can't take more than 2 weeks off work, so you need realistic downtime and cost expectations.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a detailed plan including your specific procedure (e.g., rhinoplasty) with recovery timeline and cost estimate, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific facial features or budget/time constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific procedure (like rhinoplasty or blepharoplasty) and your stated constraints (2-week recovery, moderate budget), and you left with an actionable next step (e.g., a list of questions to ask a surgeon or a realistic timeline).
