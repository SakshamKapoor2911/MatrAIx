# Beauty Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a cosmetic procedure. Share what you are interested in.

## Your goal
Get personalized advice on whether a rhinoplasty or a non-surgical nose job would better achieve a more defined nasal tip and a slight reduction in nostril width.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by expressing general interest without specifying the exact procedure; let the chatbot ask clarifying questions.', '- Mention budget concern: you can spend up to $3,000.', "- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'consult a surgeon') without specific comparisons.", '- Ask about recovery time, risks, and longevity of results for both options.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to allow for detailed exploration.

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear comparison of rhinoplasty vs. non-surgical nose job for your specific goals (tip definition and nostril width) within your budget, or after 8 turns if not resolved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it provided specific pros and cons of rhinoplasty vs. non-surgical nose job for tip definition and nostril width, mentioned cost ranges aligning with your budget, and gave realistic recovery and risk information.
