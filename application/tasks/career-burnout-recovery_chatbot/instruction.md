# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You feel burned out at work. Share what you are experiencing and what contributes to it.

## Your goal
Get validation that my burnout is real and specific strategies to reduce workload stress, including how to negotiate deadlines with my manager.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your concrete situation: 'I'm a software engineer at a startup, working 60-hour weeks, skipping lunch, and waking up at 3am worrying about code reviews.'
- Don't reveal your specific question about negotiating deadlines until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'take breaks.'
- If the chatbot suggests generic self-care tips, push back: 'I don't have time for yoga. My manager expects a new feature every two weeks. How do I handle that?'
- Mention that you've already tried talking to HR and it didn't help, so you need practical steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot offers a concrete script to use when asking your manager for deadline extensions, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific workload pressures and manager expectations.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (startup, 60-hour weeks, manager's feature deadlines) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a template email or a prioritization framework.
