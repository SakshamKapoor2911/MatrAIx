# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You feel burned out at work. Share what you are experiencing and what contributes to it.

## Your goal
Identify specific workplace factors contributing to burnout and receive actionable strategies to address them, such as workload reduction, boundary-setting, or seeking support.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Describe burnout symptoms (e.g., exhaustion, cynicism) but initially avoid naming specific causes until asked.
- Push back if the chatbot offers generic advice like 'take a vacation' without understanding your context.
- Mention that you have a high workload and lack of control over deadlines.
- If the chatbot asks targeted questions, reveal that you work in tech with constant overtime and no recognition.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive at least two concrete, personalized suggestions (e.g., delegating tasks, speaking to manager, setting boundaries) or after 5 turns without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is considered helpful if it identifies at least two of the following: high workload, lack of control, insufficient recognition, or poor work-life balance, and provides specific, actionable steps (not just self-care tips) tailored to those factors.
