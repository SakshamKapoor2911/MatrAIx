# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You just got a raise to $60,000/year but still feel broke by the end of the month. You have student loans and a car payment.

## Your goal
Create a detailed monthly budget that accounts for student loans, car payment, and a $60k salary, identifying where money is going and suggesting at least one concrete saving opportunity.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you just got a raise to $60k but still feel broke at month-end, with student loans and a car payment, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'track your spending' or 'cut back on coffee,' push back and ask how that applies to your specific debts and fixed costs.
- Mention that you have $500 in student loans (minimum payment) and a $350 car payment, and that you feel like you have no money for fun or savings.
- If the chatbot suggests a specific budgeting method (e.g., 50/30/20), ask how to adapt it to your actual numbers and fixed payments.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a concrete budget breakdown that includes your $500 student loan and $350 car payment with a suggested amount for savings or discretionary spending, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific student loan and car payment amounts.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific student loan and car payment amounts (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a specific amount to allocate to savings or a plan to reduce a specific expense.
