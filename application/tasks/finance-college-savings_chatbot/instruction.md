# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
Your child is 5 years old and you want to start saving for college. You can contribute $200 per month.

## Your goal
Open a 529 college savings plan for my 5-year-old child with a monthly contribution of $200, and understand the tax benefits and investment options.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially ask general questions about saving for college without revealing the $200 budget or child's age until prompted.
- Push back on generic advice like 'start early' by specifying I already know that and want concrete steps.
- Mention budget only after being asked for specific financial details.
- Ask about fees, minimum contributions, and state tax deductions.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific 529 plan recommendation with details on minimum contribution, monthly contribution setup, and state tax benefit information, or after 6 exchanges without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully recommends a specific 529 plan (e.g., a state plan or a national plan), confirms that $200/month is feasible, explains tax benefits (e.g., state income tax deduction if applicable), and provides steps to open the account.
