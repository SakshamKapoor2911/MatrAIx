# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
Your child is 5 years old and you want to start saving for college. You can contribute $200 per month.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for saving for college with a $200 monthly contribution, including specific account type (e.g., 529 plan) and investment strategy (e.g., age-based portfolio), and understand tax implications.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you have a 5-year-old child and can save $200 per month, but don't reveal your specific question about account types until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'consider a 529 plan, Coverdell ESA, or custodial account'), push back and ask how each applies to your $200 monthly budget and your child's age.
- Mention that you are risk-averse and prefer low-fee options; ask about fees and minimums for specific accounts.
- If the chatbot suggests a specific plan, ask for details on how to open it and what happens if your child doesn't go to college.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a step-by-step plan to open a 529 plan with a $200 monthly contribution, including investment options and tax benefits, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific $200 monthly budget and your child's age.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $200 monthly contribution and 5-year-old child (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as choosing a specific 529 plan and setting up automatic contributions.
