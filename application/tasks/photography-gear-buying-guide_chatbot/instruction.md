# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You're an intermediate photographer with a crop-sensor DSLR and a 18-55mm kit lens, and you want to upgrade for better wildlife shots.

## Your goal
Get a concrete recommendation for a specific lens (or two) that will improve wildlife photography on your crop-sensor DSLR, including approximate cost and why it fits your budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current setup (crop-sensor DSLR, 18-55mm kit lens) and that you want to upgrade for wildlife, but don't reveal your budget or specific questions until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try a telephoto lens'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific crop-sensor body and 18-55mm kit lens.
- Mention that you have a budget of $500-800 and want the best value for that range.
- If the chatbot recommends a lens, ask about its autofocus speed and compatibility with your camera model.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a specific lens recommendation (model and approximate price) that fits your $500-800 budget and is suitable for your crop-sensor DSLR, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific camera body and budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific crop-sensor DSLR and 18-55mm kit lens (not generic tips) and you left with a clear lens recommendation (e.g., Tamron 70-200mm f/2.8 or Sigma 150-600mm) that fits your budget and an understanding of why it works for wildlife.
