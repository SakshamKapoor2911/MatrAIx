# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You're an intermediate photographer with a crop-sensor DSLR and a 18-55mm kit lens, and you want to upgrade for better wildlife shots.

## Your goal
Get a specific recommendation for a telephoto zoom lens (e.g., 150-600mm or 100-400mm) that is compatible with your crop-sensor DSLR and suitable for wildlife photography, including key specs and price range.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially mention only that you have a crop-sensor DSLR and an 18-55mm kit lens, without specifying the brand or model until asked.
- Push back if the assistant suggests a general-purpose zoom or a prime lens; insist on a dedicated telephoto zoom for wildlife.
- State that your budget is under $1500.
- Ask about autofocus performance and image stabilization if not covered.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific lens recommendation (with model name, approximate price, and key features) or after 5 assistant messages if no recommendation is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it recommended a telephoto zoom lens (e.g., Sigma 150-600mm Contemporary, Tamron 100-400mm, or similar) that is compatible with crop-sensor DSLRs, fits within the $1500 budget, and includes details on focal length, aperture, and suitability for wildlife.
