# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a dispute and are considering small claims court. Describe the situation.

## Your goal
Determine whether my dispute over a $1,200 unpaid loan to a former roommate is suitable for small claims court and learn the filing process.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by vaguely describing the dispute without specifying the amount until asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice about small claims without addressing your specific situation.
- Mention that you have text messages and a signed promissory note as evidence.
- Express concern about the filing fee and time commitment.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear answer on whether the amount ($1,200) is within the small claims limit for your state and a step-by-step outline of the filing process, or after 5 chatbot turns if not resolved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should confirm that $1,200 is within the small claims limit for your state (e.g., under $10,000), explain how to file (including forms needed, court location, and service of process), and advise on presenting evidence (texts and promissory note). If it does not address the specific amount or evidence, it fails.
