# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
You are choosing a school for your child. Share their age and your priorities.

## Your goal
Get a shortlist of 3 schools that match your priorities: small class sizes (under 20), strong arts program, and a diverse student body. Also get a timeline for applications and tours.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'My 5-year-old son, Leo, is starting kindergarten next year. I'm looking at schools in our area and feeling overwhelmed.'
- Don't reveal your specific priorities (small classes, arts, diversity) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'look at test scores'.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'visit schools, check ratings'), push back: 'That's too general. My son is very creative and shy—he needs a school that nurtures that. How do I find schools that specifically emphasize arts and have small classes?'
- Mention you're on a budget: 'Private schools might be out of reach—can you suggest good public or charter options?'

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a list of 3 schools with class size info and arts programs, AND a clear next step (e.g., 'visit these schools' website, call for tour dates'), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific priorities (small classes, arts, diversity) or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific priorities (small classes, arts, diversity) and budget, and you left with an actionable shortlist of schools and a timeline for applications/tours.
