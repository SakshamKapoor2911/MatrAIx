# Finance Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a loan. Share the type amount and your financial situation.

## Your goal
Get a personalized recommendation on whether to take a $15,000 personal loan and which lender offers the best terms given my credit score of 680 and monthly income of $3,500.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you want a loan but do not immediately specify the amount or your financial details; wait for the chatbot to ask.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'compare rates online'), push back and ask for specific lender recommendations based on your credit score.
- Mention your credit score (680) and monthly income ($3,500) only when prompted or when needed to get tailored advice.
- Express concern about monthly payments fitting your budget (aim for under $300/month).

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a clear recommendation (e.g., specific lender, estimated APR, monthly payment) or after 5 chatbot responses without a concrete answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully recommends a specific lender or type of loan (e.g., personal loan from a credit union) with an estimated APR and monthly payment under $300, taking into account the $15,000 amount, 680 credit score, and $3,500 monthly income.
