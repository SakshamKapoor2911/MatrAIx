# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your children argue or fight frequently. Share their ages and what typically triggers conflicts.

## Your goal
Get a concrete strategy to reduce specific sibling conflicts between your 8-year-old and 5-year-old, including how to handle the morning routine and toy-sharing disputes.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'My two kids, ages 8 and 5, constantly argue over toys and during the morning rush. It's exhausting.'
- Don't reveal your specific question about morning routine until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'set clear rules,' push back by asking how to apply that when your 5-year-old can't understand long explanations.
- Mention that you've tried time-outs and taking toys away, but they don't work long-term.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a step-by-step plan that addresses both the morning routine with a timer and a toy rotation schedule, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific morning chaos or toy disputes.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific children's ages and conflict triggers (morning routine, toy sharing), and you left with an actionable first step like using a visual timer or implementing a toy rotation.
