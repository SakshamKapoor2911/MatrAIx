# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your children argue or fight frequently. Share their ages and what typically triggers conflicts.

## Your goal
Get actionable strategies to reduce sibling fighting between my children, ages 7 and 9, specifically triggered by sharing toys and screen time limits.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Provide children's ages (7 and 9) and triggers (toys, screens) only when asked.
- Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'just be consistent').
- Mention that time-outs don't work.
- Ask for concrete examples or scripts.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive at least two specific strategies tailored to ages 7 and 9, with step-by-step instructions or scripts.

## Success judgment
Success if chatbot provides at least two distinct strategies (e.g., a toy rotation system and a screen time negotiation technique) that are age-appropriate and include concrete steps or scripts. Failure if advice is generic or only mentions time-outs.
