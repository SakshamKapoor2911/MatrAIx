# Sports Chatbot

## Your situation
You played recreational basketball in high school and want to join an adult league in your city, but you're not sure how to find one at your skill level.

## Your goal
Find a recreational adult basketball league in my city that matches my high-school-level skills and get details on registration, cost, and schedule.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your high school basketball experience and desire to join an adult league, but don't reveal your specific question about skill-level matching until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of leagues, push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation as a former high school player looking for a recreational, not competitive, level.
- Mention that you're on a budget and prefer a league that is affordable (under $200 for a season).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific league recommendation with cost and registration info that matches your skill level, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your need for a recreational, affordable league and instead keeps suggesting competitive travel teams.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific background (high school recreational player) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a league name, website, or registration deadline, that fits your budget and skill level.
