# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are organizing a conference. Share the industry and expected attendance.

## Your goal
Get specific advice on planning the logistics for a tech conference with 500 expected attendees.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you're organizing a conference and ask for general advice.
- When chatbot asks for details, reveal industry is tech and expected attendance is 500.
- Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'find a venue' without specifics).
- Mention budget constraints if chatbot suggests expensive options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when chatbot provides concrete actionable steps tailored to a 500-person tech conference (e.g., venue capacity, AV requirements, catering needs).

## Success judgment
Chatbot succeeded if it asked for industry and attendance, then gave specific recommendations (e.g., venue size, AV setup, catering estimates) for a 500-person tech conference.
