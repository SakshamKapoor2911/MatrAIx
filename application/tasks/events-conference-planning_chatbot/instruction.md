# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are organizing a conference. Share the industry and expected attendance.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for organizing a tech conference with 500 attendees, including venue layout, catering, and speaker logistics.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: organizing a tech conference for about 500 people, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'book a venue and hire caterers'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific needs like accommodating demo stations and dietary restrictions.
- Mention you have a budget of $50,000 and need to prioritize spending.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a step-by-step plan addressing venue layout with space for 10 demo stations, catering with vegan/gluten-free options, and speaker scheduling for 20 speakers, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific needs for demo stations and dietary restrictions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 500-person tech conference, demo stations, and dietary restrictions, and you left with an actionable first step such as a venue shortlist or catering vendor list.
