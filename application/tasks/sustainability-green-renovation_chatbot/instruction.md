# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You are renovating and want to be sustainable. Share your project scope and budget.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice on sustainable renovation materials and methods for a bathroom remodel, staying within a $10,000 budget and preferring recycled or low-VOC options.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Initially provide only the room type (bathroom) and ask for general sustainable advice before revealing budget or specific constraints.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests generic or non-sustainable options (e.g., standard vinyl flooring).', 'Mention the $10,000 budget and preference for recycled/low-VOC materials after the first exchange.', 'Ask for specific product recommendations or suppliers if the chatbot gives vague advice.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a concrete list of sustainable materials (e.g., recycled glass tiles, low-VOC paint, bamboo flooring) with estimated costs that fit your budget, or after 5 exchanges without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides at least three specific sustainable material recommendations (e.g., recycled glass tiles, low-VOC paint, reclaimed wood vanity) with approximate costs that total under $10,000 and mentions environmental benefits.
