# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You are renovating and want to be sustainable. Share your project scope and budget.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for sustainable renovation within budget, including specific material recommendations and waste reduction strategies.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your renovation project (e.g., kitchen remodel, 100 sq ft) and mention you want to be sustainable, but don't reveal your specific budget or questions until the chatbot asks.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips (e.g., 'use reclaimed wood'), push back by asking how it applies to your specific project (e.g., 'I need a countertop that fits my small kitchen and budget').
- Mention your budget is $5,000 total, so you need affordable solutions.
- Ask for trade-offs between cost and sustainability if the chatbot suggests expensive options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific list of materials (e.g., bamboo flooring, recycled glass countertops) with cost estimates and a step-by-step plan, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your $5,000 budget constraint or specific room (kitchen).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific kitchen renovation and $5,000 budget (not generic sustainability tips) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'source reclaimed wood from local salvage yard') you could take.
