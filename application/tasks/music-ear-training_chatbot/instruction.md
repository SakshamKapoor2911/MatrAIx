# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been playing piano for a year and can read sheet music, but you can't identify intervals or chords by ear.

## Your goal
Get a structured, beginner-friendly ear training plan to learn to identify intervals and chords by ear, starting with intervals (minor 2nd, major 2nd, minor 3rd, major 3rd) and triads (major and minor).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Mention your current skill level: can read sheet music, played piano for a year, but cannot identify intervals or chords by ear.
- Ask for a step-by-step plan that starts with basic intervals and chords, not advanced concepts.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests generic advice like 'use an app' without specific exercises or a progression.
- If the chatbot asks about your practice schedule, mention you have about 15-20 minutes per day for ear training.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear, actionable plan that includes specific intervals to practice (e.g., minor 2nd, major 2nd, minor 3rd, major 3rd) and two types of triads (major and minor) with recommended exercises or methods.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides a structured ear training plan that includes: (1) a list of intervals to start with (at least minor 2nd, major 2nd, minor 3rd, major 3rd), (2) a list of chords to start with (at least major and minor triads), and (3) specific exercises (e.g., sing the notes, play and guess, use reference songs) or a progression order. The plan should be tailored to a beginner with limited daily practice time.
