# Music Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been playing piano for a year and can read sheet music, but you can't identify intervals or chords by ear.

## Your goal
Get a structured 4-week ear training plan that starts with intervals and progresses to chords, with specific exercises and resources for someone who can read music but can't identify sounds.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your background: you play piano for a year, can read sheet music, but can't identify intervals or chords by ear.
- Don't reveal your specific goal (a structured plan) until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'practice intervals'.
- If the chatbot suggests generic apps or vague tips, push back by asking how it applies to your specific situation: e.g., 'I've tried apps, but I need a step-by-step plan that builds from intervals to chords.'
- Mention you have 15 minutes per day to practice and prefer free resources.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete 4-week plan with specific interval exercises (like 'Week 1: minor vs major 2nds using these reference songs') and chord exercises, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided a structured plan tailored to your skill level and time constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice included a week-by-week schedule with specific interval and chord exercises (e.g., using reference songs you know), acknowledged your 15-minute daily practice limit, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'Start with Week 1: practice major and minor 2nds using 'Happy Birthday' for major 2nd').
