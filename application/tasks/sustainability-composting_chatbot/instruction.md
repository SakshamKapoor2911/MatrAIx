# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in an apartment with a small balcony and want to start composting kitchen scraps without attracting pests.

## Your goal
Get a pest-free composting solution suitable for a small apartment balcony, specifically for kitchen scraps like fruit peels, vegetable trimmings, coffee grounds, and eggshells.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Mention you live in an apartment with a small balcony and cannot have open piles or strong odors.', '- Withhold that you have a worm bin initially; only reveal if asked about space or container preferences.', '- Push back on solutions that require significant outdoor space, like large tumblers or open bins.', '- Ask about pest prevention (e.g., fruit flies, rodents) and odor control if not addressed.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you receive a specific recommendation (e.g., Bokashi bin, small tumbling composter, or worm bin setup) with clear steps to avoid pests, or after 5 turns if no actionable answer is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it recommended a compact, enclosed system (e.g., Bokashi, small tumbler, or worm bin) with explicit pest prevention tips (e.g., burying scraps, using a tight lid, adding browns) and acknowledged the balcony space constraint.
