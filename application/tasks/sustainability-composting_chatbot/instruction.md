# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in an apartment with a small balcony and want to start composting kitchen scraps without attracting pests.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step plan for setting up a small-scale composting system on your apartment balcony that handles vegetable scraps and coffee grounds without attracting pests, including specific container and material recommendations.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your apartment balcony and kitchen scrap situation, but don't reveal your specific question about pest prevention until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'use a bin, add browns, turn it'), push back and ask how to apply that to your small balcony space with limited ventilation and concerns about fruit flies and odors.
- Mention that you have a small indoor worm bin that failed due to smell, so you need a method that truly works for a small apartment.
- Ask about specific materials like a Bokashi bucket or a sealed tumbler, and whether you can compost citrus peels and onions without issues.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete plan with specific container type (e.g., Bokashi bucket or tumbler), material ratios, and a pest prevention step you can implement immediately, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific balcony space constraints and pest concerns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific apartment balcony and pest concerns (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific container to buy or a recipe for a balanced compost mix.
