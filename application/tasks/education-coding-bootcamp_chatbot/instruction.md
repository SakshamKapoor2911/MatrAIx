# Education Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a coding bootcamp. Share your background goals and budget.

## Your goal
Get a clear comparison of 2-3 bootcamps that fit my background in graphic design with no coding experience, and a realistic budget of $12,000 max, including financing options.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your background in graphic design and interest in coding, but don't reveal your specific budget or need for comparison until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of bootcamps without considering your design background, push back and ask how the curriculum would leverage your design skills.
- Mention your $12,000 budget constraint and ask about payment plans or scholarships if the chatbot suggests an expensive program.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a shortlist of 2-3 bootcamps with cost, duration, and mention of design-focused tracks, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your design background or budget constraint.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendations referenced your graphic design background (e.g., suggested bootcamps with UI/UX or front-end tracks) and you left with a clear next step like a specific bootcamp to research further or a financing option to apply for.
