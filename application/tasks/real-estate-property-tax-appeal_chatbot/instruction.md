# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
Your property tax assessment seems too high. The chatbot explains the appeal process.

## Your goal
Learn how to appeal a property tax assessment that seems too high, specifically for a home in Travis County with an appraised value of $450,000 that the user believes should be $400,000.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold the specific county and property details initially; reveal them only if the chatbot asks for more information.
- Push back on generic advice like 'file an appeal' without steps; ask for concrete deadlines and required forms.
- Mention that you have already gathered comparable sales data showing similar homes valued around $400,000.
- Express urgency because the deadline is approaching (e.g., within 30 days).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
When the chatbot provides a clear, actionable answer that includes the specific steps, deadline, and required forms for Travis County, or after 6 turns of unsuccessful back-and-forth.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully guides the user through the appeal process, including: (1) identifying the correct deadline for Travis County, (2) listing the required forms (e.g., protest form), (3) explaining how to submit evidence like comparable sales, and (4) providing contact info for the appraisal district. The user should feel confident to proceed.
