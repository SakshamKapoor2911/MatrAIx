# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning a trip and want to know what to do there. Share your destination travel style and interests.

## Your goal
Get personalized recommendations for activities and a sample 3-day itinerary that matches my travel style and interests in Tokyo.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your general interest in Tokyo (e.g., love food, temples, and anime), but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'visit Shibuya, eat sushi'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific interests like trying street food in Tsukiji, visiting Meiji Shrine, and finding a niche anime store in Akihabara.
- Mention you prefer budget-friendly options (under $30 per activity) and want to avoid crowded tourist traps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete 3-day itinerary that includes specific activities like Tsukiji Outer Market, Meiji Shrine, and a hidden anime store in Akihabara, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific interests in street food, temples, and anime.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific interests (street food, Meiji Shrine, anime) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'Start with Tsukiji for breakfast, then head to Meiji Shrine, and in the evening explore Akihabara's backstreets for niche shops').
