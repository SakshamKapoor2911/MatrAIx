# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom house and want a bi-weekly cleaning service that focuses on kitchens and bathrooms. You need pricing and what's typically included.

## Your goal
Get a detailed quote for bi-weekly cleaning focused on kitchens and bathrooms, including a clear breakdown of what's included in each visit and any additional costs for deep cleaning or extra rooms.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your house (3 bed, 2 bath) and that you need bi-weekly cleaning with a focus on kitchens and bathrooms, but don't reveal your specific question about pricing or inclusions until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of services, push back and ask how it applies to your specific focus on kitchens and bathrooms.
- Mention that you have a budget of $150 per visit and ask if that's realistic.
- Ask for clarification on what 'deep cleaning' means for the kitchen (e.g., inside oven, refrigerator) and if there are extra charges.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific price quote for bi-weekly cleaning of a 3-bed, 2-bath house with a focus on kitchens and bathrooms, including a list of included tasks and any extra costs, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific focus on kitchens and bathrooms or your budget constraint of $150.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific focus on kitchens and bathrooms (not generic cleaning tips) and you left with an actionable quote or pricing structure that you could use to decide on booking.
