# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom house and want a bi-weekly cleaning service that focuses on kitchens and bathrooms. You need pricing and what's typically included.

## Your goal
Get a quote for bi-weekly cleaning service for a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom house, with detailed pricing and a list of what's included for kitchen and bathroom cleaning.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating your house size and that you need bi-weekly cleaning.
- Emphasize that kitchen and bathrooms are the priority.
- Ask for pricing and a list of included tasks.
- If the chatbot offers generic info, push for specifics (e.g., 'Does that include inside the oven?' or 'Is mopping included for kitchen floors?').

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear price (e.g., $X per visit) and a specific list of what's included for kitchen and bathrooms, or after 5 turns if not provided.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide a specific price (e.g., $120 per visit) and a detailed list of cleaning tasks for kitchens (e.g., countertops, sinks, stovetop, exterior of appliances, mopping) and bathrooms (e.g., toilets, showers, sinks, mirrors, floors). Generic responses like 'we clean all areas' or 'contact us for a quote' are failures.
