# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have limited space but want to grow plants. Share your space type and sun exposure.

## Your goal
Get a concrete starting plan: which 3-5 plants will thrive in my specific conditions, and a simple layout for my small balcony garden.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your small balcony (4x6 ft, west-facing, gets afternoon sun 2-4 PM) but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of 'easy plants', push back and ask how it applies to your specific sun exposure and space constraints.
- Mention your budget: you can spend up to $50 total on seeds/seedlings and soil.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a concrete list of 3-5 plants with spacing instructions for your 4x6 west-facing balcony and a total cost under $50, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific west-facing sun exposure or space constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific west-facing balcony with afternoon sun and 4x6 size (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable list of plants and a planting layout you could implement with your $50 budget.
