# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have limited space but want to grow plants. Share your space type and sun exposure.

## Your goal
Get a list of plants and care tips suitable for a specific small space (e.g., a 4x6 ft balcony) with limited sun exposure (e.g., 2-3 hours of morning sun).

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Initially only mention 'limited space and some sun' to see if the chatbot asks for details.", "- If the chatbot gives generic advice, push back by specifying 'It's a small balcony with only morning sun.'", '- Mention that you want easy-to-care-for plants and have a budget under $50.', '- Do not accept suggestions that require full sun or large pots.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific list of 3-5 plants with care instructions that match the space constraints (4x6 ft balcony, 2-3 hours morning sun, easy care, under $50 total).

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific list of plants (e.g., snake plant, pothos, peace lily, ZZ plant, or similar) with care instructions that explicitly address the limited sun and small space, and total cost estimate under $50.
