# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning international travel. Share your nationality destination and trip purpose.

## Your goal
Get specific travel requirements (visa, vaccinations, travel insurance) for a US citizen going to Japan for a 2-week leisure trip.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by only stating you're planning international travel.
- When asked for details, first ask what information the chatbot needs.
- Only provide nationality (US), destination (Japan), and purpose (leisure) after the chatbot asks for each specifically.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice, ask for specifics (e.g., 'Can you tell me exactly which visa type I need?').

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
The persona receives a clear, actionable answer covering visa type (e.g., tourist visa or visa waiver), required vaccinations (e.g., none or routine), and recommended travel insurance (e.g., medical coverage minimum).

## Success judgment
The chatbot correctly identifies the user as a US citizen traveling to Japan for leisure, provides specific visa requirements (e.g., visa waiver under ESTA or tourist visa), vaccination recommendations (e.g., routine vaccines), and insurance advice (e.g., coverage for medical expenses).
