# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning international travel. Share your nationality destination and trip purpose.

## Your goal
Get a clear, actionable packing list and itinerary outline tailored to your specific trip: a 10-day business trip from the US to Tokyo, Japan, with 2 extra days for sightseeing.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: traveling from the US to Tokyo for a 10-day business trip with 2 extra sightseeing days, but don't reveal your specific packing or itinerary question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'pack light' or 'visit famous temples', push back and ask how it applies to your need to pack business attire (suits, dress shoes) while also having comfortable clothes for sightseeing, and how to balance work and leisure time.
- Mention you have a tight budget for this trip (company covers business expenses but personal sightseeing must be cheap), so ask for cost-effective sightseeing options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a specific packing list that separates business and casual items, and recommends at least two free or low-cost sightseeing spots in Tokyo, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need to pack suits and dress shoes alongside sightseeing clothes or your budget constraints for sightseeing.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific trip (US to Tokyo, business + sightseeing) and your constraints (packing suits and dress shoes, cheap sightseeing) rather than generic travel tips, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a packing list or a specific sightseeing plan).
