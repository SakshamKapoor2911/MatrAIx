# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your child is ready for potty training. Share their age and current readiness signs.

## Your goal
Get a personalized potty training plan tailored to my child's specific age, readiness signs, and temperament, including step-by-step instructions and a timeline.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing my child's age (2.5 years old) and readiness signs (stays dry for 2 hours, shows interest in the toilet, can pull pants up/down), but don't reveal my specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to my child who is stubborn and resists sitting on the potty.
- Mention that we have a busy schedule with daycare and want a method that works with that routine.
- Mention budget constraints: we don't want to spend a lot on special equipment or rewards.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) I receive a concrete step-by-step plan that includes handling resistance and fits our daycare schedule, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed my child's stubbornness or our daycare constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced my child's specific age (2.5), stubbornness, and daycare schedule, and I left with an actionable first step I could take, such as a specific technique for encouraging sitting or a communication plan with daycare.
