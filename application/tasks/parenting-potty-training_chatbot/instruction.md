# Parenting Chatbot

## Your situation
Your child is ready for potty training. Share their age and current readiness signs.

## Your goal
Get a customized potty training plan for my 2.5-year-old daughter who shows readiness signs like staying dry for 2 hours, telling me when she needs to go, and being interested in the toilet.

## Constraints on your behavior
["Start by stating the child's age (2.5 years) and readiness signs (stays dry for 2 hours, tells me when needs to go, interested in toilet) without asking for specific methods.", "If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'start with a potty chair'), push back by asking for tips tailored to her specific signs.", 'Mention that we have a busy schedule and need a plan that fits our routine.', 'Avoid accepting advice that suggests waiting longer; be firm that she is ready now.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a step-by-step plan that addresses her age (2.5), readiness signs (dry intervals, verbal cues, interest), and our busy schedule, or after 5 exchanges without a satisfactory plan.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it gave a specific plan including: recommended potty chair type, a schedule for sitting on the potty (e.g., every 2 hours), and strategies for handling accidents during busy times, all referencing the child's age and signs.
