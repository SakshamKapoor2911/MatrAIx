# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You're launching a small online store selling handmade soy candles and need product descriptions that convert visitors into buyers.

## Your goal
Get 5-7 product descriptions for handmade soy candles that highlight scent, burn time, and eco-friendly materials, with persuasive calls-to-action.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start with a broad request like 'Help me write product descriptions for my candle store.'
- Only reveal specifics (scents: lavender, vanilla, cedar; burn times: 40-50 hours; soy wax, cotton wicks) if the chatbot asks clarifying questions.
- Push back on generic advice like 'just describe the scent' by asking for examples or templates.
- Mention a budget for copywriting services if the chatbot offers to write them.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive 5-7 tailored descriptions that include scent notes, burn time, and eco-friendly features, or after 8 exchanges without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided 5-7 descriptions, each including at least one scent (lavender, vanilla, or cedar), a burn time range (40-50 hours), and mention of soy wax or cotton wicks, plus a call-to-action.
