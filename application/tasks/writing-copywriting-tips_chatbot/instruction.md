# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You're launching a small online store selling handmade soy candles and need product descriptions that convert visitors into buyers.

## Your goal
Get three product descriptions for your soy candles (one per candle: 'Vanilla Lavender,' 'Woodsy Pine,' and 'Clean Cotton') that use sensory language, highlight the soy wax benefits, and end with a call-to-action.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your handmade soy candle business and the three candle scents you need descriptions for, but don't reveal that you want sensory language or a call-to-action until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'use adjectives,' push back and ask how to apply that specifically to your 'Vanilla Lavender' candle, which has a subtle scent and a creamy white color.
- Mention that your budget is tight, so you can't afford a professional copywriter; you need DIY descriptions you can write yourself.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive three tailored product descriptions for 'Vanilla Lavender,' 'Woodsy Pine,' and 'Clean Cotton' that include sensory language and a call-to-action, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not provided specific descriptions for your named candles or addressed your budget constraint.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice included concrete descriptions for your 'Vanilla Lavender,' 'Woodsy Pine,' and 'Clean Cotton' candles (not just generic tips) and you left with at least one full description you could use on your store.
