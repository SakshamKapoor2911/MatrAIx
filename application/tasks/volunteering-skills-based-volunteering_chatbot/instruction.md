# Volunteering Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a graphic designer with 5 hours a month to spare and want to create marketing materials for a local food bank.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for creating marketing materials (e.g., flyers, social media graphics) for a local food bank, including a prioritized list of deliverables and a timeline that fits within 5 hours per month.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your background as a graphic designer with 5 hours monthly, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'use Canva templates', push back and ask how to apply that to your specific food bank's needs (e.g., promoting a holiday food drive).
- Mention that you have limited software access (only free tools like GIMP and Canva free tier) and need advice on working within those constraints.
- State that you want to measure impact, so ask how to track engagement (e.g., QR codes on flyers).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a specific, actionable plan referencing your 5-hour limit and free tools, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your limited time and software constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific constraints (5 hours/month, free tools) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a template for a flyer or a content calendar for the month.
