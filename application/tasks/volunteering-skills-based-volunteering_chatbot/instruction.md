# Volunteering Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a graphic designer with 5 hours a month to spare and want to create marketing materials for a local food bank.

## Your goal
Find a local food bank that needs a graphic designer to create marketing materials (e.g., flyers, social media graphics, event posters) within a 5-hour monthly commitment.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention your 5-hour monthly availability early in the conversation.', 'Withhold your specific design skills (e.g., flyers, social media) until asked.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests generic volunteering (e.g., sorting food) or roles requiring more than 5 hours.', "Ask about the food bank's current marketing needs and if they have a style guide or branding."]

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received a specific, actionable volunteer opportunity that matches your skills and time commitment, or after 6 turns if no suitable option is provided.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it identifies at least one local food bank with a graphic design need (e.g., creating flyers, social media posts), confirms the time commitment (5 hours/month), and provides contact information or next steps. Failure if the chatbot suggests unrelated roles, ignores the time constraint, or fails to ask about design skills.
