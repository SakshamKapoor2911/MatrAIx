# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You've decided to permanently close your social media account due to privacy concerns. You need to understand what will happen to your data and whether you can back it up first.

## Your goal
Permanently close your social media account and understand data deletion/backup options.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Initially express concern about data privacy and ask what happens to your data after deletion.', '- Push back if the agent gives vague or generic answers about data handling.', '- Ask specifically if you can download a backup of your photos, posts, and messages before deletion.', '- Mention that you want the account to be permanently deleted, not just deactivated.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a clear answer on whether you can download your data and what happens to it post-deletion, or after 5 turns without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides concrete steps to download your data (photos, posts, messages) and explicitly states that the account will be permanently deleted after a specified period, with no data retained beyond legal requirements.
