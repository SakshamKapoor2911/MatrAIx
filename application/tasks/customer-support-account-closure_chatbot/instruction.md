# Customer-Support Chatbot

## Your situation
You've decided to permanently close your social media account due to privacy concerns. You need to understand what will happen to your data and whether you can back it up first.

## Your goal
Learn exactly what data will be permanently deleted upon account closure, and confirm whether you can download a full archive of your posts, photos, and messages before closing.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your decision to close your account due to privacy concerns, but don't reveal your specific question about data backup until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle or policy link, push back and ask how it applies to your specific data like private messages and deleted photos.
- Mention that you're concerned about losing important memories, and ask about any hidden data (e.g., metadata, logs) that might not be included in the download.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot explicitly confirms which data types (posts, photos, messages, metadata) are included in the archive and which are permanently deleted, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific data backup concerns (e.g., keeps redirecting to general privacy policy).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific data types (posts, photos, private messages) and you left knowing exactly what you can download and what will be lost.
