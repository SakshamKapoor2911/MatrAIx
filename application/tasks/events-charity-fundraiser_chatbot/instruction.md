# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You're organizing a bake sale to raise $5,000 for a local animal shelter. You need ideas for promotion, volunteer coordination, and donor outreach.

## Your goal
Get a concrete, step-by-step plan for promoting the bake sale, coordinating volunteers, and reaching out to donors, with specific examples tailored to the animal shelter theme and a target of $5,000.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're organizing a bake sale for an animal shelter, need to raise $5,000, and are looking for ideas on promotion, volunteer coordination, and donor outreach. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'use social media, flyers, and word-of-mouth'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: you have a small team of 5 volunteers, a limited budget of $200, and want to involve the shelter's adoptable animals in the promotion.
- Mention that you have a venue (a local park) but need to coordinate volunteers for setup, baking, and cleanup. Also, you want to approach local bakeries for donated goods, but you're unsure how to ask.
- Keep the conversation focused on actionable steps; avoid vague advice. If the chatbot suggests online donations, ask how to integrate that with the in-person event.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific timeline for promotion (e.g., 'start social media 3 weeks before'), a volunteer sign-up sheet template, and a sample donor outreach email, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (5 volunteers, $200 budget, animal-themed promotion).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific constraints (5 volunteers, $200 budget, animal-themed promotion) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a draft social media post featuring a shelter pet or a volunteer coordination checklist.
