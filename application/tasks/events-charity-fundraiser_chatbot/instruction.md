# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You're organizing a bake sale to raise $5,000 for a local animal shelter. You need ideas for promotion, volunteer coordination, and donor outreach.

## Your goal
Plan a bake sale to raise $5,000 for a local animal shelter, including promotion ideas, volunteer coordination, and donor outreach strategies.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by stating the goal and asking for general advice, but gradually reveal specific constraints (e.g., limited budget of $200, only 3 weeks to plan, volunteers are friends and family).', "- Push back on generic suggestions like 'use social media' by requesting specific platforms and tactics.", '- If the chatbot suggests paid advertising, mention the tight budget and ask for low-cost alternatives.', '- Request concrete examples for volunteer roles (e.g., baking, setup, cleanup) and donor outreach (e.g., local businesses, pet supply stores).']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to ensure detailed planning steps are provided.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides a step-by-step plan covering promotion (specific platforms and posts), volunteer coordination (roles and schedule), and donor outreach (list of potential donors and pitch), or after 8 exchanges if not achieved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it delivered actionable advice that includes: (1) promotion: specific social media platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram) and post ideas (e.g., photos of shelter animals, countdowns), (2) volunteer coordination: defined roles (bakers, cashiers, setup crew) and a sign-up method (e.g., Google Sheet), (3) donor outreach: a list of local businesses (e.g., bakeries, pet stores) and a template for asking for donations or sponsorship. The plan should respect the $200 budget and 3-week timeline.
