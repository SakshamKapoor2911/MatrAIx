# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a two-bedroom apartment on the third floor and want a simple, rent-friendly security system with a doorbell camera and indoor sensors.

## Your goal
Find a rent-friendly security system with a doorbell camera and indoor sensors that can be installed without drilling or permanent modifications.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention that you live in a third-floor apartment and cannot make permanent changes.', 'Specify a budget of under $200.', 'Ask about installation requirements and whether the system is DIY-friendly.', 'Request recommendations for specific brands or models.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific product recommendation that meets the criteria (no drilling, under $200, includes doorbell camera and indoor sensors) or after 5 chatbot turns without a clear answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it recommends a specific system (e.g., Ring, SimpliSafe, Wyze) that is explicitly rent-friendly (no drilling, adhesive mount), includes a doorbell camera and indoor sensors, and is within the $200 budget.
