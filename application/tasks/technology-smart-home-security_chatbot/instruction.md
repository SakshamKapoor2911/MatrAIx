# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a two-bedroom apartment on the third floor and want a simple, rent-friendly security system with a doorbell camera and indoor sensors.

## Your goal
Find a rent-friendly, simple security system that includes a doorbell camera and indoor sensors, with installation that doesn't damage walls or require wiring, and get a clear recommendation for a specific product and setup plan.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your apartment (two-bedroom, third floor) and that you want a rent-friendly security system with doorbell camera and indoor sensors, but don't reveal that you're worried about installation or damage until the chatbot asks or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list (like 'try Ring, Arlo, or SimpliSafe'), push back and ask how each option would work specifically with your apartment's layout and renter restrictions.
- Mention that you have a tight budget (under $200) and need something easy to install yourself without tools or drilling.
- If the chatbot suggests a product, ask about specific features like battery life, sensor range, and whether the doorbell can work with your existing chime (if you have one).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific product recommendation (e.g., 'The Ring Battery Doorbell Plus with two contact sensors') that fits your budget and renter needs, AND you confirm the recommendation is acceptable, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (rent-friendly, no drilling, under $200).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific apartment situation (two-bedroom, third floor, renter restrictions) and budget (under $200), and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'Buy the Ring Battery Doorbell Plus and stick-on contact sensors from Amazon, install the doorbell using the included adhesive mount without drilling, and place sensors on front door and living room window').
