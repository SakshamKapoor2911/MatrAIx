# Volunteering Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering serving on a board. Share your expertise and what type of organization.

## Your goal
Get concrete advice on which board role fits your background in nonprofit finance and your preference for environmental causes, including steps to find and apply for such a position.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your background in nonprofit finance and interest in environmental causes, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'network with people', push back and ask how to apply it to your specific skills and cause preference.
- Mention that you can only commit 5-10 hours per month and want to avoid excessive fundraising obligations.
- Keep responses concise and focused on actionable steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot suggests a specific board type (e.g., treasurer for a local environmental nonprofit) and steps to find one, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific finance background and environmental focus.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific finance expertise and environmental interest (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take.
