# Volunteering Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering serving on a board. Share your expertise and what type of organization.

## Your goal
Find a board position at a nonprofit organization that aligns with your expertise in financial management and your interest in youth development.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Initially state you have expertise in financial management but do not specify the type of organization until asked.', "Mention you are open to board service but want to ensure the organization's mission matches your values.", 'Ask about time commitment and expectations for board members.', 'If the chatbot suggests generic opportunities, push back and ask for specifics aligned with your expertise.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific board opportunity at a youth development nonprofit that requires financial expertise, or after 5 chatbot turns without a viable match.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identifies at least one specific board position at a youth development nonprofit, confirms that financial management expertise is needed, and provides details on how to apply or get involved.
