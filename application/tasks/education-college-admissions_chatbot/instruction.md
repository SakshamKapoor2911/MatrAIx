# Education Chatbot

## Your situation
You are applying to college. Share your target schools and what part of the process you need help with.

## Your goal
Get specific guidance on application strategy or essay writing for MIT and Stanford's computer science programs.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially only mention that you're applying to top STEM schools.
- When asked for specifics, reveal MIT and Stanford.
- Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'just be yourself').
- Mention you have strong grades but need help with extracurricular presentation.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive actionable advice on how to highlight your robotics club leadership in your essays or application, or after 5 turns without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully provided concrete suggestions (e.g., specific essay topics, how to frame activities) for your robotics club leadership to strengthen your MIT/Stanford CS applications.
