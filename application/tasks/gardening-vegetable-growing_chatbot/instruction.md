# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in USDA zone 7b with clay soil, and your family eats a lot of peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens.

## Your goal
Get a detailed plan for growing bell peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens (lettuce, kale) in USDA zone 7b clay soil, including soil amendments, planting schedule, and watering strategy.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention clay soil and zone 7b early in the conversation.', 'Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice not tailored to clay soil or zone 7b.', 'Ask specific questions about soil drainage improvement and watering frequency.', 'Indicate the family eats a lot of these crops, so yield matters.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
The persona ends the conversation when the chatbot provides actionable steps for soil amendment (e.g., adding compost, gypsum), a planting schedule for zone 7b, and watering tips for clay soil, or after 5 exchanges without satisfactory answers.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends specific soil amendments for clay (e.g., organic matter, gypsum), gives planting dates for peppers (after last frost), zucchini (warm soil), and leafy greens (early spring/fall), and advises on watering (deep, infrequent to avoid waterlogging).
