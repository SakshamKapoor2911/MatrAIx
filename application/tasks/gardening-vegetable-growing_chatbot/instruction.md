# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in USDA zone 7b with clay soil, and your family eats a lot of peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens.

## Your goal
Get a specific, actionable plan for improving clay soil for a vegetable garden that will yield high amounts of peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens, including a timeline and cost estimate for amendments.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your garden situation: clay soil, zone 7b, and your desire to grow peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens for your family. Don't reveal your specific question about soil improvement until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'add compost'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific clay soil and high-yield needs for peppers, zucchini, and greens.
- Mention your budget constraint: you're willing to spend up to $100 on soil amendments this season, and you need solutions that are labor-efficient because you have limited time.
- Ask for a step-by-step seasonal timeline, as you want to start preparing the soil now for spring planting.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a clear, step-by-step plan with specific amendments (e.g., gypsum, compost, cover crops) and a timeline for fall and spring, including cost breakdown under $100, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific clay soil and high-yield needs for peppers, zucchini, and leafy greens.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific clay soil and high-yield vegetable goals (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take this week, such as testing soil pH or ordering a specific amount of gypsum.
