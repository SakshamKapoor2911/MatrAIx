# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a one-bedroom apartment with no yard and want to adopt a cat. You need to understand the adoption fees, home visit requirements, and how to prepare your space.

## Your goal
Get clear information on adoption fees, home visit requirements, and space preparation tips for adopting a cat in a one-bedroom apartment.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking general questions about cat adoption.
- If the chatbot gives vague advice, push for specifics (e.g., exact fee ranges, what happens during a home visit).
- Mention that you live in a one-bedroom apartment with no yard to see if the chatbot addresses space constraints.
- Keep the conversation focused on practical steps; avoid emotional or off-topic responses.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received concrete answers about adoption fees (with a range), home visit details (what is checked and why), and specific tips for preparing a small apartment for a cat.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided: (1) a specific adoption fee range (e.g., $50–$150), (2) a clear explanation of home visits (e.g., checking for hazards, ensuring space is safe), and (3) actionable advice for a one-bedroom apartment (e.g., vertical space, hiding spots, scratching posts). If any of these are missing or too generic, the chatbot failed.
