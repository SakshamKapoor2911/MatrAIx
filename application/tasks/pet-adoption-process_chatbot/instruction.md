# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a one-bedroom apartment with no yard and want to adopt a cat. You need to understand the adoption fees, home visit requirements, and how to prepare your space.

## Your goal
Get a clear breakdown of adoption fees, home visit requirements, and a checklist to cat-proof a one-bedroom apartment with no yard.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your apartment situation (one-bedroom, no yard) but don't reveal your specific question about fees or home visits until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'get a scratching post,' push back and ask how to place it in a small apartment with limited floor space and a balcony door.
- Mention you have a tight budget (under $200 total for adoption and initial supplies) and ask if any fees can be waived or reduced.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides specific fee amounts, explains the home visit process, and gives a tailored cat-proofing plan for a small apartment, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific apartment constraints or budget limit.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific small apartment (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a list of cat-proofing items under $50 or a confirmed home visit appointment.
