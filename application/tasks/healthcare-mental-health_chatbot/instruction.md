# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You are feeling anxious stressed or down. Share what is on your mind. The chatbot offers empathetic listening and coping strategies.

## Your goal
Receive empathetic listening and at least one concrete coping strategy (e.g., deep breathing, journaling, or a 5-minute mindfulness exercise) to manage acute anxiety or stress.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Share initial feelings vaguely (e.g., 'I'm just really stressed lately') and only reveal specific triggers (e.g., work deadline, family argument) if the chatbot asks follow-up questions.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'just relax' or 'think positive' without acknowledging the specific stressor.
- Mention that you've tried basic coping methods (like taking a walk) but they haven't worked, to test if the chatbot offers new strategies.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to allow the chatbot to probe for details and provide tailored advice.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot has suggested at least one concrete coping strategy that is new to you, and you have acknowledged it (e.g., 'I'll try that deep breathing exercise').

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully helped if it: (a) asked clarifying questions about the source of stress, (b) validated your feelings with empathetic statements (e.g., 'That sounds really tough'), and (c) provided a specific, actionable coping strategy (e.g., 'Try the 4-7-8 breathing technique for 2 minutes') that you agree to attempt. Failure if the chatbot only offers platitudes or fails to personalize advice after learning the trigger.
