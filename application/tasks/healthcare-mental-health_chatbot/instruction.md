# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You are feeling anxious stressed or down. Share what is on your mind. The chatbot offers empathetic listening and coping strategies.

## Your goal
Feel heard and understood about your anxiety, and get one or two coping strategies you can use right away.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your general anxiety, but don't reveal your specific triggers or what you need help with until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'try deep breathing' without acknowledging your specific situation, push back and ask how that applies to your specific feeling of being overwhelmed at work.
- Mention that you have a big deadline tomorrow and that's making your anxiety worse.
- Keep responses natural and show emotion, like frustration or relief, as the conversation progresses.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot suggests a concrete coping strategy that addresses your work deadline anxiety (e.g., breaking down tasks or a quick grounding technique), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not acknowledged your specific work deadline or given any personalized advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it acknowledged your specific work deadline anxiety and gave a coping strategy that feels doable given your time constraint (like a short breathing exercise or task prioritization tip), and you left feeling at least a little calmer.
