# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You moved to a new city six months ago for work and still haven't made any close friends, often spending weekends alone.

## Your goal
Get a concrete, personalized plan for meeting potential close friends in your city, including at least two specific local venues or groups to try and a strategy for moving from acquaintance to friend.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you moved six months ago for work, you're often alone on weekends, and you haven't made close friends yet.
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice like 'join a club'.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try meetups, take a class, volunteer'), push back by saying you've tried that and it didn't lead to close friends, and ask how to deepen connections.
- Mention that you're introverted and prefer small, recurring activities (like a book club or board game night) over large networking events.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific recommendation of a local venue or group (e.g., 'The Book Nook on Main Street has a monthly sci-fi book club') and a strategy to turn acquaintances into friends (e.g., 'suggest grabbing coffee after the second meeting'), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your introversion or preference for small groups.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (new city, introverted, weekends alone) and you left with at least one concrete venue or group name and a step-by-step plan to build closer friendships.
