# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You moved to a new city six months ago for work and still haven't made any close friends, often spending weekends alone.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice on how to make close friends in a new city, specifically targeting activities or groups that match my interests (e.g., book clubs, hiking groups, or volunteer opportunities).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Express loneliness and frustration early on.
- Ask for specific local resources or events (e.g., 'Are there any book clubs near downtown?').
- Push back if advice is too generic (e.g., 'I've tried meetup.com, but it didn't work.').
- Mention that I'm an introvert and prefer small groups.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides at least three concrete suggestions (e.g., specific local groups, apps, or recurring events) that match my stated interests and introvert preference.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it suggests at least three specific, local resources (e.g., 'Downtown Book Club meets every Tuesday at 7pm,' 'Sunday morning hiking group at Riverside Park,' 'Volunteer at the animal shelter on Saturdays') and acknowledges my introversion by recommending low-pressure settings.
