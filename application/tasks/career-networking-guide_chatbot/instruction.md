# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a software developer looking to transition into a tech lead role and want to expand your network by attending industry meetups and connecting with senior engineers on LinkedIn.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for identifying 2-3 relevant industry meetups and a strategy for crafting LinkedIn connection requests to senior engineers that lead to informational interviews.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current role as a software developer and your goal to move into a tech lead role, but don't reveal that you want help with meetups or LinkedIn until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'attend more meetups' or 'connect with people', push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation, e.g., 'Which meetups would be relevant for a backend developer aiming for tech lead?' or 'What should I say in my LinkedIn request to a senior engineer I don't know?'
- Mention that you have a limited budget for paid meetups or courses, and prefer free or low-cost options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you have a list of 2-3 specific meetup names (e.g., 'Tech Lead Meetup' or 'Backend Leaders NYC') and a sample LinkedIn message template tailored to your background, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your need for specific meetups and LinkedIn message advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific background as a software developer aiming for tech lead (not generic networking tips) and you left with actionable next steps, such as a meetup to attend this month and a draft LinkedIn message to send.
