# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a software developer looking to transition into a tech lead role and want to expand your network by attending industry meetups and connecting with senior engineers on LinkedIn.

## Your goal
Get specific recommendations for 3-4 industry meetups in your area focused on tech leadership and a strategy to connect with senior engineers on LinkedIn.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Initially mention you're a software developer looking to move into a tech lead role.", "- Push back if the chatbot suggests generic advice (like 'just network more') without specific meetup names or LinkedIn outreach templates.", '- Mention you have a budget of $50/month for events and limited time (evenings only).', '- Ask for concrete examples of LinkedIn messages to senior engineers.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive at least 3 meetup suggestions with names and locations, and a sample LinkedIn outreach message, or after 5 chatbot responses if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provides at least 3 specific meetup names (e.g., 'Tech Lead Meetup SF') with details on timing/cost, and a template message for LinkedIn (e.g., 'Hi [Name], I admire your work on [Project]...').
