# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You're moving to a rural area with zip code 12345 and need reliable 5G coverage for remote work.

## Your goal
Get a specific recommendation for a 5G plan that works in zip code 12345, including coverage details, pricing, and any equipment needed.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: moving to a rural area with zip code 12345 for remote work, need reliable 5G.
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle, push back and ask how it applies to your specific zip code 12345.
- Mention budget constraints: you can spend up to $70 per month.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific plan recommendation with coverage details for zip code 12345, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific zip code or budget.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific zip code 12345 and budget, and you left with a specific plan name and coverage assurance for remote work.
