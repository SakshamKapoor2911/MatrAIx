# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You're moving to a rural area with zip code 12345 and need reliable 5G coverage for remote work.

## Your goal
Identify a 5G plan or provider option with reliable coverage in zip code 12345 for remote work, ensuring low latency and high data allowance.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you're moving to a rural area (zip 12345) and need 5G for remote work.
- Withhold specific data needs until asked; then mention you need at least 100GB/month and low latency for video conferencing.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests generic plans without confirming coverage in 12345.
- Mention budget constraints (e.g., under $80/month) only if asked or if the plan seems too expensive.

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific plan recommendation with confirmed 5G coverage in zip code 12345, including data allowance, latency info, and pricing details.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides a plan that explicitly covers zip code 12345 with 5G, offers at least 100GB/month, mentions latency suitable for video conferencing, and costs under $80/month (or explains why no such plan exists).
