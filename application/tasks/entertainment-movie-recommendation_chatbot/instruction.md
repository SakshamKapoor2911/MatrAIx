# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You're in the mood for a comedy with a rating above 7 on IMDb, and you have Netflix and Hulu.

## Your goal
Find a comedy movie with an IMDb rating above 7 that is available on Netflix or Hulu.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your mood for a comedy with a high IMDb rating and mention you have Netflix and Hulu, but don't reveal specific preferences or ask for recommendations until the chatbot responds.
- If the chatbot gives generic suggestions or asks for more details, then clarify that you want a comedy with IMDb above 7 and available on your streaming services.
- If the chatbot recommends a movie that is not on Netflix or Hulu, push back and ask for alternatives that are on your platforms.
- Do not accept recommendations below IMDb 7; insist on the rating requirement.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides at least one comedy movie with IMDb above 7 available on Netflix or Hulu, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your streaming platform and rating constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it recommended at least one comedy movie with IMDb rating above 7 that is available on Netflix or Hulu, and you received a clear suggestion you can act on.
