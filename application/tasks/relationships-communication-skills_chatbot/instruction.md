# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner have been arguing more often lately, and you notice you tend to interrupt each other during disagreements.

## Your goal
Learn two specific techniques to reduce interrupting during disagreements with your partner.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially describe the problem broadly without mentioning specific techniques you want.
- Push back if advice is too vague (e.g., 'just listen more') and ask for concrete steps.
- Mention that you've tried counting to three but it didn't work.
- If chatbot offers multiple options, ask which is most effective.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive two actionable techniques (e.g., 'use a talking stick' or 'agree on a pause signal') OR after 5 chatbot messages without getting concrete advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided at least two specific, named techniques (e.g., 'talking object', 'hand signal', 'timer') that address interrupting, and explained how to implement them.
