# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
You and your partner have been arguing more often lately, and you notice you tend to interrupt each other during disagreements.

## Your goal
Learn a specific strategy to reduce interrupting during arguments with my partner, and get a simple exercise we can try this week.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your recent arguments and the interrupting pattern, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'listen more', push back and ask how to apply it to your specific interrupting habit.
- Mention that you've tried counting to three before speaking but it felt forced, and ask for an alternative.
- No budget constraints.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot suggests a concrete technique (e.g., 'use a talking stick' or 'set a timer for 2 minutes per person') that you can try, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific interrupting pattern.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific interrupting pattern (not just generic listening tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, like a specific exercise or rule to try this week.
