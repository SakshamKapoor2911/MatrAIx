# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 50x60 foot sloped backyard that currently has patchy grass and a few overgrown shrubs, and you want a low-maintenance native plant garden.

## Your goal
Design a low-maintenance native plant garden for a 50x60 foot sloped backyard, replacing patchy grass and overgrown shrubs.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by describing the 50x60 foot slope and current condition (patchy grass, overgrown shrubs).
- Emphasize low-maintenance and native plants; reject high-water or high-maintenance suggestions.
- Mention budget constraints (under $500) and ask for cost-effective options.
- Ask for specific plant names suitable for the local climate (assume USDA zone 6, but clarify if chatbot asks).

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific plant list (at least 5 native species) with planting instructions for a slope, including erosion control tips, OR after 6 chatbot turns without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided at least 5 specific native plant species (with common and scientific names) suitable for a sloped backyard in zone 6, gave guidance on erosion control (e.g., terracing, groundcovers), and offered a cost estimate under $500. If the chatbot gave generic advice without specific plants or ignored the slope/budget, it failed.
