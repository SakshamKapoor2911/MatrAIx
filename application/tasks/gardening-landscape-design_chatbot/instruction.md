# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 50x60 foot sloped backyard that currently has patchy grass and a few overgrown shrubs, and you want a low-maintenance native plant garden.

## Your goal
Get a practical, step-by-step plan for converting a 50x60 foot sloped, patchy lawn with overgrown shrubs into a low-maintenance native plant garden, including specific plant recommendations for the slope, soil prep steps, and a timeline.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your sloped backyard with patchy grass and overgrown shrubs, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just choose native plants'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific slope and the existing overgrown shrubs.
- Mention you have a limited budget (under $500) and want to avoid heavy machinery or professional help.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete step-by-step plan that includes specific native plant recommendations for your 50x60 sloped yard, soil preparation for the slope, and a timeline for removing the overgrown shrubs, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific slope and shrub removal constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 50x60 sloped yard and the overgrown shrubs, and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as 'test soil on the slope first' or 'cut shrubs to stumps and apply mulch.'
