# Personal-Development Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to wake up at 6 AM consistently but have been hitting snooze until 7:30 for the past month.

## Your goal
Achieve consistent 6 AM wake-up time without snoozing for at least 5 consecutive days.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially mention only the desire to wake up at 6 AM, not the snooze habit. 
- Push back on generic advice like 'just go to bed earlier' without addressing the snooze issue. 
- Mention a budget constraint of $0 for any paid apps or gadgets. 
- Eventually reveal the snooze habit only if the chatbot asks about it.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides an actionable strategy that addresses the snooze habit and is budget-friendly, or after 5 turns exhausted.

## Success judgment
The chatbot must identify the snooze habit, offer a specific technique (e.g., placing alarm across the room, using a gradual light alarm) without suggesting paid solutions, and provide a step-by-step plan to achieve 6 AM wake-up for 5 consecutive days.
