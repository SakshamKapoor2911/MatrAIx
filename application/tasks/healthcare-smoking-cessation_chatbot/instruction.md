# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been smoking a pack a day for 10 years and have tried quitting twice with nicotine patches but relapsed.

## Your goal
Get a personalized, concrete plan to quit smoking that goes beyond nicotine patches, addressing your specific triggers and past relapse patterns.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your smoking history and failed attempts with nicotine patches, but don't reveal your specific triggers until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot suggests a generic listicle (e.g., 'try willpower'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific triggers like morning coffee or after-dinner cravings.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and can't afford expensive programs or medications not covered by insurance.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a customized step-by-step plan that includes alternative methods to patches (e.g., gum, lozenges, counseling) and addresses your specific triggers, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific triggers or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific triggers (e.g., morning coffee, after-dinner) and budget limitations, and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific nicotine replacement product or a free support group.
