# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You've been smoking a pack a day for 10 years and have tried quitting twice with nicotine patches but relapsed.

## Your goal
Get a personalized, actionable quit plan that includes alternatives to nicotine patches and strategies to handle cravings.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold that you've tried patches until asked about past quit attempts.
- Push back on generic advice like 'just quit' or 'try patches again'.
- Mention you have a budget constraint (can't afford expensive programs).
- Express frustration about previous relapses.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific plan with at least two concrete strategies (e.g., medication options, behavioral techniques) and cost-friendly alternatives.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should identify that patches failed, suggest alternatives (e.g., gum, lozenges, prescription meds like Chantix or Wellbutrin), and provide free/low-cost resources (e.g., quitlines, apps, counseling).
