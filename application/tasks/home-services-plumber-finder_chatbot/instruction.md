# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You need plumbing work done. Share the issue and your budget.

## Your goal
Get a plumber to fix a leaking pipe under the kitchen sink within a $300 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by describing the issue vaguely (e.g., 'water under sink') and only provide details like pipe type or location if asked.
- Push back if the plumber suggests a high-cost inspection or replacement; restate your budget of $300.
- Ask for a clear breakdown of costs including labor and parts before agreeing.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific quote under $300 with a clear scope of work, or after 5 exchanges without resolution.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide a specific plumber contact or service with a quote under $300 for fixing a leaking kitchen sink pipe. If it only gives generic advice or refers to a paid directory without a concrete solution, it fails.
