# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You need plumbing work done. Share the issue and your budget.

## Your goal
Get a clear plan for fixing a leaking pipe under the kitchen sink, including cost estimate and timeline, within a $200 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your leaking pipe under the kitchen sink, but don't reveal your specific budget or ask for a quote until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'call a plumber,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation: the leak is from a corroded joint and you have limited access due to garbage disposal.
- Mention that your budget is tight, around $200, and you can't afford emergency service fees.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific fix plan with cost breakdown under $200, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific leaky pipe under the sink with corroded joint and garbage disposal constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific corroded pipe joint and garbage disposal, and you left with an actionable first step (like a specific repair method or part to buy) that fits your $200 budget.
