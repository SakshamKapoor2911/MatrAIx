# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a graphic designer needing a laptop that can handle Adobe Creative Suite smoothly, with a budget of $1,500. You also want good color accuracy.

## Your goal
Find a specific laptop model (or shortlist of 2-3) under $1,500 with a high-quality display (sRGB 100% or DCI-P3 coverage) that runs Adobe Creative Suite smoothly, and get a clear recommendation on whether to prioritize GPU or RAM for your workflow.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your need for a laptop for graphic design work with Adobe Creative Suite, but don't reveal your budget or specific display requirements until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'get a laptop with an i7 and 16GB RAM'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific need for color-accurate photo editing and whether a dedicated GPU is worth the extra cost.
- Mention your budget of $1,500 only after the chatbot suggests a high-end model or if it asks about price range.
- If the chatbot recommends a MacBook, ask about compatibility with Adobe plugins you use (e.g., specific font or brush packs).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific laptop model under $1,500 with a display that covers at least 100% sRGB and explains why it fits your workflow, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget of $1,500 or your need for color accuracy.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific need for color-accurate display (e.g., mentioned sRGB or DCI-P3 coverage) and budget constraint, and you left with a concrete laptop model or a clear trade-off between GPU and RAM that you can act on.
