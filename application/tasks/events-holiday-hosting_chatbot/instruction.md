# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are hosting a holiday gathering. Share which holiday and how many guests.

## Your goal
Plan a holiday gathering for New Year's Eve with 15 guests, including menu, drinks, and activities.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating the holiday (New Year's Eve) and guest count (15) without additional details initially.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests generic party ideas (e.g., 'just have snacks') without specifics.
- Mention a budget of $200 for food and drinks after the first suggestion.
- Request at least one activity that accommodates both adults and children.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a concrete menu with 3 dishes, a drink recipe for a non-alcoholic punch, and a game idea suitable for ages 5-50, or after 5 exchanges without a complete plan.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific menu (e.g., finger sandwiches, veggie platter, mini quiches), a non-alcoholic punch recipe (e.g., sparkling cider with fruit), and a game (e.g., charades with New Year's themes) within the $200 budget.
