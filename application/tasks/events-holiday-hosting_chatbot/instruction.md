# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are hosting a holiday gathering. Share which holiday and how many guests.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for hosting a holiday gathering, including a timeline for setup, a menu that accommodates dietary restrictions, and a layout for seating 15 guests.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your holiday gathering: Christmas dinner for 15 guests, including your parents (who are vegan), your cousin with a gluten allergy, and your partner's family who are picky eaters. But don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'plan ahead, make a menu'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific situation with vegan and gluten-free guests.
- Mention that you have a budget of $200 for food and decorations, and you're hosting in a small apartment with limited counter space.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific timeline for the week before Christmas, a menu that includes vegan and gluten-free options, and a seating layout for your apartment, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your vegan and gluten-free guests or your budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific vegan and gluten-free guests, your $200 budget, and your small apartment, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a shopping list or a seating chart).
