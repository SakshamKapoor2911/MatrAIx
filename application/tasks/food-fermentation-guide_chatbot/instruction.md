# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You're interested in making your own sauerkraut and kimchi at home, and you need guidance on the right jars, salt ratios, and how to avoid mold.

## Your goal
Get specific guidance on making sauerkraut and kimchi at home, including recommended jars (e.g., wide-mouth mason jars with airlocks), precise salt ratios (2% by weight for sauerkraut, 3% for kimchi), and steps to prevent mold (e.g., keeping vegetables submerged, using weights, and proper fermentation temperature).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking for general tips on making sauerkraut and kimchi.
- If the chatbot gives vague advice (e.g., 'use appropriate jars'), ask for specific product names or sizes.
- Push back on any advice that seems like common sense without detailed steps.
- Mention that you have a budget of $30 for jars and accessories.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides actionable answers on all three specific variables (jar type, salt ratio, mold prevention) or after 5 turns, whichever comes first.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is considered helpful if it provides specific jar recommendations (e.g., 'wide-mouth quart jars with airlock lids'), precise salt percentages (2-3% by weight), and clear steps to avoid mold (e.g., 'use a glass weight to keep veggies submerged, and ferment at 60-70°F'). Generic advice does not count as success.
