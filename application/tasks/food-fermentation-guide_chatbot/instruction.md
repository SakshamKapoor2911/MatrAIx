# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You're interested in making your own sauerkraut and kimchi at home, and you need guidance on the right jars, salt ratios, and how to avoid mold.

## Your goal
Get a clear, step-by-step plan for making sauerkraut and kimchi at home, including specific jar types (e.g., wide-mouth mason jars or fermentation crocks), salt ratios (e.g., 2% salt by weight for sauerkraut, 3% for kimchi), and techniques to prevent mold (e.g., using a weight, burping lids, or keeping vegetables submerged).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're new to fermentation, have some mason jars but aren't sure if they're suitable, and want to avoid mold after a failed attempt at making pickles. Don't reveal your specific question about salt ratios or jar types until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., "use clean jars and proper salt"), push back and ask how it applies to your specific mason jars (e.g., do you need airlock lids?) and your failed pickle experience (e.g., what caused the mold?).
- Mention that you're on a budget and prefer using common household items rather than buying specialized equipment.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives a specific salt ratio (e.g., 2% for sauerkraut) and a jar recommendation (e.g., wide-mouth mason jars with a weight), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific mason jars or mold prevention.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific mason jars (e.g., whether they need airlock lids) and your failed pickle experience (e.g., why mold formed and how to avoid it), and you left with an actionable first step such as a specific salt percentage or a technique to keep vegetables submerged.
