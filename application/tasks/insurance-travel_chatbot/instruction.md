# Insurance Chatbot

## Your situation
You're planning a $5,000 family trip to Europe and want coverage for trip cancellation and medical emergencies.

## Your goal
Get a travel insurance policy quote that covers trip cancellation and medical emergencies for a $5,000 family trip to Europe, with specific cost and coverage details.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Start by asking about general travel insurance options for Europe.', 'Reveal the $5,000 trip cost and family size only after the chatbot asks or offers generic advice.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests a policy without specifying coverage limits for cancellation and medical.', "Mention that you're looking for affordable options but prioritize comprehensive coverage."]

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific policy recommendation with clear coverage amounts for trip cancellation and medical emergencies, and a price quote.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific policy name, coverage limits (e.g., $5,000 cancellation, $100,000 medical), and a premium quote. It addressed the $5,000 trip cost and family coverage needs.
