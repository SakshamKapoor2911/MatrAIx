# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a single-family home in Portland with a large backyard and want to build a 500-square-foot ADU to rent out. You need guidance on permits and estimated costs.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step guide on permits and estimated costs for building a 500-square-foot ADU in Portland, including specific requirements like setback rules and utility connection fees.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking for a general overview of the process before diving into details.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice, push back by asking for Portland-specific regulations.
- Mention that you have a large backyard and want to confirm if that affects permit requirements.
- Ask about cost breakdowns for permits, design, and construction.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received a clear list of required permits, estimated costs (including permit fees and construction costs per square foot), and any specific Portland zoning rules for ADUs.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided:
- A list of permits (e.g., building permit, zoning permit, utility connection permits).
- Estimated costs: permit fees (e.g., $2,000-$5,000), construction costs ($150-$250 per sq ft), and total projected cost ($75,000-$125,000).
- Portland-specific rules: setback requirements (e.g., 5 ft from property line), height limits (e.g., 20 ft), and owner-occupancy rules.
If any of these are missing or vague, the chatbot failed.
