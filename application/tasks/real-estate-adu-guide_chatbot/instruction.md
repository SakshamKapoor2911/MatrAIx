# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a single-family home in Portland with a large backyard and want to build a 500-square-foot ADU to rent out. You need guidance on permits and estimated costs.

## Your goal
Get a clear, step-by-step guide on the permit process for building a 500-square-foot ADU in Portland, including estimated costs for permits, design, and construction.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you own a single-family home in Portland with a large backyard and want to build a 500-square-foot ADU for rental income. But don't reveal your specific question about permits and costs until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'check local zoning laws,' push back and ask how it applies to your specific property (e.g., 'My lot is 7,000 square feet; does Portland's ADU code allow a 500 sq ft unit on a lot this size?').
- Mention that you're on a tight budget (under $150k total) and want to avoid costly mistakes.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete list of required permits (e.g., building, electrical, plumbing) with estimated fees and a rough timeline, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific property constraints or given cost estimates.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced Portland-specific ADU regulations (e.g., setback requirements, parking exemptions) and you left with a clear next step (e.g., 'submit a pre-application to the Bureau of Development Services') and a ballpark cost range (e.g., permits: $5k–$8k, total construction: $150–$200 per sq ft).
