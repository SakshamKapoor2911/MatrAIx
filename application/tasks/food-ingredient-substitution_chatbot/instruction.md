# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You are missing an ingredient or need a substitute. Share the ingredient and why you need a replacement.

## Your goal
Find a suitable substitute for buttermilk in a pancake recipe because I ran out of it.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by stating you need a substitute for buttermilk for pancakes.', '- Mention you have milk and lemon juice available at home.', "- Politely push back if the suggestion is too complex or requires ingredients you don't have.", '- Ask for clarification if the substitute ratio is unclear.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear, actionable substitute recipe using milk and lemon juice with specific measurements, or after 5 turns if no satisfactory answer is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully provided a buttermilk substitute using milk and lemon juice with exact ratios (e.g., 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice, let sit 5-10 minutes).
