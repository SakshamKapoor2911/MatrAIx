# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a certified financial planner who wants to write a book for millennials on investing basics, but you're unsure how to structure chapters.

## Your goal
Get a concrete chapter outline for a book on investing basics for millennials, with a focus on actionable steps and relatable examples.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're a CFP wanting to write a book for millennials on investing basics, but you're unsure how to structure chapters. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just write an intro, chapters, and conclusion'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific target audience (millennials) and their unique financial challenges.
- Mention that you want the book to be practical and engaging, not textbook-like, and that you have a budget for research but not for a professional editor.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific chapter outline that addresses the unique needs of millennials (e.g., includes topics like student loans, budgeting, and robo-advisors), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific target audience (millennials) or given generic advice only.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific target audience (millennials) and their financial challenges (e.g., student loans, low starting salaries, desire for ethical investing) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a proposed chapter structure or a resource to start outlining).
