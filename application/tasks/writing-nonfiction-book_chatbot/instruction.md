# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a certified financial planner who wants to write a book for millennials on investing basics, but you're unsure how to structure chapters.

## Your goal
Get a concrete chapter outline for a millennial investing basics book that covers budgeting, emergency funds, index funds, and retirement accounts (e.g., Roth IRA, 401k).

## Constraints on your behavior
['- State upfront that you are a certified financial planner but need help structuring a book for millennials.', "- Push back on generic advice like 'just start writing' or 'write what you know'; demand specific chapter titles and order.", '- Mention that the book must be beginner-friendly, avoid jargon, and include practical examples.', '- If the chatbot suggests too many chapters (e.g., >10), ask to prioritize the most essential ones for young readers.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total): your initial request, chatbot response, your follow-up with a specific constraint, and chatbot revised answer.

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a structured outline with at least 5 chapter titles and a brief description for each that addresses budgeting, emergency funds, index funds, and a retirement account option.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides a chapter outline that explicitly includes: (1) a chapter on budgeting basics, (2) a chapter on building an emergency fund, (3) a chapter on index fund investing, (4) a chapter on retirement accounts (Roth IRA and 401k), and (5) a logical order that starts with mindset/goals and ends with taking action. Bonus if it suggests a chapter on avoiding common mistakes.
