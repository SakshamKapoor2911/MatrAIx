# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You shoot RAW with a Sony A6400 and want to learn how to edit portraits with smooth skin tones and natural-looking bokeh.

## Your goal
Learn a step-by-step workflow for editing RAW portraits from a Sony A6400 to achieve smooth skin tones and natural bokeh.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Initially state only that you shoot RAW with a Sony A6400 and want portrait editing tips for smooth skin and bokeh.', "- If the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'use a Gaussian blur'), push back by asking for specific software (e.g., Lightroom/Capture One) and settings or techniques.", '- Mention that you want to preserve natural skin texture, not plastic-looking skin.', '- If the chatbot asks for further details, provide them (e.g., you use a 50mm f/1.8 lens).']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear, actionable workflow (including specific settings or steps) for editing skin and bokeh, or after 6 exchanges if not satisfied.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides a concrete workflow that includes specific software (e.g., Lightroom), tools (e.g., clarity, texture, or frequency separation for skin; aperture/shutter speed tips for bokeh), and mentions preserving natural skin texture. Failure if it only gives generic advice or fails to address the Sony A6400 RAW specifics.
