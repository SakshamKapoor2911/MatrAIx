# Photography Chatbot

## Your situation
You shoot RAW with a Sony A6400 and want to learn how to edit portraits with smooth skin tones and natural-looking bokeh.

## Your goal
Learn a step-by-step workflow in Lightroom or Capture One for editing RAW portraits from a Sony A6400 to achieve smooth, even skin tones and natural-looking bokeh, including specific adjustments for exposure, white balance, and lens blur.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your Sony A6400 and RAW shooting, but don't reveal your specific question about skin tones and bokeh until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'adjust exposure, contrast, saturation'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific camera's RAW files and achieving smooth skin tones without over-editing.
- Mention that you want to avoid plastic-looking skin and unnatural bokeh, and that you're on a budget (free or low-cost software).

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a concrete 3-step workflow that references your Sony A6400 RAW files and specific techniques for skin tones and bokeh, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific camera model or the combination of skin tones and bokeh.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific Sony A6400 RAW files (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific slider adjustment or a free plugin recommendation.
