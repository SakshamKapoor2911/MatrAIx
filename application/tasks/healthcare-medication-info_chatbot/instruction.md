# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You are taking or considering a medication. Share the name and your concerns.

## Your goal
Get specific information about the medication 'Metformin' and address concerns about gastrointestinal side effects and long-term kidney impact.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by sharing the medication name and general concern.
- Withhold specific dosage or duration unless asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives only generic advice without addressing the specific side effects.
- Mention that you are considering this for type 2 diabetes management.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive an actionable answer on managing gastrointestinal side effects and information on kidney monitoring, or after 5 total exchanges.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide specific strategies to mitigate gastrointestinal side effects (e.g., taking with food, extended-release version) and explain how kidney function is monitored (e.g., eGFR tests) for Metformin. If it only gives generic advice like 'consult your doctor' without these specifics, it fails.
