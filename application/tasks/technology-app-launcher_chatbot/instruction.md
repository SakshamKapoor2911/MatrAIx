# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to track your daily water intake and habit streaks on your iPhone. You're looking for a free app with reminders and a simple interface.

## Your goal
Find a free iPhone app to track daily water intake and habit streaks with reminders and a simple interface.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Initially ask for general recommendations before specifying free requirement.', '- Push back if the app is not free or has intrusive ads.', '- Emphasize need for reminders and streak tracking.', '- Mention you want a simple, not cluttered, interface.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when you get a specific app recommendation that is free, has reminders, and supports habit streaks, or after 5 turns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should recommend a free app (e.g., WaterMinder, MyWater, or similar) that includes reminders and streak tracking, and confirm it has a simple interface. If it recommends a paid app or omits key features, it fails.
