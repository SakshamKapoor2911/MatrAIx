# Technology Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to track your daily water intake and habit streaks on your iPhone. You're looking for a free app with reminders and a simple interface.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for a free iPhone app that tracks daily water intake and habit streaks, with reminders and a simple interface.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your desire to track water intake and habit streaks, but don't reveal your specific need for a free app until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot recommends a paid app or one with a complex interface, push back and ask how it applies to your need for a free, simple solution.
- Mention that you prefer an app with no ads or minimal ads, but budget is a constraint.
- If the chatbot suggests multiple options, ask for a single best recommendation for your specific requirements.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific free app (e.g., WaterMinder, Plant Nanny) with reminders and simple interface, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your need for a free app with reminders and simplicity.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendation referenced your specific need for a free app with reminders and simple interface (not generic tips) and you left with a concrete app name to try.
