# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You have been asked to sign a non-compete agreement or are subject to one. Share your situation.

## Your goal
Determine whether the non-compete agreement you've been asked to sign is enforceable in your state, and get a clear list of specific steps to challenge or modify it.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you work as a software engineer at a tech startup and have been asked to sign a non-compete that lasts 2 years and covers all tech companies nationwide. Don't reveal your specific question about enforceability until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'consult a lawyer,' push back by asking how it applies to your specific state (California) and your role.
- Mention you have a limited budget of $500 for legal help and cannot afford a full-blown lawsuit.
- Be skeptical of overly broad terms and ask for concrete examples of what you can and cannot do under the agreement.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a clear, actionable plan that references your specific state (California), your job role, and the 2-year nationwide scope, including steps to negotiate or void the agreement, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (California law, budget of $500, nationwide scope).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (California, software engineer, 2-year nationwide non-compete) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a template for a modification letter or a list of specific legal arguments to raise.
