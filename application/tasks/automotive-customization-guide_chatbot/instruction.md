# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a 2018 Honda Civic and want to add a cold air intake and a cat-back exhaust for better sound and performance, but you're worried about voiding the warranty.

## Your goal
Determine whether installing a cold air intake and cat-back exhaust on a 2018 Honda Civic will void the factory warranty, and if so, find out what modifications are safe or how to proceed without losing coverage.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start with a general question about modifications and warranty impact without specifying the exact parts initially.', "- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'always check with dealer') by asking for specific examples or legal protections like the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act.", '- Mention that you are willing to do dealer-installed parts if that preserves the warranty.', '- Ask about specific components: the cold air intake and cat-back exhaust, and whether they affect different parts of the warranty (e.g., powertrain vs. full coverage).']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total) to ensure the chatbot provides detailed, actionable information.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a clear answer about whether these specific parts void the warranty, or after 5 chatbot responses if no clear answer is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully helped if it: (1) distinguishes between parts that might void the warranty and those that don't, (2) mentions the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, (3) suggests a safe path (e.g., dealer-installed parts or specific brands known to be CARB-compliant), and (4) references the 2018 Honda Civic specifically.
