# Automotive Chatbot

## Your situation
You own a 2018 Honda Civic and want to add a cold air intake and a cat-back exhaust for better sound and performance, but you're worried about voiding the warranty.

## Your goal
Determine whether installing a cold air intake and cat-back exhaust on your 2018 Honda Civic will void your factory warranty, and if so, get advice on how to proceed without losing coverage.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 2018 Honda Civic and your interest in adding a cold air intake and cat-back exhaust for better sound and performance, but don't reveal your specific warranty concern until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about aftermarket parts and warranties, push back and ask how it applies to your specific 2018 Honda Civic and the two specific parts you're considering.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and can't afford to lose warranty coverage on major components like the engine or transmission.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot explicitly tells you whether the cold air intake and cat-back exhaust will void your 2018 Honda Civic's warranty and provides a clear next step, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific parts or warranty concern.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 2018 Honda Civic, the cold air intake and cat-back exhaust, and the warranty implications, and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., contacting a dealer, checking a specific warranty clause, or using a particular brand known to be warranty-safe).
