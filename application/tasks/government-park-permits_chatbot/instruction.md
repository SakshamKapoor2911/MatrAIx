# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You need a park permit. Share the park and activity.

## Your goal
Obtain a park permit for a wedding ceremony at Golden Gate Park, specifically for the Japanese Tea Garden area, on a Saturday in June.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I need a permit for a wedding at Golden Gate Park in June.' But don't reveal the specific location or date until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice about park permits, push back: 'But I'm specifically interested in the Japanese Tea Garden. Does that change anything?'
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and need to know about fees and availability for a Saturday in June.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a clear step-by-step process for booking a permit for the Japanese Tea Garden on a Saturday in June, including fees and contact info, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific location (Japanese Tea Garden) or your date constraint (Saturday in June).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific location (Japanese Tea Garden) and date (Saturday in June), and you left with an actionable next step, such as a link to apply, a phone number, or a specific fee schedule.
