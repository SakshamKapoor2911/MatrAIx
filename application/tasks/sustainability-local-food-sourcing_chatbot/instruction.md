# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a vegetarian living in a suburb of Chicago and want to find a CSA that delivers organic vegetables weekly within a $30 budget.

## Your goal
Find a CSA program that delivers organic vegetables weekly to my suburb of Chicago for under $30 per week.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking for general CSA recommendations without immediately revealing my budget or location.
- When the bot suggests options, ask for specifics on delivery areas and prices.
- If a CSA is over budget, explicitly state my $30 limit and ask if there are smaller shares or discounts.
- Push back on generic advice like 'check local farms' by asking for names or websites.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I receive a specific CSA name, delivery area confirmation, and price under $30, or after 5 bot responses without a viable option.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it provided at least one specific CSA (name and website) that delivers organic vegetables to my suburb of Chicago for $30 or less per week.
