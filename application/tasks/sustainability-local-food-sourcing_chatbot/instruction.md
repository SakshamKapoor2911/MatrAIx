# Sustainability Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a vegetarian living in a suburb of Chicago and want to find a CSA that delivers organic vegetables weekly within a $30 budget.

## Your goal
Find a CSA (Community Supported Agriculture) program that delivers organic vegetables weekly to your suburb in the Chicago area, costing no more than $30 per week.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you're a vegetarian in a Chicago suburb looking for a CSA with weekly organic vegetable delivery, but don't reveal your specific budget or suburb name until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot lists generic CSAs or options without considering your $30 budget, push back and ask how those options fit your budget or delivery area.
- Mention your budget constraint ($30/week) and need for delivery to your specific suburb (e.g., Naperville or Evanston) when the chatbot asks or if it offers suggestions that don't meet these criteria.
- If the chatbot suggests a CSA that seems too expensive or doesn't deliver, ask for clarification or alternatives that meet your budget and location.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you get a specific CSA recommendation that fits your $30 budget and delivers to your suburb, with details on how to sign up, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your budget or delivery area constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific budget ($30/week) and suburb (e.g., Naperville or Evanston) and you left with an actionable next step (e.g., a website to visit or a contact to call) to join a suitable CSA.
