# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You have an idea for a 30-minute comedy pilot set in a failing community theater, but you've never written a screenplay before.

## Your goal
Get a concrete starting plan: how to outline a 30-minute comedy pilot, including a sample scene structure and the first step to write the opening scene.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your idea for a comedy pilot set in a failing community theater, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just write every day'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific premise and lack of experience.
- Mention you have no screenplay format knowledge and want to avoid wasting time on wrong formatting.
- Budget is not relevant, but time is: you have limited evenings to work on this.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a step-by-step outline tailored to your comedy pilot, including a specific opening scene suggestion, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your lack of screenplay experience or given a concrete first step.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific premise (failing community theater) and your beginner status, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'write a logline' or 'outline three scenes') rather than generic encouragement.
