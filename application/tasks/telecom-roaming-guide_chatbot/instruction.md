# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You are traveling internationally. The chatbot explains roaming plans passes and settings.

## Your goal
Activate a 7-day, 5GB international roaming pass for my trip to Japan, starting on October 15, 2024.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Initially state that I need help with roaming for an upcoming trip, without specifying the destination or duration until asked.', "- If the chatbot offers generic advice (e.g., 'turn on data roaming'), ask for specific plans available for my destination.", "- Mention that my current plan is a postpaid family plan and I need a pass that doesn't require a plan change.", '- If the chatbot suggests a plan, ask for the exact price, data limit, and validity period.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I have received confirmation that the 7-day, 5GB pass is activated for Japan starting October 15, 2024, and I have been given instructions on how to use it (e.g., enable data roaming, APN settings).

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully identified my destination (Japan), provided a specific 7-day, 5GB roaming pass option, confirmed activation for the correct start date, and gave clear instructions for using the pass while traveling.
