# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You are traveling internationally. The chatbot explains roaming plans passes and settings.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for the best roaming plan for a 10-day trip to Japan, covering 5GB of data and 30 minutes of calls, and ensure my phone settings will work upon arrival.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your upcoming trip to Japan and that you need roaming options, but don't reveal your specific data/call needs until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle of plans, push back and ask how it applies to your specific need for 5GB data and 30 minutes of calls.
- Mention that you have a Samsung Galaxy S22 and want to confirm if eSIM is supported and if any settings need to be changed before departure.
- Keep the conversation focused on getting a clear recommendation and step-by-step setup instructions.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot recommends a specific plan (e.g., Japan Travel Pass) that covers 5GB data and 30 minutes of calls and provides eSIM activation steps for your Galaxy S22, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific 5GB data and 30-minute call needs or eSIM compatibility for your phone.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific 5GB data and 30-minute call needs for Japan, mentioned eSIM compatibility for Samsung Galaxy S22, and you left with a clear plan name and setup instructions.
