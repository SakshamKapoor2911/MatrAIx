# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a small urban yard and want to start composting kitchen scraps to improve your sandy soil, but you're worried about smell and pests.

## Your goal
Get a step-by-step plan for composting kitchen scraps in a small urban yard that minimizes smell and pests, and improves sandy soil.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Mention small yard and sandy soil early.', '- Express concerns about smell and pests.', '- Push back on complex/expensive setups.', '- Ask for specific tips on what scraps to avoid.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
Persona ends conversation when they receive a clear, actionable plan that addresses smell/pest concerns and fits a small yard, or after 5 turns of unhelpful advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific method (e.g., tumbler, bin type) with instructions for layering greens/browns, a list of acceptable scraps, and tips to reduce odors and deter pests, all tailored to a small urban space and sandy soil.
