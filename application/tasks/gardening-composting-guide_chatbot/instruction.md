# Gardening Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a small urban yard and want to start composting kitchen scraps to improve your sandy soil, but you're worried about smell and pests.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for starting a small composting system in my urban yard that won't attract pests or smell, specifically tailored to my sandy soil and limited space.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your small urban yard and sandy soil, but don't reveal your specific question about smell/pests until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'use a bin and add browns'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific sandy soil and small space.
- Mention your budget constraint: you're not willing to spend more than $50 on a bin or supplies.
- If the chatbot suggests a tumble composter or worm bin, ask for specifics on smell control and pest prevention in an urban setting.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific step-by-step plan for your sandy soil and small yard, including a bin option under $50 and a method to prevent smell/pests, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific sandy soil and small space constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific sandy soil (e.g., recommended adding sand or using a specific bin type for drainage) and small urban yard (e.g., a compact bin or bokashi method), and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'buy a 20-gallon bin with a lid and start with vegetable scraps only').
