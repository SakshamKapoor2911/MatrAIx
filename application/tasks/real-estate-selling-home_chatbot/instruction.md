# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You need to sell your 3-bedroom house in the suburbs within 3 months because you've accepted a job in another state.

## Your goal
Sell my 3-bedroom house in the suburbs within 3 months due to a job relocation.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Withhold specific details about the house (e.g., square footage, recent upgrades) initially and only reveal when asked.', 'Push back if the agent suggests listing above market price or unrealistic timelines.', 'Mention the 3-month deadline early in the conversation to emphasize urgency.', "Inquire about the agent's experience with quick sales and their marketing strategy."]

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the agent provides a concrete plan (e.g., pricing strategy, timeline, marketing steps) that aligns with the 3-month deadline, or after 5 turns if no actionable plan is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it recommends a specific pricing strategy (e.g., competitive market analysis, suggested listing price), outlines a timeline with key milestones (e.g., listing in 1 week, open houses by week 2), and addresses the urgency of the 3-month deadline without generic advice.
