# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You need to sell your 3-bedroom house in the suburbs within 3 months because you've accepted a job in another state.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan to sell my 3-bedroom house in the suburbs within 3 months, including recommended repairs, pricing strategy, and timeline.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you need to sell your 3-bedroom house in the suburbs within 3 months due to a job relocation, but don't reveal your specific concerns about repairs or pricing until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips (e.g., 'declutter and stage your home'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific house, which has an outdated kitchen and a cracked driveway.
- Mention that you have a tight timeline and a limited budget for repairs (max $5,000), so you need cost-effective recommendations.
- If the chatbot suggests major renovations, express concern about time and budget, and ask for alternatives.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific list of 3-5 repairs with estimated costs and a pricing strategy (e.g., list at $X, expect Y% below market), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific house (outdated kitchen, cracked driveway) or your budget constraint.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific house (outdated kitchen, cracked driveway) and budget ($5,000 limit), and you left with an actionable first step you could take (e.g., 'fix the driveway crack and paint kitchen cabinets').
