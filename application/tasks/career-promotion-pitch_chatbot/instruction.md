# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You've led three major projects in the past year that increased revenue by 20%, and you want to prepare a promotion pitch to your manager for a senior analyst position.

## Your goal
Prepare a promotion pitch for a senior analyst position, highlighting three major projects that increased revenue by 20%.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Provide details about the projects only when asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice without specifics.
- Mention that you need concrete examples to include in the pitch.
- Keep the focus on quantifiable results and leadership.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a structured outline or template for the pitch that includes how to frame the revenue increase and project leadership.

## Success judgment
The chatbot should provide a clear pitch structure that explicitly references the three projects and the 20% revenue increase, with suggestions on how to present leadership and results effectively.
