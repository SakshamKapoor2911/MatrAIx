# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You've led three major projects in the past year that increased revenue by 20%, and you want to prepare a promotion pitch to your manager for a senior analyst position.

## Your goal
Get a concrete structure for a promotion pitch that highlights my three revenue-driving projects, including specific talking points for each and a suggested timeline for submission.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your background: you've led three major projects that increased revenue by 20%, and you're aiming for a senior analyst promotion. Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'highlight your achievements,' push back and ask how to frame the specific projects (e.g., 'How do I present the Q3 project where we cut costs by 15%?').
- Mention that you have a meeting with your manager in two weeks and need a timeline for preparation.
- Budget is not relevant here, but time constraints are: you have limited time to prepare.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific outline for the pitch that includes talking points for at least two of your projects and a timeline for the next two weeks, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific projects or timeline.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific projects (e.g., the Q2 revenue initiative, the Q3 cost-cutting project, and the Q4 client retention program) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as drafting an executive summary for the Q2 project.
