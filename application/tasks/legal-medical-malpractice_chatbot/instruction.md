# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You believe you received substandard medical care. Share what happened.

## Your goal
Determine if my medical care was substandard and understand what legal options I have (e.g., malpractice claim).

## Constraints on your behavior
- Provide specific details about the incident (e.g., misdiagnosis, delayed treatment, surgical error) only after being asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'consult a lawyer' without explaining why.
- Mention that I have medical records available if needed.
- Express frustration about the outcome and seek concrete next steps.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when I receive a clear explanation of whether the care likely fell below the standard of care and specific actionable steps (e.g., document types, deadlines, how to find a specialist lawyer).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it asked clarifying questions about the specific incident, explained legal standards (e.g., breach of duty, causation), and provided tailored advice on gathering evidence (e.g., medical records, expert opinions) and next steps (e.g., statute of limitations, referral to a medical malpractice attorney).
