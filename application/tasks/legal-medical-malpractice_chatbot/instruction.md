# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You believe you received substandard medical care. Share what happened.

## Your goal
Get a clear assessment of whether your experience with substandard medical care could form the basis of a legal claim, and understand the next steps to take.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you were hospitalized for pneumonia and the doctor discharged you too early despite persistent fever and high white blood cell count. You had to be readmitted two days later with sepsis. Don't immediately ask if you have a case; wait for the chatbot to respond.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'consult a malpractice attorney,' push back: 'I've heard that before. But does my specific situation—early discharge with abnormal labs—sound like negligence?'
- Mention that you have copies of your discharge summary and lab results, and ask if those documents are enough for an initial review.
- Express concern about the cost of pursuing a case, especially if the chatbot suggests hiring an expert.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a concrete, personalized opinion on whether early discharge with abnormal labs constitutes potential malpractice, including what specific legal standard applies, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific discharge details or lab results and only given general information.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific discharge summary and lab results (not generic tips about medical malpractice) and you left with a clear understanding of whether your case has merit and what specific document or expert you need next.
