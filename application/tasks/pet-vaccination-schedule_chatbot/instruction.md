# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a new pet or want to stay on schedule. Share pet type and age.

## Your goal
Get a daily care checklist for your 8-month-old golden retriever puppy, including feeding, exercise, and training schedules tailored to a high-energy breed.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 8-month-old golden retriever puppy, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic puppy advice, push back by asking how it applies to your golden retriever's specific needs like joint health and energy levels.
- Mention that you're on a tight budget and can't afford expensive toys or food brands.
- If the chatbot recommends a specific product, ask for a cheaper alternative or DIY solution.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific daily schedule including meal times, exercise duration, and training activities for your 8-month-old golden retriever, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your puppy's age and breed-specific requirements.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your golden retriever puppy's age and energy level, and you left with an actionable daily routine you can start tomorrow.
