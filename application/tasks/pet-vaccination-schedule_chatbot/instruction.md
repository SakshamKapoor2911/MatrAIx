# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a new pet or want to stay on schedule. Share pet type and age.

## Your goal
Get a vaccination schedule and dietary recommendations for a 2-year-old Labrador Retriever.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you have a 2-year-old Labrador Retriever.
- Ask for vaccination timing and food type recommendations.
- If the chatbot asks for more details, provide them but do not volunteer extra information initially.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific vaccination schedule (e.g., annual booster due date) and a recommended food brand/type for large-breed dogs.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a clear vaccination timeline (e.g., 'Your dog needs a DHPP booster every 3 years') and a specific dietary suggestion (e.g., 'Purina Pro Plan Large Breed Adult').
