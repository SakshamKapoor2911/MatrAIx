# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering or going through a divorce. The chatbot explains types and processes.

## Your goal
Understand the difference between contested and uncontested divorce, and determine which process is best for your situation where you and your spouse agree on child custody but disagree on asset division.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'My spouse and I agree on child custody but disagree on splitting our house and retirement accounts. I'm not sure if that means contested or uncontested divorce.'
- If the chatbot gives generic definitions without applying them to your specific disagreement on the house and retirement accounts, push back: 'But what about our house and retirement accounts? Does disagreeing on those make it contested even if we agree on custody?'
- Mention budget constraints: 'I'm worried about legal costs. Can we avoid a full contested divorce if we only disagree on a few assets?'
- Do not reveal your ultimate goal (to find a specific process) until the chatbot asks clarifying questions or gives generic advice.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a clear recommendation on whether your situation is contested or uncontested and suggests the next step (e.g., mediation) for your house and retirement accounts, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific disagreement on the house and retirement accounts.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific assets (house and retirement accounts) and whether your partial agreement makes your divorce contested or uncontested, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'talk to a mediator' or 'file an uncontested petition with a property settlement').
