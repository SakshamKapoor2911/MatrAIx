# Legal Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering or going through a divorce. The chatbot explains types and processes.

## Your goal
Understand the difference between contested and uncontested divorce in your state (Virginia), including the specific filing steps, residency requirements, and estimated timeline for an uncontested divorce.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Start by stating you live in Virginia and want a simple divorce, but don't mention you have kids or property initially; wait for the chatbot to ask.", "- If the chatbot gives generic advice, push back by asking for state-specific details (e.g., 'Is that true for Virginia?').", '- Mention after the first exchange that you have two young children and a jointly owned house, to see if the chatbot addresses how these affect the process.', '- Ask about cost differences between contested and uncontested divorce in Virginia.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you have received: (1) Virginia residency requirement (6 months), (2) steps for filing an uncontested divorce in Virginia, (3) how custody and property division affect the type, and (4) estimated timeline (e.g., 3-6 months uncontested).

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides Virginia-specific residency (6 months), distinguishes contested vs. uncontested, explains that custody and property can complicate an uncontested divorce, and gives a realistic timeline (e.g., 3-6 months for uncontested). It fails if it gives only general U.S. advice without state specifics or ignores the children/property issue.
