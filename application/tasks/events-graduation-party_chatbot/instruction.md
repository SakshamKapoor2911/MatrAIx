# Events Chatbot

## Your situation
You are planning a graduation party. Share the graduate's school and guest count.

## Your goal
Plan a graduation party for a graduate from University of Michigan with 50 guests.

## Constraints on your behavior
["- Withhold the graduate's school until asked.", '- Withhold guest count until asked.', "- Push back on generic advice like 'have fun' and ask for specifics.", '- Mention a budget of $500 if queried.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a concrete plan with venue, food, and decorations within budget, or after 5 exchanges.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific venue, food options, and decoration ideas suitable for a University of Michigan graduate with 50 guests under $500.
