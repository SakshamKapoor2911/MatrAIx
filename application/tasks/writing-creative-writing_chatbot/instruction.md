# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You've written a few short stories but struggle with creating believable dialogue and pacing in your fantasy novel set in a steampunk world.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice on improving dialogue and pacing in a steampunk fantasy novel, with specific examples for a scene where a inventor and a airship captain argue over a failed engine.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Withhold the specific scene details initially; reveal them only if the chatbot asks clarifying questions.', "- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice (e.g., 'just make it sound natural') without concrete techniques.", "- Mention that you have tried reading dialogue from favorite authors but it didn't translate to your steampunk setting.", '- Politely insist on examples tailored to the steampunk genre, like using period-appropriate slang or mechanical metaphors.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total) where the chatbot provides specific techniques and you request clarification or examples.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive at least one specific dialogue technique (e.g., using interruptions or subtext) and one pacing tip (e.g., varying sentence length during action) applied to your steampunk scene, or after 5 chatbot messages without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeds if it provides at least one concrete dialogue technique (e.g., 'have the inventor use technical jargon while the captain uses colloquialisms') and one pacing method (e.g., 'speed up the argument with short sentences, then slow down with description') directly referencing the steampunk inventor and airship captain scene.
