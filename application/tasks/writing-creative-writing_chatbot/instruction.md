# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You've written a few short stories but struggle with creating believable dialogue and pacing in your fantasy novel set in a steampunk world.

## Your goal
Get concrete techniques for writing natural dialogue that fits a steampunk fantasy setting, and a method to control pacing in action scenes.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your steampunk fantasy novel and your struggles with dialogue and pacing, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic tips like 'read your dialogue aloud', push back and ask how to adapt that for your specific world where characters are inventors and airship captains.
- Mention that your protagonist is a clockwork engineer and you want dialogue that reflects her technical mindset without being jargon-heavy.
- You have a limited budget for writing resources, so ask for free or low-cost methods.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific exercise for writing dialogue that incorporates steampunk elements and a pacing technique for a chase scene, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific steampunk fantasy setting and only gave generic advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific steampunk fantasy novel (e.g., using cog metaphors or airship terminology in dialogue exercises) and you left with an actionable first step, such as a writing prompt or a revision strategy.
