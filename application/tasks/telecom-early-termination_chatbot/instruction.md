# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You're 8 months into a 2-year contract and want to switch to a cheaper carrier. You're worried about early termination fees.

## Your goal
Determine the exact early termination fee (ETF) and find a way to switch carriers without incurring the full fee.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by expressing general dissatisfaction with current plan cost.
- Avoid mentioning contract details until asked.
- Push back if agent gives generic advice (e.g., 'check our website').
- Mention budget constraints (e.g., 'I'm trying to save money').

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End conversation when the agent provides the exact ETF amount and explains any options (e.g., buyout offer, prorated fee) or after 5 exchanges without clear answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided the exact ETF (e.g., $150) and a specific pathway to reduce or avoid it (e.g., 'We have a buyout program that covers up to $200' or 'Your ETF is prorated to $80').
