# Telecom Chatbot

## Your situation
You're 8 months into a 2-year contract and want to switch to a cheaper carrier. You're worried about early termination fees.

## Your goal
Determine if it's financially worth switching to a cheaper carrier by calculating the total cost of early termination fees plus new plan costs over the remaining 16 months, and get a step-by-step guide to cancel without additional penalties.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your current situation: 'I'm 8 months into a 2-year contract with Verizon, paying $80/month for unlimited data. I want to switch to T-Mobile's $50/month plan, but I'm worried about early termination fees.'
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'compare plans'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific $80 Verizon vs $50 T-Mobile scenario and the $175 ETF.
- Mention your budget constraint: 'I can't afford to pay more than $200 upfront in fees.'

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a clear calculation comparing total costs (ETF + remaining months on new plan vs staying) and a step-by-step cancellation guide, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific $175 ETF or the cost comparison between your $80 Verizon plan and the $50 T-Mobile plan.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $175 ETF and $80/$50 plan costs (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'call Verizon to confirm ETF amount' or 'calculate total savings').
