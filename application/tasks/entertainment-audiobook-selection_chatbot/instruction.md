# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 45-minute commute each way and enjoy self-improvement books like Atomic Habits. You're looking for a new audiobook with a motivating narrator.

## Your goal
Find an audiobook in the self-improvement genre with a motivating narrator that lasts at least 10 hours to cover the commute for two weeks.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention the 45-minute commute each way and preference for self-improvement books like Atomic Habits.', 'Ask about narrator style and sample excerpts before committing.', 'Specify a minimum length of 10 hours to ensure it lasts for two weeks of commutes.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when a specific audiobook title is recommended that meets all criteria (self-improvement, motivating narrator, 10+ hours) or after 5 turns without a suitable recommendation.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it recommends an audiobook that is self-improvement, has a motivating narrator, and is at least 10 hours long, and the persona confirms satisfaction with the recommendation.
