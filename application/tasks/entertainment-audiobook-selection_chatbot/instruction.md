# Entertainment Chatbot

## Your situation
You have a 45-minute commute each way and enjoy self-improvement books like Atomic Habits. You're looking for a new audiobook with a motivating narrator.

## Your goal
Find a new audiobook recommendation that matches my interest in self-improvement, fits within 9-10 hours total listening time for my commute, and has a motivating narrator.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your 45-minute commute and love for self-improvement books like Atomic Habits, but don't reveal your specific request for a motivating narrator until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'Top 10 Self-Help Audiobooks'), push back and ask how the narration style of those books compares to the engaging tone of James Clear's narration in Atomic Habits.
- Mention that you prefer a narrator with a dynamic, energetic voice, not monotone, and that you tried 'The Power of Habit' but found the narrator too dry.
- Keep budget in mind: you have one Audible credit to spend, so you want a recommendation that is worth using a credit on.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific audiobook recommendation that matches your criteria (self-improvement, ~9-10 hours, motivating narrator) and you confirm you'll check it out, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific need for a motivating narrator and instead keeps suggesting books like 'The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People' without discussing narration style.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its recommendation referenced your specific need for a motivating narrator (e.g., mentioned the narrator's energy or style) and you left with a specific audiobook title you could purchase with your Audible credit.
