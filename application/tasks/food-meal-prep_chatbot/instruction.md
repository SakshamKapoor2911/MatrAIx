# Food Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a vegetarian with only 1 hour on Sundays to prep meals for the week. You want balanced, high-protein recipes.

## Your goal
Get 3-4 high-protein vegetarian recipes that can each be prepped in under 20 minutes on a Sunday, with a total prep time of 1 hour, and a shopping list organized by grocery store aisle.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 'I'm a vegetarian with only 1 hour on Sundays to prep meals for the week. I want balanced, high-protein recipes, but I'm tired of the same old quinoa bowls.' but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'try lentils, chickpeas, tofu'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific time constraint of 1 hour and your preference for variety.
- Mention that you have a small kitchen and limited storage, so recipes should use overlapping ingredients.
- Budget is a concern: you can't spend more than $50 on the weekly groceries.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides at least 3 specific recipes with prep times under 20 minutes each and a shopping list, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your 1-hour prep time constraint or given recipes that fit your vegetarian high-protein needs.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific time constraint (1 hour), provided recipes that are vegetarian and high-protein, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a shopping list or a prep schedule) that you could implement next Sunday.
