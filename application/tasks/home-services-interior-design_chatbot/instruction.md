# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to redesign your small living room with a mid-century modern style on a $2,000 budget. You need help choosing a color palette and furniture layout.

## Your goal
Get a concrete plan for a mid-century modern living room redesign: a specific color palette (with paint color names or hex codes) and a furniture layout diagram or step-by-step arrangement guide for a room that is 12x14 feet with a single window on the north wall, all within a $2,000 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your small living room (12x14 ft, north-facing window, current bland beige walls and mismatched furniture) and your desire for mid-century modern style, but don't reveal your budget or ask for specific color/layout advice until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic tips.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'use warm colors' or 'arrange furniture in a U-shape,' push back by asking how that applies to your small space with a single window and your specific budget constraints.
- Mention your $2,000 budget only after the chatbot proposes expensive items or ignore it initially. If the chatbot suggests costly designer pieces, remind it that you need affordable alternatives (e.g., IKEA hacks or thrifted finds).
- Ask for specific paint color names (e.g., 'what shade of mustard or teal?') and a concrete furniture layout (e.g., 'should the sofa face the window or the wall opposite?').

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific color palette with at least three named colors (e.g., 'Sherwin-Williams Naval' and 'Benjamin Moore Lemon Drop') and a clear furniture layout (e.g., 'place the sofa against the south wall, a coffee table in the center, and two armchairs flanking the window'), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your small room dimensions or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific room dimensions (12x14 ft) and north-facing window, gave paint color names (not just 'warm neutrals'), and suggested a furniture arrangement that fits within $2,000 (e.g., recommending a specific IKEA sofa under $500 or a thrifted credenza).
