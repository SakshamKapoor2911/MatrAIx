# Home-Services Chatbot

## Your situation
You want to redesign your small living room with a mid-century modern style on a $2,000 budget. You need help choosing a color palette and furniture layout.

## Your goal
Get specific mid-century modern color palette recommendations and a furniture layout for a small living room, staying within a $2,000 budget.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by stating you have a small living room (approx. 12x15 ft) and a $2,000 budget.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests expensive items or generic advice.
- Ask for specific color hex codes or paint names, and a scaled furniture arrangement.
- Mention you prefer neutral tones with a pop of color (e.g., mustard or teal).

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a concrete palette with at least 3 colors and a rough furniture layout diagram or description, or after 5 turns without progress.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides a specific mid-century modern color palette (e.g., 'Warm beige, olive green, and mustard yellow') and a furniture layout that fits a 12x15 ft room with a budget of $2,000, including cost estimates for key pieces.
