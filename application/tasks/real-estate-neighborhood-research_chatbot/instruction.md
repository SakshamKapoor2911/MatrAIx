# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a neighborhood. Share the area name and what matters most to you.

## Your goal
Decide whether to move to the Highland Park neighborhood by getting specific information about school quality, commute times, and safety that directly applies to my family's needs.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing that you're considering the Highland Park neighborhood and mention you have two kids, but don't reveal your specific concerns about schools, commute, and safety until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'check crime stats online,' push back and ask how the data applies to your specific street or block within Highland Park.
- Mention you have a tight budget for housing, so any recommendations must be realistic for your price range.
- Stay focused on your three priorities: school quality, commute time to downtown, and safety for children.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides specific data on at least two of your three priorities (e.g., school ratings, commute time, crime stats) for Highland Park, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific named items (school quality, commute time, safety) and only gives generic advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific named items (school quality, commute time, safety) for Highland Park and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific website to check school ratings or a suggestion to visit the neighborhood at different times.
