# Real-Estate Chatbot

## Your situation
You are considering a neighborhood. Share the area name and what matters most to you.

## Your goal
Get detailed information about the 'Eastwood' neighborhood, focusing on safety ratings, school quality, and average commute time to downtown.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by sharing only the neighborhood name 'Eastwood' and ask for general info.
- If the bot gives generic advice, push back by specifying that safety and schools are top priorities.
- Mention a budget for housing (e.g., $300k-$400k) only if the bot asks about price range.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the bot provides specific data on Eastwood's crime rates, school ratings (e.g., GreatSchools rating 7+), and average commute time (under 30 minutes).

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it gave concrete numbers or sources for Eastwood's safety (e.g., crime rate per 1000), school ratings (e.g., 8/10), and commute time (e.g., 25 min). Failure if it only gave generic advice or suggested other neighborhoods without addressing Eastwood specifically.
