# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a web developer with 3 years of experience wanting to go freelance. You need help setting rates and finding first clients.

## Your goal
Get a concrete strategy for setting freelance rates and finding first clients, with specific numbers and platforms.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Ask for rate-setting advice without initially mentioning your specific skills or location.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'research the market' without specifics.
- Mention your budget for tools or marketing is $200 max.
- Request actionable steps, not just theory.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when you receive a specific rate range (e.g., $50-$100/hr) and a list of 2-3 client-finding platforms or methods.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific hourly rate range (e.g., $50-$100/hr) tailored to your 3 years of experience, and named at least two concrete platforms (e.g., Upwork, LinkedIn) or methods (e.g., networking events) for finding clients, within your $200 budget.
