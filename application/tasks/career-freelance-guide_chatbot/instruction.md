# Career Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a web developer with 3 years of experience wanting to go freelance. You need help setting rates and finding first clients.

## Your goal
Get a concrete strategy for setting freelance rates based on your current salary and market research, plus a prioritized list of 3 channels to find your first 3 clients within 2 months.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: 3 years as a web dev, want to go freelance, but don't reveal your specific question about rates or clients until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'just network'), push back and ask how it applies to your specific skills (React, Node.js, MongoDB) and location (mid-sized city with no major tech hub).
- Mention you have a budget of $500 for initial marketing/software and can't afford expensive tools.
- Be skeptical of overly optimistic advice; ask for realistic timelines and examples.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific rate calculation formula using your current salary ($70k) and desired overhead (30%), AND a list of 3 client-finding channels with actionable steps for each, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific skills (React, Node.js, MongoDB) or budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific tech stack (React, Node.js, MongoDB) and budget ($500), and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., 'update LinkedIn with project examples' or 'join local meetup for tech startups') that you can implement this week.
