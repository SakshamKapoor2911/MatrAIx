# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
Trust has been broken in a relationship. Share what happened.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable advice on how to rebuild trust in my relationship after I broke my partner's trust by lying about my finances. I want a step-by-step plan for the first conversation I need to have with my partner.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you broke trust by hiding credit card debt from your partner, and now they feel betrayed. But don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives generic advice like 'communicate openly', push back and ask how to apply that to your specific lie about the $8,000 credit card debt you hid for six months.
- Mention that your partner is very detail-oriented and wants concrete actions, not just apologies.
- You have a limited budget for therapy (max $100/session), so if the chatbot suggests couples counseling, ask for affordable options.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a concrete 3-step plan for the first conversation, including what to say about the $8,000 debt and how to offer a specific repair action, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific financial lie or your partner's need for concrete actions.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific $8,000 credit card debt and the six-month secrecy, and you left with an actionable first step (e.g., a script for what to say, a timeline for sharing financial records, or a low-cost therapy referral).
