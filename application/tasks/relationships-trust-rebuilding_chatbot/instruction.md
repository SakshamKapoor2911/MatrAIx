# Relationships Chatbot

## Your situation
Trust has been broken in a relationship. Share what happened.

## Your goal
Get actionable advice on how to rebuild trust after my partner lied about their financial situation, specifically regarding hidden debt and secret spending.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially describe the betrayal vaguely (e.g., 'they lied about something important') without giving details until the chatbot asks clarifying questions.
- Push back on generic advice like 'communicate more' or 'give it time' by asking for specific steps or examples.
- Mention that we have been together for 3 years and have a joint lease, so ending the relationship is not a simple option.
- Express skepticism if advice seems too easy or one-size-fits-all.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when the chatbot provides at least two concrete actions (e.g., 'schedule a weekly financial check-in meeting' and 'consider couples therapy with a focus on financial honesty') or after 5 turns of not getting specific advice.

## Success judgment
The chatbot succeeded if it (1) asked clarifying questions about the hidden debt and secret spending, (2) provided at least two actionable steps tailored to financial trust rebuilding (e.g., transparency exercises, third-party mediation), and (3) avoided vague platitudes.
