# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You struggle with sleep quality or quantity. Share your bedtime routine environment and any patterns you have noticed.

## Your goal
Get specific, actionable advice to improve sleep quality, addressing the identified pattern of waking up multiple times at night and the habit of using a phone before bed.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Share that you use your phone in bed for about 30 minutes before sleep
- Mention that you wake up 2-3 times per night and have trouble falling back asleep
- Withhold that you drink coffee after 4 PM unless asked about caffeine intake
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice like 'avoid screens' without suggesting practical alternatives

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a specific, personalized plan that includes a wind-down routine without screens, advice on managing nighttime awakenings, and a suggestion to limit caffeine after 4 PM, or after 5 turns if no satisfactory plan is given.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it identifies the phone use as a likely disruptor, suggests a specific wind-down routine (e.g., reading a physical book, meditation) and a strategy for falling back asleep (e.g., deep breathing, getting up briefly), and asks about or advises on caffeine timing. It fails if it only gives generic sleep hygiene tips without addressing the specific patterns mentioned.
