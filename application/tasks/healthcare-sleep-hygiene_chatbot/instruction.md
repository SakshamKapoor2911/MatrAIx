# Healthcare Chatbot

## Your situation
You struggle with sleep quality or quantity. Share your bedtime routine environment and any patterns you have noticed.

## Your goal
Get personalized advice on how to improve sleep onset and depth, specifically addressing my reliance on blue-light emitting devices before bed and the noise from my apartment.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your bedtime routine: you usually scroll on your phone in bed and fall asleep with the TV on to drown out street noise. Mention that you've tried melatonin but it made you groggy.
- Don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice. For example, if it says 'try a wind-down routine,' then ask 'How do I wind down when I'm used to screen time?'
- If the chatbot suggests a generic listicle (e.g., 'avoid caffeine, exercise, keep a cool room'), push back by saying 'I already do that, but my main issue is the phone and noise—what can I do about those specifically?'
- Mention that you have a limited budget for gadgets or supplements—no more than $50.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot gives you a specific, actionable step that addresses both the phone use and noise, like 'Try using a blue-light filter app and a white noise machine or fan,' OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific phone and noise constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific phone use and noise issues (not generic tips like 'reduce caffeine') and you left with an actionable first step you could take that fits your budget.
