# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You're spending $2,500 on a non-refundable trip to Japan next month and are worried about medical coverage abroad.

## Your goal
Confirm that my travel insurance covers medical emergencies in Japan (including hospital stays and evacuation), understand how to file a claim if needed, and get a clear checklist of what documents to carry.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your upcoming $2,500 non-refundable trip to Japan and your general worry about medical coverage abroad, but don't reveal your specific question about claim procedures until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about travel insurance, push back and ask how it applies to your specific Japan trip and non-refundable situation.
- Mention that you have a pre-existing condition (mild asthma) and ask if that affects coverage.
- Indicate that you're on a tight budget and can't afford additional premium beyond what you already paid for the trip.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific list of documents to carry for claims in Japan and confirms whether pre-existing conditions are covered, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific Japan trip, non-refundable cost, or asthma pre-existing condition.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific Japan trip (including non-refundable $2,500 cost and mild asthma) rather than generic tips, and you left with a clear actionable step (e.g., 'call your insurer to confirm pre-existing coverage' or 'download this claim form').
