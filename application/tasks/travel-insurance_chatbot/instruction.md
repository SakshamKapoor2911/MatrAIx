# Travel Chatbot

## Your situation
You're spending $2,500 on a non-refundable trip to Japan next month and are worried about medical coverage abroad.

## Your goal
Confirm that my existing travel insurance covers medical expenses in Japan, or identify a supplemental policy that fills gaps.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Withhold the exact trip cost and destination until asked.
- Push back if the chatbot gives generic advice without checking my specific policy details.
- Mention my budget for supplemental insurance is up to $100.
- Ask about pre-existing condition coverage if relevant.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when I receive a clear, actionable answer about whether my current policy covers Japan medical expenses, or a specific supplemental policy recommendation with cost and coverage details.

## Success judgment
The chatbot correctly identifies that I need medical coverage in Japan, asks about my current policy and trip specifics (cost, destination), and provides either a confirmation of coverage or a specific supplemental policy (e.g., 'World Nomads Standard Plan') with a price within $100 and clear medical coverage details.
