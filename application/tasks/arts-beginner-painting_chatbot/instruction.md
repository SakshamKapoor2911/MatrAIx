# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You've never painted before but want to start with watercolors. You have a $50 budget for supplies.

## Your goal
Get a list of specific watercolor supplies (paints, brushes, paper) that total no more than $50.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Mention you are a complete beginner.
- State your $50 budget early.
- Ask for clarification if recommendations are too vague (e.g., 'student-grade' without brands).
- Push back if total exceeds $50.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a concrete list of items (brands, sizes, quantities) that sum to $50 or less, or after 5 chatbot turns without a satisfactory answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided specific product recommendations (e.g., 'Winsor & Newton Cotman watercolor set', 'Princeton round brush size 6', 'Canson XL watercolor pad') that fit within the $50 budget and are appropriate for a beginner.
