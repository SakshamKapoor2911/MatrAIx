# Arts Chatbot

## Your situation
You've never painted before but want to start with watercolors. You have a $50 budget for supplies.

## Your goal
Learn the essential watercolor supplies I can buy for $50 and get a simple first project to practice basic techniques.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your interest in watercolors and your $50 budget, but don't reveal your specific question until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'buy paints, brushes, paper'), push back and ask how to choose specific items within your budget.
- Mention that you're a complete beginner and want to avoid wasting money on unnecessary supplies.
- If the chatbot suggests an expensive item, remind them of your $50 limit.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific list of supplies (with approximate prices) totaling under $50 and a simple first project (e.g., a color wheel or a simple landscape), OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your $50 budget or given specific product recommendations.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your $50 budget (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable first step you could take, such as a specific shopping list and a project to start.
