# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You're researching the history of your house and want to access property records and previous sale prices from the county assessor's office.

## Your goal
Obtain the property records and previous sale prices for your house at 123 Oak Street, including the names of previous owners and any historical documents the assessor's office can provide.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation: you've recently moved into a house at 123 Oak Street and want to learn its history, but don't reveal your specific question about property records and sale prices until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'check the county website'), push back and ask how to access records specifically for your house at 123 Oak Street, including sale prices from the last 20 years and previous owner names.
- Mention that you've tried the county website but couldn't find historical sale prices, only current tax assessments.
- Be polite but persistent; if the chatbot suggests contacting the assessor's office, ask for specific contact details and what information you need to provide to get the records.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a direct link or specific instructions to obtain the property records and sale prices for 123 Oak Street, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your request for historical sale prices and previous owner names for your specific address.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if it referenced your specific address (123 Oak Street) and provided actionable steps to obtain historical sale prices and previous owner names (e.g., a form to fill, a phone number for the records department, or a link to a database with historical data).
