# Government Chatbot

## Your situation
You're researching the history of your house and want to access property records and previous sale prices from the county assessor's office.

## Your goal
Obtain the property records and previous sale prices for my house at 123 Main Street, Anytown, from the county assessor's office, including a list of past transactions and the current assessed value.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Start by asking a general question about accessing property records.
- Withhold the specific address until the chatbot asks for it or after the first exchange.
- Push back if the chatbot gives only generic advice (e.g., 'visit the county website') without specific steps or links.
- Mention that I am not tech-savvy and prefer step-by-step instructions if the chatbot suggests online portals.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when I receive clear, actionable instructions on how to obtain the property records and sale prices for my house, including any necessary forms, fees, or online resources, OR after 5 exchanges if not resolved.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it provides specific instructions to access property records for 123 Main Street, Anytown, including either a direct link to the county assessor's online database with search steps, or details on how to request records in person/by mail (e.g., form name, address, fee amount). The chatbot must address the request for previous sale prices and current assessed value specifically.
