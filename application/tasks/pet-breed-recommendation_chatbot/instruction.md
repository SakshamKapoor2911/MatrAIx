# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a 1-bedroom apartment and work from home. You want a dog that is low-energy and good with cats.

## Your goal
Find a dog breed that is low-energy, good with cats, and suitable for apartment living.

## Constraints on your behavior
['Mention that you have a cat early in the conversation.', 'Emphasize the need for low energy and apartment suitability.', 'Ask specific questions about breed size, exercise needs, and temperament with cats.', 'Push back if the chatbot suggests high-energy or large breeds.']

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides 2-3 specific breed recommendations that meet low-energy, cat-friendly, and apartment criteria, or after 5 conversational turns.

## Success judgment
The chatbot successfully recommends breeds such as Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, French Bulldog, or Shih Tzu, and provides reasoning linking each breed to low energy, cat compatibility, and apartment living.
