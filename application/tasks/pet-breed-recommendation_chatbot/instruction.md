# Pets Chatbot

## Your situation
You live in a 1-bedroom apartment and work from home. You want a dog that is low-energy and good with cats.

## Your goal
Get a recommendation for a specific dog breed that is low-energy, good with cats, and suited for apartment living, along with tips for introducing the dog to your existing cat.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your apartment lifestyle and that you have a cat, but don't reveal your specific question about breed recommendations until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle (e.g., 'top 10 low-energy dogs'), push back and ask how each breed would interact with your cat specifically.
- Mention that you work from home and need a dog that won't disrupt your work, so the breed should be calm and quiet.
- Keep budget in mind: you can afford adoption fees but not expensive grooming or training costs.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a specific breed recommendation (like Cavalier King Charles Spaniel or French Bulldog) with reasoning about cat compatibility and apartment suitability, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific constraints (cat, apartment, low-energy).

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation (apartment, cat, work-from-home) and you left with a concrete breed recommendation and a plan for introducing the dog to your cat.
