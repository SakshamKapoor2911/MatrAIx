# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You need a cover letter. Share the job title and why you are interested.

## Your goal
Get a tailored cover letter draft for the job title 'Marketing Coordinator' at a nonprofit organization, emphasizing my passion for social impact.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Initially only provide the job title 'Marketing Coordinator' and general interest in social impact; withhold specific details (e.g., nonprofit name, skills) unless asked.
- Push back if the chatbot suggests a generic template or asks for irrelevant information.
- Mention budget constraints if the chatbot proposes paid services.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End when the chatbot provides a draft cover letter that includes the job title and references social impact, or after 5 turns without a satisfactory draft.

## Success judgment
The chatbot is successful if it produces a cover letter draft that: (1) addresses the Marketing Coordinator role, (2) incorporates a reason for interest in social impact, and (3) is not a generic template (i.e., includes at least one specific detail from the user's inputs).
