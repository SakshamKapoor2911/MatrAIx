# Writing Chatbot

## Your situation
You need a cover letter. Share the job title and why you are interested.

## Your goal
Get a customized cover letter template that highlights my project management experience and explains my passion for sustainable design.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing that you're applying for a 'Sustainability Coordinator' role at a green building firm, but don't reveal you need a cover letter until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic list of tips, push back by saying you have specific project management experience in LEED-certified projects and need that emphasized.
- Mention you have a tight deadline (apply in 3 days) and want a draft that can be personalized quickly.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) you receive a cover letter draft that incorporates your LEED project experience and sustainability passion, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your specific project management background or the tight deadline.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice or draft referenced your specific LEED project management experience and sustainability passion, and you left with an actionable cover letter draft you can tailor and submit within 3 days.
