# Insurance Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a self-employed graphic designer with no sick leave. You want to protect your income if you get injured.

## Your goal
Get a clear explanation of how disability insurance works for self-employed people, and a specific recommendation for a policy that covers at least 60% of my income with a 90-day waiting period.

## Constraints on your behavior
- Open by describing your situation as a self-employed graphic designer with no sick leave, but don't reveal your specific question about disability insurance until the chatbot asks a follow-up or gives generic advice.
- If the chatbot gives a generic listicle about disability insurance, push back and ask how it applies to your specific needs as a freelancer with variable income.
- Mention that you have a limited budget and cannot afford high premiums, so you need a cost-effective solution.

## Interaction requirements
At least two back-and-forth exchanges (4+ messages total). Each exchange should move the conversation forward.

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when EITHER (a) the chatbot provides a specific policy recommendation with coverage percentage and waiting period that fits your budget, OR (b) after 5 exchanges the chatbot has still not addressed your self-employment income variability and budget constraints.

## Success judgment
The chatbot helped if its advice referenced your specific situation as a self-employed graphic designer (not generic tips) and you left with an actionable next step, such as a specific policy name or a clear understanding of how to calculate your coverage amount based on your variable income.
