# Insurance Chatbot

## Your situation
You're a self-employed graphic designer with no sick leave. You want to protect your income if you get injured.

## Your goal
Find an income protection insurance policy that covers self-employed graphic designers, with a focus on injury-related claims, and determine the monthly premium for a benefit amount of $3,000 per month.

## Constraints on your behavior
['- Start by asking for general info about income protection for self-employed individuals.', '- Mention that you are a graphic designer and work from home.', '- Push back if the agent suggests employer-provided benefits or generic disability insurance.', '- Ask about waiting periods and coverage for partial disability.', '- Mention a budget of up to $150 per month for premiums.']

## Interaction requirements
At least three back-and-forth exchanges (6+ messages total).

## Termination criteria
End the conversation when you receive a specific quote for $3,000/month benefit with a 30-day waiting period, or after 5 turns of not getting a clear answer.

## Success judgment
The chatbot provided a specific monthly premium for a $3,000/month income protection policy with a 30-day waiting period, tailored for a self-employed graphic designer, and confirmed coverage for injuries.
